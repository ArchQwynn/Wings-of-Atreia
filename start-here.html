Start Here

Never played Wings of Atreia before? Follow this short path. In about 10–15 minutes, you should understand what your character is, choose a faction and class direction, know the rules that matter first, and be ready for Session 0.

1

What is Wings of Atreia?

Wings of Atreia is a heroic fantasy tabletop roleplaying game set in the shattered world of Atreia. You play a mortal who undergoes Ascension and becomes a Daeva—an ageless winged hero able to take part in conflicts that ordinary people cannot survive.

The game combines character-driven adventuring with aerial movement, class-based combat, exploration, spellcasting, political conflict, and large-scale Warfare. Characters advance from Level 1 to Level 20 in a single class through milestone advancement; multiclassing is not used.

2

Choose Your Faction

Your first major identity choice is Elyos or Asmodian. This choice establishes homeland, culture, political loyalties, visual traditions, and the society your character knows. It does not grant a racial or faction ability-score bonus, and neither faction is mechanically superior.

Elyos

Daevas of Elysea, the southern half of shattered Atreia. Elyos civilization is centered on Sanctum and shaped by the Seraphim Lords, with regions ranging from fertile Poeta to arid Eltnen and the devastated lands of Theobomos.

Explore the Elyos →

Asmodians

Daevas of Asmodae, the northern half of shattered Atreia. Asmodian civilization is centered on Pandaemonium and shaped by the Shedim Lords, with lands ranging from the severe frontiers of Altgard and Morheim to frozen Beluslan.

Explore the Asmodians →

You do not need to choose based on which side your friends pick. Wings of Atreia can place Elyos and Asmodian characters in situations where survival, shared objectives, or greater threats force cooperation despite the war between their peoples.

Find the Class That Sounds Like You

There are 11 playable classes. You do not need to understand every feature before choosing—start with the fantasy and playstyle you want, then read that class page.

I want precision and high damage.

Assassin — close-range precision and Rune Marks.
Ranger — ranged pressure, Focus, traps, and positioning.
Gunslinger — Pistols, Muskets, and Tempo.

I want powerful magic.

Sorcerer — destructive and controlling Aetheric spellcasting.

I want to support the party.

4

Learn Only the Rules You Need First

Do not try to memorize the whole ruleset. For your first session, focus on the basic d20 rule, what you can do on your turn, your class's Level 1 features, and how Flight works.

  • Core resolution: d20 + relevant modifier, plus Proficiency when applicable.
  • Your turn: Action, movement, Bonus Action when granted, and normally one Reaction per round.
  • Your class: know your main resource and Level 1 features.
  • Flight: Wings are a real gameplay system, including Combat Flight Slots.

5

Create Your Character in the Character Manager

The official character-management method is the Wings of Atreia Character Manager. Use it for character creation records, advancement, live HP and resources, equipment, inventory, spells, specialization state, Daeva Points, professions, and other tracked character information.

At creation, choose your faction and class, assign the fixed ability array 16, 14, 14, 12, 10, 8, choose your Background and skills, and establish your character's name, appearance, history, relationships, and motivation. Your class's starting equipment and other starting values should be recorded there.

6

Know Where the Campaign Begins

The default campaign takes place during the Abyss War, centuries after Atreia was shattered. Elyos and Asmodians contest the Abyss while the Balaur stand as a third great power, and the conflict reaches beyond familiar homelands toward the Balaurean frontier.

You do not need to learn every region or Lord before playing. Knowing your homeland, your faction's worldview, and the basic reason the Abyss matters is enough; the rest can be discovered naturally through play.

7

Arrive Ready for Session 0: Ascension

Your first Wings of Atreia experience normally begins with your character still unascended. During Session 0, the character undergoes Ascension, becomes a Daeva, and receives their Wings. The continuing campaign begins with that newly Ascended hero stepping into a much larger world.

Bring a clear character concept rather than a completed personal epic. Know who your character was as a mortal, what matters to them, how they feel about their faction, and why becoming a Daeva changes their life. The campaign will give you room to discover who they become afterward.
