Wings of Atreia — Multiplayer Connection Harness

An isolated multiplayer role and connection test. This does not load or modify the VTT tabletop.

Test only: this harness uses the public PeerJS signaling service and browser WebRTC. Capacity is configured for up to 25 players, plus 1 GM. Do not use real campaign secrets or sensitive data. Display names are not authentication. The GM must approve every connection. Closing the GM tab ends the session; host recovery and persistent sessions are not implemented here.
