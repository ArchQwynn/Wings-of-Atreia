Offsets reposition the map image relative to the grid. Positive horizontal moves it right; positive vertical moves it toward the top of the map.

Pixel sizing keeps the prop image at a chosen screen size as you zoom. The ground stand remains visible around it and marks its grid location.

Upload a prop or environmental asset image, add it, then drag it across the grid to position it. Assets are separate from tokens.
