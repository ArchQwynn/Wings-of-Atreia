Spiritmaster

“Never fight alone. Your spirit is with you.”

Website rules source: Wings of Atreia Player Handbook v3.10 — Publication Master, 8 September 2026. This page preserves the handbook's class material and ordering.

The Spiritmaster is a two-body tactical caster. One active elemental spirit shares the Spiritmaster’s turn and action economy; together they equal one complete player character. The class establishes two battlefield positions, casts through the spirit, and earns Spirit Bond only through meaningful master-spirit coordination.

Class Identity

Spiritmasters are binders and commanders of elemental spirits who fight as a partnership rather than as a solitary caster. Their strength comes from coordinating their own spellcraft with a Greater Spirit, applying pressure, disruption, and control across more than one position on the battlefield. Spirit Bond represents the relationship and command structure that makes this divided presence function as one class.

Tradition & Outlook

The Spiritmaster tradition develops from Atreia’s mage disciplines but turns outward toward spirits, elemental forces, and the difficult art of directing entities shaped by Aether. Spiritmasters are often patient observers, researchers, summoners, or specialists in threats that cannot be understood through ordinary physical study. Their beliefs about spirits differ: some emphasize command, others partnership, responsibility, curiosity, or respect for elemental power.

Appearance & Adventuring

Spiritmasters use cloth armor and magical implements, while the visible presence of a summoned spirit often defines their silhouette more strongly than clothing does. Their equipment may include ritual, scholarly, or elemental motifs without creating additional mechanics. They adventure to understand unusual spirits, recover lost summoning knowledge, counter supernatural threats, serve as versatile magical operatives, or deepen the bond between Daeva and elemental ally.

Core Engine — Summon Spirit

You know all four fundamental spirits—Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind—and choose among them tactically. Only one Spiritmaster spirit may normally be active. Summoning uses an Action and places the spirit in a visible unoccupied space within 15 feet. A defeated spirit disperses into Aether, makes no death saves, and cannot be resummoned until the start of your next turn. Outside combat a newly summoned spirit appears at full HP; voluntary replacement during combat preserves the dismissed spirit’s current HP percentage rather than healing by switching forms.

Spirit Bond

Spirit Bond begins at 0 when genuine combat starts and resets to 0 when combat ends. Maximum Bond is 3 at Level 1, 4 at Level 2, 5 at Level 5, and 6 at Level 9; Bondweaver increases this maximum by 1 from Level 3 onward. Once on each of your turns, gain 1 Spirit Bond if both you and your spirit successfully affect hostile creatures during that turn. Successfully Affect means damage, a hit, a failed save, meaningful hostile condition/penalty, forced movement, or another real combat effect. Merely targeting or applying an effect to which the target is immune does not count. Independent riders do not recursively generate Bond.

A level that only changes passive numerical scaling, grants a Feat/ASI or Spell Advancement, or automatically upgrades an already listed option does not need a duplicate row; use the level progression for the new value. Shared Aether

Once on each of your turns when you cast a Spiritmaster spell that affects a hostile creature within 10 feet of your spirit, choose one: Channel Through Spirit measures range, origin, cover, and line of effect from the spirit’s space while you remain the caster; or Resonant Target gives +1 to the spell attack or +1 to the first save DC against one creature adjacent to your spirit. Self-range spells cannot normally be projected through the spirit.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1

Gain spellcasting, Summon Spirit, Spirit Bond (max 3), Bond Techniques, and Shared Aether. Maximum Spell Level 1.

Level 2

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 2. Spirit Bond maximum becomes 4. Spirit Link lets you know spirit direction/distance and whether above/below half HP within 60 ft; Bonus Action sensory perception makes attacks against your body gain Advantage until next turn. Aetheric Command adds Aggressive, Guarding, or Mobile command modes once each turn.

Level 3

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and choose Spirit Caller, Hexbinder, or Bondweaver.

Spirit Caller — Empowered Spirit / Predatory Bond

Once/turn on basic Spirit Attack hit add one attack die: Fire +1d8; Water/Earth/Wind +1d6, doubled on crit. When spending Bonded Assault, spirit may move 5 ft without provoking from the creature hit.

Hexbinder — Lingering Hex / Cursed Conduit

Once/turn after a creature fails a Spiritmaster spell save choose Enfeeble (-PB next damage, min1), Hinder (Speed -10), or Expose (+2 next spirit attack) until your next turn. When Shared Aether originates a save spell from spirit, one creature within 5 ft of spirit takes -1 on that save.

Bondweaver — Deepened Bond / Efficient Bond / Shared Guard

Maximum Bond +1. Once/turn when you spend Bond, roll d6; on 6 regain 1 after resolution. Protective Bond within 15 ft reduces an additional PB damage.

Level 4

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 3, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 5

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Bond maximum becomes 5. Bonded Assault becomes 1d10, Protective Bond 1d8 + INT, Spirit Recall 40 ft. Coordinated Manifestation triggers once/turn after both master and spirit affect hostiles: choose 1d8 Force damage, Speed -10 (or extra -5), or PB + INT temporary HP.

Level 6

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 4, and your Level 6 specialization feature.

Spirit Caller — Relentless Spirit

Once/turn on basic hit: Fire deals INT Fire at start target next turn; Water adds another -5 Speed; Earth gives Disadvantage on target next attack; Wind moves spirit 10 ft safely. It cannot stack immediate damage with later Ascendance unless one effect is delayed/control/movement.

Hexbinder — Malignant Echo

Once/turn after a failed Spiritmaster save, choose a creature within 5 ft of spirit: -2 next save vs your Spiritmaster spell, -2 next successful damage, or no Reactions until your next turn.

Bondweaver — Resonant Bond

Once/turn after normal Bond generation choose: you/spirit heal PB; both move 5 ft safely; or next Protective Bond before your turn reduces +PB.

Level 7

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Spiritual Reconstitution makes post-defeat summoning a Bonus Action after the normal delay, gives the new spirit PB + INT temporary HP, and grants the spirit Advantage vs forced reposition/dismiss/incapacitate while within 30 ft.

Level 8

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 5, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 9

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Bond maximum becomes 6. Spirit Sight functions to 120 ft with exact position/HP/conditions and no body-vulnerability for sensory sharing. Resonant Casting adds Piercing (-1 first save; no stack Cursed Conduit), Bonded (spirit Advantage next basic attack), or Protective (PB temp HP) resonance when casting through Shared Aether.

Level 10

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 6, and your Level 10 specialization feature.

Primal Convergence — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Convergent Damage. On a hit, the target takes fixed Force damage: 90 at Levels 10-14, 110 at Levels 15-19, or 130 at Level 20.

Primal Suppression. On a hit, the target's Speed becomes 0, it cannot take Reactions, and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of your next turn. At Level 15, the suppression instead lasts until the end of your next turn. At Level 20, the target also cannot benefit from increases to its Speed or teleport while suppressed. This is a bespoke restriction, not Stunned or Incapacitated.

Spirit Caller — Spirit Ascendance

Fire: next Fire damage before your turn +1d8. Water: first damaged creature gets additional Speed reduction. Earth: resistance to next damage instance before your turn. Wind: immediate 15-ft safe move; if ending adjacent different hostile, +1 next attack against it.

Hexbinder — Compounding Hex

Once/turn when already-Hexed creature fails another Spiritmaster save choose Wither (-PB next damage), Suppress (no Reactions/no Dash), or Expose Aether (+1 DC or spell attack for your next Spiritmaster spell against it).

Bondweaver — Overflowing Bond

Once/round when valid Bond would be gained at maximum, instead choose heal 1d8 + INT, PB + INT temp HP, or both master/spirit move 10 ft safely.

Level 11

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Twin Aether lets one Action-cost Spiritmaster spell include one basic Spirit Attack immediately before/after the spell, freeing your Bonus Action but never granting a second normal spirit attack. Greater Spirit Chassis changes HP formula to 8 + 6×level + INT, grants all-save proficiency + Adv vs Charm/Fear, and adds one attack die: Fire 2d8 + PB; others 2d6 + PB before specialization dice.

Level 12

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 7, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 13

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Greater Shared Aether reaches 120 ft and adds one Relay option: Extend eligible spell +30 ft, Stabilize spirit next save with Advantage, or Empower next spirit basic attack vs affected hostile +1d8 damage of that spirit’s Basic Attack’s damage type. Spirit Persistence once/round spends 2 Bond when spirit would fall to 0, leaving it at 1 HP plus PB + INT temp HP.

Level 14

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 8, and your final specialization feature.

Spirit Caller — Apex Spirit

Fire ignores resistance (not immunity) and once/turn +INT to one Fire roll; Water strengthens Speed suppression and prevents Speed increases until your next turn; Earth once/round resists B/P/S attack damage; Wind gains flying Speed equal Speed, may pass hostile spaces as difficult terrain, and OAs vs it have Disadvantage after 10+ ft movement.

Hexbinder — Dominion Hex

Once/turn when an already-Hexed creature fails a Spiritmaster save, spend 1 Bond for one until your next turn: Weakness halves its next successful damaging attack/ability; Binding sets Speed 0 and no Dash; Silence removes Reactions and gives Disadvantage on first save to maintain a spell/magical effect. Flat save penalties do not stack.

Bondweaver — Perfect Resonance

Efficient Bond refunds on 5–6. Shared Vitality heals the other pair member for half when one receives Bondweaver-feature healing. Harmonic Guard may spend +1 Bond with Protective Bond to apply the same rolled reduction to the next damage instance suffered by the other member before your next turn; one active at a time.

Level 15

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Perfect Coordination once/turn after both master/spirit affect hostiles: choose 2d8 independent Force damage; -2 next save vs your Spiritmaster spell; regain 1 Bond or heal PB + INT at max; or both move 15 ft safely from one creature.

Ascendant Primal Convergence

Your Primal Convergence now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and a Feat / ASI. Maximum Spell Level remains 8.

Level 17

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 9. Apex Twin Aether applies one Aetheric Command to its granted spirit attack and then moves spirit 10 ft safely. Bonded Assault becomes 2d10, Protective Bond 2d8 + INT, and Recall can exchange positions within 60 ft.

Level 18

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Sovereign Spirit Link extends Spirit Sight/Shared Aether to 300 ft; within 60 ft grants Advantage vs Charm, Fear, force movement, banishment/dismissal, or magical incapacitation. Spirit Persistence costs 1 Bond and grants 2×PB + INT temp HP. Master and spirit gain Aetheric Anchor protection against forced teleport/banishment/movement and suppression.

Level 19

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and the universal Empyrean Boon.

Level 20

Gain final +2 Spell Advancements. Perfect Bond starts genuine combat with 3 Spirit Bond. Perfect Manifestation makes summoning a Bonus Action and can immediately resummon a spirit actually reduced to 0 with full HP after the normal source restrictions; voluntary replacement still preserves HP%. Perfect Coordination chooses two different benefits instead of one, and each completed coordination grants the active spirit its element-specific Perfect Spirit Interaction rider.

Apex Primal Convergence

Your Primal Convergence uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Example Combat Sequence

  1. Cast a debuff through Shared Aether so the spell originates from the spirit’s position, then command the spirit to attack the same or another hostile. If both halves successfully affect hostiles during the turn, generate 1 Spirit Bond.
  2. Spend Bonded Assault only after the spirit hits, preserving Bond if the attack misses. Use Protective Bond off-turn when a dangerous attack lands and the pair remains within 30 feet.
  3. From Level 11, cast an Action-cost Spiritmaster spell and use Twin Aether to include the spirit’s one basic attack in the same Action; the spirit still does not gain a second independent turn.
  4. At Level 15, the same successful master-spirit turn can both generate ordinary Bond and satisfy Perfect Coordination, but the coordination rider itself cannot recursively count as another qualifying effect.
