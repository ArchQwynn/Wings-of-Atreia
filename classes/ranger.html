Ranger

“Chase me or run away. Either way, you'll only die tired.”

The Ranger is a nonmagical ranged hunter that controls enemy movement with persistent battlefield traps, spends Focus on precision or evasive shots, and is rewarded for exploiting targets that walk into prepared terrain. Its sustained damage comes from two ranged attacks, not from spellcasting or a third standard attack.

Class Identity

Rangers are mobile ranged specialists who control distance through marksmanship, movement, traps, and battlefield awareness. They are most effective when they can read terrain before committing, choose favorable lines of attack, and punish enemies that approach carelessly. Focus represents the Ranger’s ability to convert patience and perception into precise action.

Tradition & Outlook

The Ranger tradition also descends from Atreia’s scout disciplines, but where Assassins close with a chosen target, Rangers cultivate distance, fieldcraft, and control of the approach. Rangers are common among scouts, hunters, pathfinders, border wardens, reconnaissance troops, and independent Daevas accustomed to surviving beyond secure settlements. They tend to value preparedness, observation, mobility, and freedom of movement.

Appearance & Adventuring

Rangers usually wear mobile armor and carry bows, ammunition, traps, and field equipment suited to extended movement. Their gear often reflects the terrain in which they learned their craft. Rangers adventure to explore dangerous regions, hunt monsters or enemy operatives, guide expeditions, secure routes, protect remote communities, or prove that no battlefield is truly open ground when preparation and range are on their side.

Core Engine — Focus

Maximum Focus equals your PB + WIS modifier, minimum 2. A Long Rest restores all expended Focus. A Short Rest restores Focus equal to PB, up to your maximum. Focus is a tactical reserve rather than ammunition or a tax on ordinary ranged attacks; Ranger Traps normally cost no Focus.

Trapcraft

  • Deploy one known Ranger Trap as a Bonus Action in an unoccupied space within 5 feet. A trap occupies one 5-foot space.
  • You normally deploy no more than one Ranger Trap during a turn unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.
  • Only hostile creatures trigger your traps unless you designate otherwise when deploying a trap.
  • A trap remains until triggered, dismantled, made inert because you exceed your active-trap limit, or until you finish a Long Rest.
  • Base active-trap limit is 2 through Level 10 and 3 from Level 11 onward.
  • Traps known: 3 at Level 1, 4 at Level 3, 5 at Level 7, 6 at Level 9, and 7 at Level 13.
  • Trap damage is independent damage and does not double because one of your weapon attacks was a critical hit.

Exploit Opening

Once on each of your own turns, when you make a ranged weapon attack against a creature that triggered one of your Ranger Traps since the end of your previous turn, you may make that attack with Advantage.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Focus, Trapcraft and Exploit Opening

Focus

Maximum Focus is PB + WIS, minimum 2. Long Rest restores all; Short Rest restores PB.

Focus Techniques

You gain Precision Shot +1d6, Pinning Shot -10 Speed, and Evasive Step 10 feet, each for 1 Focus under the timing rules above.

Trapcraft

You know 3 Ranger Traps and may maintain 2 active traps. Choose from Snare, Spike, Disrupting, Revealing, and Repulsion when selecting known traps.

Exploit Opening

Once on your turn, one ranged attack against a creature that triggered your trap since the end of your previous turn can be made with Advantage.

Level 2 — Hunter’s Awareness and Quick Reposition

Aether Disturbance

You sense an Invisible creature within 10 feet if it is not behind Full Cover. You know its occupied space, it cannot become Hidden from you solely because it is Invisible, and you do not suffer Disadvantage on ranged attacks against it solely for that invisibility.

Watchful Hunter

If you are not already proficient, add PB to a WIS (Perception) or WIS (Survival) check used to detect concealed creatures, ambushes, tracks, disturbed terrain, or hidden traps. This does not stack with proficiency.

Quick Reposition

Once on each of your turns after making a ranged weapon attack, move 5 feet without provoking an Opportunity Attack from the creature you attacked. This costs no action and no Focus. Quick Reposition and Evasive Step do not stack on the same attack unless a later feature explicitly allows it.

Level 3 — Ranger Specialization and Additional Trap

Additional Trap

Learn one additional Ranger Trap, bringing your base total to 4 known.

Choose Deadeye, Trapper, or Windrunner. Specialization milestones occur at Levels 3, 6, 10, and 14.

Deadeye — Patient Aim

Once on each of your turns, if you moved no more than half your Speed before making a ranged weapon attack, empower one hit: add 1d6 damage, or 1d8 if the target triggered one of your traps since the end of your previous turn. This costs no Focus.

Trapper — Expanded Trapcraft and Potent Traps

Learn 2 additional Ranger Traps beyond the normal class total, and increase your active-trap limit by 1. Once on each of your turns when a creature fails a save against one of your traps, choose one Potent rider: Pressured Position reduces Speed by an additional 5 feet; Exposed Footing gives +1 to the next ranged attack against it before your next turn; or Disrupted Recovery prevents the creature from benefiting from Dash before the start of your next turn.

Windrunner — Mobile Archer and Running Shot

Quick Reposition increases to 10 feet for you. Once on each of your turns after moving at least 10 feet from your turn-start position, the next ranged weapon hit that turn deals +1d6 damage.

Level 4 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Feat / ASI

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 5 — Extra Attack and Improved Focus Techniques

Extra Attack

When you take the Attack action, make two ranged or otherwise legal weapon attacks instead of one. Ranger never gains a third standard Attack-action attack.

Improved Focus Techniques

Precision Shot becomes +1d8, Pinning Shot becomes Speed -15 feet, and Evasive Step becomes 15 feet.

Level 6 — Specialization Decision / Advancement

Deadeye — Steady Hand

If you have not moved before a ranged attack on your turn, choose one benefit for that attack: ignore Half Cover; or, if the target triggered one of your traps since the end of your previous turn, the attack scores a critical hit on 19–20.

Trapper — Hair-Trigger

Once per round when a hostile creature fails a saving throw against one of your Ranger Traps, use your Reaction to make one ranged weapon attack against that creature if it is within range and can be legally targeted.

Windrunner — Slipstream Shot

Once on each of your turns, if you moved at least 15 feet before a ranged weapon attack, one hit deals +1d6 damage and you may move 5 feet after the hit without provoking Opportunity Attacks from that target.

Level 7 — Skirmisher’s Escape and Additional Trap

Skirmisher’s Escape

When a hostile creature ends its movement within 10 feet of you, use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed. This movement does not provoke an Opportunity Attack from the triggering creature, grants no attack, and does not ignore difficult terrain or barriers.

Additional Trap

Learn one additional Ranger Trap, bringing the base class total to 5 known.

Level 8 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Feat / ASI

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 9 — Hunter’s Rhythm and Advanced Trapcraft

Hunter’s Rhythm

Once on each of your turns after you hit a creature and satisfy at least one tactical condition, regain 1 Focus: the target triggered one of your traps since the end of your previous turn; you moved at least 15 feet from the position where you began the turn before hitting; or the target’s Speed is currently reduced by a Ranger feature. Hunter’s Rhythm restores no more than 1 Focus on each of your turns.

Advanced Trapcraft

Learn one additional Ranger Trap, bringing the base total to 6. You may now choose Blinding Trap, Binding Trap, or Aether Disruption Trap whenever you learn a Ranger Trap.

Level 10 — Specialization Decision / Advancement

Starfall Volley — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate ranged weapon attack against a creature within the weapon's normal range. Starfall Volley ignores Half Cover; Full Cover remains absolute. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Damage. On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed additional damage of the weapon's type: 100 at Levels 10-14, 120 at Levels 15-19, or 140 at Level 20.

Falling Volley. On a hit, every other hostile creature within 15 feet of the primary target makes a DEX save against your Ranger Class Save DC. A creature takes fixed damage of the weapon's type: 40 at Levels 10-14, 50 at Levels 15-19, or 60 at Level 20 on a failure, or half that amount on a success. The radius becomes 20 feet at Level 20.

Deadeye — Kill Zone

Once on each of your turns when Patient Aim damages a target that triggered one of your traps since the end of your previous turn, add one additional weapon damage die to that hit. For this bonus damage instance, you may ignore resistance to the weapon’s damage type, but never immunity.

Trapper — Chain Reaction

Once on each of your turns when one of your traps triggers, you may activate one different active trap within 15 feet of the triggering trap against the same creature if that creature is in or legally enters the second trap’s trigger space. The second trap resolves normally. A trap activated by Chain Reaction cannot trigger another Chain Reaction.

Windrunner — Untouchable Momentum

Once on each of your turns after you move at least 20 feet and then hit with a ranged weapon attack, choose one: move 10 feet without provoking from the target; gain +1 AC until the start of your next turn; or restore 1 Focus if you currently have none.

Level 11 — Focus Mastery and Exploit Weakness

Focus Mastery

Once on each of your turns when you spend Focus on a Ranger technique, reduce that expenditure by 1, minimum 0. Only one Focus expenditure each turn receives this reduction. A technique reduced to 0 still counts as using the technique but not as spending Focus.

Exploit Weakness

Whenever a creature triggers one of your Ranger Traps, it becomes Exposed to you until the end of your next turn. Once during that duration when you hit it with a ranged weapon attack, add 2d8 damage to the attack; then that exposure ends. You can deal Exploit Weakness damage only once on each of your turns. Your base active-trap limit increases from 2 to 3.

Level 12 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Feat / ASI

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 13 — Master Hunter and Additional Trap

Master Hunter

Aether Disturbance increases to 30 feet. Once on each of your turns, when you have a reasonable indication a Hidden creature may be present or designate an area you are actively searching, use Survey the Field to make a WIS (Perception) check to locate it within 60 feet without using an action. Resolve normally against Stealth. You also gain Surefooted Pursuit: nonmagical difficult terrain no longer costs additional movement, though hazards, barriers, unsupported gaps, and magical terrain still function.

Additional Trap

Learn one additional Ranger Trap, bringing your base total to 7 known.

Level 14 — Final Specialization Decision / Advancement

Deadeye — Perfect Shot

Once on each of your turns when you make a ranged weapon attack after moving no more than half your Speed, spend 1 Focus to choose one shot before the roll: Piercing Shot ignores Half Cover; Killing Shot adds 2d8 damage on a hit against a creature at half HP or fewer; Unerring Shot gains Advantage unless the attack already has Disadvantage from a non-cover source.

Trapper — Master Trapper and Remote Trigger

Your active-trap limit increases by 1 again. Once on each of your turns as a Bonus Action, remotely trigger one active trap you can perceive within 60 feet if a hostile creature currently occupies its trigger space. Resolve that trap normally against that creature.

Windrunner — Gale Step and Flowing Assault

Once on each of your turns after a ranged weapon hit, move 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from that target. If you moved at least 30 feet during the turn before hitting with Slipstream Shot, Slipstream Shot’s bonus damage becomes 2d6 for that attack.

Level 15 — Relentless Hunt

Relentless Hunt

Once on each of your turns when your ranged weapon attack or Ranger Trap reduces a hostile creature to 0 HP, choose another hostile creature you can perceive within 60 feet as your Quarry until the end of your next turn. You may immediately move up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from one creature. Your first ranged attack against the Quarry before the effect ends has Advantage; if it hits, regain 1 Focus. Hunter’s Rhythm and this refund can both occur in the same turn, but normal maximum Focus still applies.

Ascendant Starfall Volley

Your Starfall Volley now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Feat / ASI

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 17 — Apex Focus Techniques and Perfect Exploitation

Apex Focus Techniques

Precision Shot becomes +2d8. Pinning Shot becomes Speed -20 feet; if this reduces the target’s Speed to 0, it also cannot Dash until the start of your next turn. Evasive Step becomes 25 feet, does not provoke Opportunity Attacks during that movement, and ignores nonmagical difficult terrain.

Perfect Exploitation

Exploit Weakness increases from 2d8 to 3d8. You may deal Exploit Weakness damage twice on your turn if the two instances affect different Exposed creatures; each exposure is still consumed after use.

Level 18 — Apex Hunter

Apex Hunter

Aether Disturbance increases to 60 feet. Survey the Field is made with Advantage. A creature you successfully locate with Survey the Field cannot become Hidden from you until the start of your next turn unless it gains Full Cover. Skirmisher’s Escape now lets you move up to your full Speed and ignore nonmagical difficult terrain during that Reaction movement.

Level 19 — Empyrean Boon

Boon

Gain the universal Empyrean Boon.

Level 20 — Perfect Ranger

Prepared Battlefield

When Initiative is rolled for genuine combat, you may immediately deploy up to two known Ranger Traps in legal unoccupied spaces you can perceive within 30 feet, without using an action. You cannot place them directly under a hostile creature, and normal active-trap limits still apply.

Perfect Focus

When you roll Initiative, if you have fewer than 3 Focus, gain enough Focus to reach 3, up to your maximum.

Perfect Hunter’s Rhythm

The first time on each of your turns Hunter’s Rhythm would trigger, choose one reward instead of only regaining Focus: regain 1 Focus; gain Advantage on your next ranged weapon attack before the end of the turn; or move 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from one creature you can see.

Apex Starfall Volley

Your Starfall Volley uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Specialization Reassignment

Initial choice — Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is the final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Example Combat Sequences

  1. Baseline setup: deploy a trap before the enemy reaches your lane. When it triggers, use Exploit Opening for Advantage, then spend Focus on Precision Shot or Pinning Shot only if the extra damage/control changes the turn.
  2. Level 9 sustain: move at least 15 feet from your starting position, hit with a ranged attack, then regain 1 Focus through Hunter’s Rhythm. This supports an active skirmishing loop rather than infinite Focus from standing still and firing.
  3. Level 11 trap payoff: a hostile triggers a trap and becomes Exposed. Make an Exploit Opening attack with Advantage and add 2d8 from Exploit Weakness on the hit; use Focus Mastery to reduce one technique to 0 if useful.
  4. Trapper: maintain a wider network, use Hair-Trigger when an enemy truly fails a trap save, then use Chain Reaction once on your turn to sequence a second legal trap without allowing recursion.
  5. Windrunner: move before the shot, apply Slipstream/Flowing Assault, then use the granted safe movement to preserve distance and qualify movement-based Focus recovery.
