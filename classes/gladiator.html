Gladiator

“Nothing can stand in my way!”

The premier sustained melee damage class, built around deliberate attack sequences, Momentum, Chain Links, and Finishers.

Class Overview

The Gladiator is the premier sustained melee damage class. It wins through sequences rather than single isolated attacks: open a Chain, spend Momentum on Links that alter the battlefield, then cash the sequence out with a Finisher. Polearms provide the highest sustained weapon output, while weapon discipline and specialization determine whether the Gladiator emphasizes execution, cleaving pressure, or relentless pursuit.

Class Identity

Gladiators are Daevas who turn martial mastery into relentless battlefield pressure. They are not defined by a single weapon or one decisive blow, but by the ability to seize momentum, force an opening, and keep an enemy under pressure until the fight breaks in their favor. In Wings of Atreia, this identity is expressed through Momentum and Chain Attacks: the Gladiator becomes more dangerous when attacks, movement, and finishers are linked into a deliberate sequence.

Tradition & Outlook

The Gladiator tradition descends from the broad warrior disciplines of Atreia and is associated with soldiers, arena fighters, legion veterans, weapon masters, and Daevas who believe that skill must survive contact with a real battlefield. Gladiators commonly value courage, physical discipline, adaptability, and decisive action. Some fight for glory or personal mastery; others see themselves as the hard edge of a legion, the Daeva who advances when everyone else needs the line to move.

Appearance & Adventuring

Gladiators usually present themselves as practical combatants: heavy armor, battle-worn weapons, and equipment chosen for reach, leverage, or sustained close combat. Polearms and other approved Gladiator weapons may suggest very different fighting schools, but the class is recognized by an aggressive, forward-driving presence. A Gladiator adventures to test mastery, protect comrades through offensive pressure, earn renown, serve a military cause, or become strong enough to face the threats of the Abyss and Balaurea directly.

Core Engine — Momentum and Chains

Momentum represents the Gladiator forcing the battle forward. It exists only in genuine combat and resets to 0 when combat ends. Normal Momentum does not decay simply because you failed to spend it; later features care about maintaining an active Chain instead.

  • First melee hit on your own turn: gain 1 Momentum.
  • Aggressive Momentum: once on each of your turns, gain 1 additional Momentum if a melee hit follows at least 15 feet of movement toward that target, is a critical hit, or reduces a hostile creature to 0 HP. Multiple qualifying conditions on one attack still grant only 1 additional Momentum.
  • Maximum Momentum: 3 at Levels 1–4; 4 at 5–8; 5 at 9–12; 6 at 13–16; 7 at 17–20.
  • A single attack cannot normally grant more than the Momentum allowed by the generation rules above. Refunds occur after the triggering feature resolves and are not new Momentum-generation events unless stated otherwise.

Chain Attacks

A Chain begins when you declare a successful melee hit on your own turn to be an Opener. You can maintain only one Chain at a time. The Chain belongs to you, not to a particular enemy, so later Links may strike another legal target. A miss does not automatically end the Chain. It lasts until the end of your next turn, until you use a Finisher, abandon it, or become unable to continue it.

  • One Chain technique may modify a given attack.
  • Chain Links and Finishers normally apply only to attacks made on your own turn.
  • A Finisher ends the Chain after it resolves.
  • Chain Dice are d6 at Levels 1–4, d8 at 5–10, d10 at 11–16, and d12 at 17–20.
  • Chain Dice added directly to the triggering weapon attack double on a critical hit. Independent secondary damage does not.

Coverage. This reference covers every class option in the current manuscript that creates or materially changes an Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, triggered player choice, or signature-resource expenditure, refund, recovery, overflow conversion, or combat starting state. A level that only changes passive numerical scaling, grants a Feat/ASI, or automatically upgrades an already listed option does not need a duplicate row; use the level progression for the new value.

Timing rule. “Triggered / No Action” means the option modifies or responds to its stated event without consuming your Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction. Specific feature text remains authoritative.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Momentum, Chain Attacks, Battle Rush

Momentum

Your maximum Momentum is 3. Your first melee hit on your turn grants 1 Momentum, and Aggressive Momentum can grant 1 additional Momentum once on your turn from a qualifying aggressive hit.

Chain Attacks

You gain the Chain rules and the Level 1 techniques Driving Strike, Sweeping Strike, and Crushing Finisher. At this level your Chain Die is d6 and a Chain may contain one or more Links before the Finisher, subject to available attacks and Momentum.

Battle Rush

Moving at least 15 feet toward a creature before hitting it with a melee weapon attack satisfies the movement condition for Aggressive Momentum. The movement must be actual movement through legal spaces; forced movement does not count.

Level 2 — Combat Discipline

Choose one discipline. Heavy Weapon lets you reroll one weapon damage die showing 1 or 2 once per attack; it does not reroll a Chain Die. Polearm Discipline increases the reach of your polearm melee attacks by 5 feet during your own turn. Twin Blade adds your STR modifier to the damage of your additional light-weapon attack; two Shortswords are explicitly a valid Twin Blade setup. Defensive Weapon Discipline grants +1 AC while wielding a melee weapon and not using a shield. You may retrain this choice when you gain a class Feat/ASI.

Level 3 — Gladiator Specialization

Choose Destroyer, Tempest, or Warpath. The choice is permanent unless the campaign later introduces an explicit retraining rule. Your specialization grants new features at Levels 3, 6, 10, and 14.

Destroyer — Relentless Finisher

Once on each of your turns when Crushing Finisher hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, regain 1 Momentum after the Finisher resolves.

Tempest — Widening Sweep

When you use Sweeping Strike, you may choose one additional eligible secondary creature. That second creature takes one Chain Die of damage without adding STR.

Warpath — Charging Assault

Battle Rush now requires only 10 feet of movement toward the target. After that qualifying hit resolves, you may move up to 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature you hit, and the movement must leave you closer to that creature or another hostile creature you can perceive.

Level 4 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

Level 5 — Extra Attack, Improved Momentum, Hamstring Strike

Extra Attack

When you take the Attack action on your turn, make two attacks instead of one. This is the Gladiator’s standard attack ceiling; the class does not later gain a third ordinary Attack-action attack.

Improved Momentum and Chain Die

Maximum Momentum increases to 4 and your Chain Die becomes d8. You can now more reliably create an Opener with the first attack and advance the Chain with the second.

Hamstring Strike

You unlock Hamstring Strike for 1 Momentum. On a qualifying melee hit, add 1d8 Chain damage and reduce Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. The Speed reduction follows the class non-stacking rule.

Level 6 — Specialization Advancement

Destroyer — Executioner’s Weight

When Crushing Finisher hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, add 1 additional current Chain Die to the attack.

Tempest — Storm of Steel

Every secondary creature damaged by Sweeping Strike now takes one current Chain Die + your STR modifier, including the additional creature from Widening Sweep.

Warpath — Relentless Charge

When moving toward a creature for Battle Rush, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain. Opportunity Attacks made against you during that qualifying approach have Disadvantage.

Level 7 — Relentless Advance

When a hostile creature within your melee reach willingly moves away from you, use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed. You must end closer to that creature than where you started and cannot end farther from it. This movement does not itself provoke an Opportunity Attack from the triggering creature and grants no attack.

Level 8 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 9 — Momentum Mastery

Greater Momentum

Maximum Momentum increases to 5.

Momentum Mastery

Once on each of your turns when you spend Momentum on a Chain Link that advances an already active Chain, regain 1 Momentum after that Link resolves. The refund cannot exceed your maximum and cannot recursively trigger another Gladiator rider.

Level 10 — Specialization Advancement

Heavenbreaker — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation — 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, move up to your Speed toward a creature you can see without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You must end within your melee reach, then make one Ultimate melee weapon attack against it. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Damage. On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed additional damage of the weapon's type: 100 at Levels 10–14, 120 at Levels 15–19, or 140 at Level 20.

Shockwave. On a hit, every other hostile creature within 10 feet of the primary target makes a DEX save against your Gladiator Class Save DC. A creature takes fixed Force damage of 40 at Levels 10–14, 50 at Levels 15–19, or 60 at Level 20 on a failure, or half that amount on a success. The radius becomes 15 feet at Level 15. At Level 20, the primary target is also knocked Prone if it is Huge or smaller.

Destroyer — Execution Window

When a Finisher attack hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, that Finisher scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

Tempest — Cyclone Sweep

Sweeping Strike secondary targets no longer need to be within 5 feet of the original target; each only needs to be within your melee reach.

Warpath — Overrun

When a Battle Rush hit follows at least 20 feet of movement toward the target, the target makes a STR save. On a failure, choose to push it 10 feet or knock it Prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage on the save.

Level 11 — Greater Chains

Greater Chain Die

Your Chain Die becomes d10.

Staggering Strike

Spend 1 Momentum on a qualifying Link to add 1d10 and prevent the target from taking Reactions until the start of your next turn.

Devastating Finisher

After at least two prior successful Links in the current Chain, spend 3 Momentum on a melee hit to add 3d10. The target makes a STR save; on a failure it falls Prone. A creature more than one size larger has Advantage. The Chain then ends.

Level 12 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 13 — Unbroken Momentum

Greater Momentum

Maximum Momentum increases to 6.

Unbroken Momentum

A successful Chain Link refreshes the duration of your active Chain. In addition, if you begin your turn with an active Chain and 0 Momentum, gain 1 Momentum. This start-turn recovery can occur only once per turn and cannot exceed your maximum.

Level 14 — Final Specialization Advancement

Destroyer — Perfect Execution

Executioner’s Weight is replaced by Perfect Execution. Any Finisher that hits a creature at half maximum HP or fewer adds 1 current Chain Die. If that Finisher reduces the target to 0 HP, regain 2 Momentum instead of the 1 Momentum refund from Relentless Finisher, once per turn.

Tempest — Maelstrom Sweep

Sweeping Strike may now damage up to three eligible secondary creatures, each for one current Chain Die + STR. If at least two secondary creatures actually take damage, regain 1 Momentum after the Sweep resolves, once per turn.

Warpath — Breakthrough

After a creature fails the save against Overrun, you may move up to 10 feet. You may pass through the space of the affected creature if it is no more than one size larger, but cannot end there. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks from that creature and should end closer to another hostile creature when one is reasonably reachable.

Level 15 — Endless Assault

Endless Assault

Once on each of your turns after a Finisher hits and fully resolves, you may spend 1 Momentum to make one melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action. This attack cannot carry a Chain Link or Finisher, but it may be declared the Opener of a new Chain after the previous Chain has ended.

Ascendant Heavenbreaker

Your Heavenbreaker now uses all Levels 15–19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16 — Feat / Ability Score Improvement

Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 17 — Apex Chains

Apex Momentum and Chain Die

Maximum Momentum increases to 7 and your Chain Die becomes d12. Your active Chain may contain up to four successful Links before its Finisher.

Perfect Sequence

After you successfully resolve three different Chain Links within the same Chain, reduce the Momentum cost of the next Finisher in that Chain by 1, minimum 1. The reduction is consumed when that Finisher is used or the Chain ends.

Titanic Finisher

After at least three prior Links, spend 4 Momentum on a melee hit to add 4d12. The target makes a STR save. On a failure choose: knock it Prone and push it 20 feet; knock it Prone without pushing; or push it 30 feet. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage. The Chain ends after resolution.

Level 18 — Master of the Frontline

Frontline Stability

You have Advantage on checks and saving throws made to resist being knocked Prone, shoved, or forcibly moved.

Additional Martial Reaction

You gain one additional Reaction each round. This extra Reaction can be used only for an Opportunity Attack or Relentless Advance. You cannot spend both Reactions on the same triggering event, and both refresh at the start of your turn.

Level 19 — Empyrean Boon

Gain the universal Empyrean Boon available at Level 19.

Level 20 — Perfect Gladiator

Battle-Ready Momentum

When you roll Initiative for genuine combat, you gain 3 Momentum.

Perfect Finisher Flow

Once on each of your turns after a Finisher successfully hits and resolves, regain 2 Momentum. This is a refund, not Momentum generation, and cannot recursively trigger itself.

Perfect Endless Assault

Endless Assault no longer costs Momentum. It still grants only one Bonus Action melee attack, still cannot carry a Link or Finisher, and still does not grant another Attack action.

Apex Heavenbreaker

Your Heavenbreaker uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Example Combat Sequences

  1. Level 5 baseline: move 15 feet toward a hostile, hit to generate Momentum and open the Chain, apply Driving Strike, make the second attack and apply Hamstring Strike, then continue the Chain next turn if no Finisher was affordable.
  2. Level 11 prepared Chain: Opener → Driving Strike → Hamstring Strike across the available attacks/turns → Devastating Finisher once two prior Links exist. Momentum Mastery refunds one Link expenditure along the way.
  3. Destroyer: build a full Chain and delay the Finisher until the target reaches half HP, then use the widened critical window and extra Chain Die.
  4. Tempest: position a polearm so multiple hostiles remain in reach, then use Sweeping Strike each turn to pressure clustered enemies without creating extra attack rolls.
  5. Warpath: approach through difficult terrain, trigger Battle Rush, force an Overrun save, then use Breakthrough to pass the target and threaten the formation behind it.
