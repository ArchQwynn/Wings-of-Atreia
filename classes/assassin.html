The Assassin is a precision melee striker that builds target-bound Rune Marks, converts them into burst damage or execution riders, and uses Shadow Step to remain difficult to pin down. Unlike a conventional Extra Attack martial, its Level 5 Assassination Sequence is a conditional follow-up attack tied to a successful Sneak Attack.

Class Identity

Assassins are precision predators who win through positioning, timing, concealment, and carefully prepared execution. Their art is not simply “attacking from stealth.” An Assassin studies a target, creates the right opening, carves Rune Marks, and converts preparation into a sudden lethal advantage. Their greatest strength is the ability to decide where and when a close-range confrontation becomes dangerous.

Tradition & Outlook

The Assassin tradition descends from Atreia’s scout disciplines and is practiced by infiltrators, covert operatives, hunters of dangerous individuals, intelligence agents, and Daevas who favor certainty over spectacle. Assassins often prize patience, awareness, self-control, and economy of motion. Their motives vary widely: service to a faction, revenge, professional duty, protection through preemptive action, or the pursuit of perfect technique.

Appearance & Adventuring

Assassins favor equipment that permits mobility and unobstructed movement, with Shortswords and Daggers forming the class’s approved weapon identity in Wings of Atreia. Their appearance may be subdued or ceremonial, but practical Assassins avoid anything that compromises movement or control. They adventure when a threat must be found before it strikes, a route must be infiltrated, an enemy leader must be neutralized, or a mystery demands someone comfortable operating unseen.

Core Engine — Sneak Attack and Rune Marks

Sneak Attack applies once on each of your own turns when an eligible finesse melee weapon attack has Advantage, or when another qualifying enemy is adjacent to the target and you do not have Disadvantage. Sneak Attack damage scales from 1d6 at Level 1 to 10d6 at Level 19.

  • Rune Carving: once on your turn, a qualifying melee hit that actually deals Sneak Attack places 1 Rune Mark after that hit resolves. Alternatively, hitting the same creature with two different melee attacks during your turn can place 1 Rune Mark after the second hit.
  • Normal attack-based Rune placement is limited to 1 Mark per turn unless a feature explicitly increases it.
  • Newly created Rune Marks cannot be consumed by the same hit that created them.
  • If you exceed your current marked-target limit, choose one previously marked creature and remove all of your Marks from it.
  • Marks disappear when consumed, when the marked creature is defeated, or when the encounter ends.

Rune Burst and Critical Hits

When you hit a creature bearing your Rune Marks, you may consume 1, 2, or 3 Marks to add the same number of Rune Dice to that weapon attack. Rune Dice added directly to the attack double on a critical hit. Secondary status riders of an Execution are not doubled. A Rune Burst resolves as part of the hit and therefore cannot consume a Mark that is only created after that same hit resolves.

Specialization Reassignment

Initial choice — Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is the final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Sneak Attack, Rune Carving, Rune Burst, Shadow Step

Sneak Attack

Once on each of your own turns, add 1d6 damage to one eligible finesse melee weapon hit.

Rune Marks and Rune Carving

Your Rune Die is d4 and you may maintain Marks on one creature. After a qualifying Sneak Attack hit, place 1 Rune Mark. As an alternate carving route, two different melee hits on the same creature in one turn place 1 Mark after the second hit.

Rune Burst

On a hit against a marked creature, consume 1–3 Marks to add the same number of d4 Rune Dice to that attack.

Shadow Step

Once on each of your turns after a qualifying Sneak Attack hit, move up to 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature you hit. This is movement, not teleportation, until Ghostwalk improves it later.

Level 2 — Predator’s Senses

Aether Trace

You sense an Invisible creature within 10 feet if it is not behind Full Cover. You know the space it occupies, it cannot become Hidden from you solely because it is Invisible, and you do not suffer Disadvantage on attacks against it solely from that invisibility.

Predator’s Search

When you make a Perception or Investigation check specifically to find Hidden creatures, tracks, ambushes, or deliberate concealment, add your PB if you are not already proficient in the check. This does not stack with proficiency.

Level 5 — Assassination Sequence and Improved Runes

Assassination Sequence

Once on each of your turns, immediately after a melee weapon hit that actually deals Sneak Attack damage, make one additional eligible finesse melee attack. It requires no Action or Bonus Action. This follow-up attack cannot deal Sneak Attack and cannot trigger another Assassination Sequence.

Improved Runes

Your Rune Die becomes d6 and Sneak Attack becomes 3d6. Because the Sequence creates a second melee hit, you now have a reliable route to the two-hit Rune Carving condition on the same target.

Death's Eclipse — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet and teleport to a legal unoccupied space within 5 feet of it. If no such space exists, you cannot choose that creature. Make one Ultimate attack against it with a Dagger or Shortsword. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Execution Damage. On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed additional damage of the weapon's type: 144 at Levels 10-14, 168 at Levels 15-19, or 192 at Level 20.

Eclipsed Response. On a hit, the target cannot take Reactions until the end of your next turn. Death's Eclipse has no area-damage or allied-support component; its power is concentrated into the primary target.

Level 11 — Greater Rune Execution and Execution Strike

Greater Rune Die

Your Rune Die becomes d8 and Sneak Attack becomes 6d6.

Greater Executions

When you consume all 3 Marks on a melee hit, you may choose a Greater Execution instead of ordinary Rune Burst: Rupture adds 4 Rune Dice; Crippling adds 3 Rune Dice and forces a CON save, with failure setting Speed to 0 and preventing Reactions until the start of your next turn; Aether Severance adds 2 Rune Dice and forces a CON save, with failure preventing spellcasting until the start of your next turn. Save DC is your Assassin Class Save DC. These hard-edged effects are brief and save-dependent.

Execution Strike

Once on each of your turns after Masterful Carving causes a creature to reach 3 Rune Marks, you may use your Bonus Action to make one melee weapon attack against it. The attack cannot deal Sneak Attack or trigger Assassination Sequence, but it may consume the Marks with Rune Burst or an Execution. If a light-weapon Bonus Action attack is also available, choose one; they do not stack.

Level 13 — Ghostwalk

Ghostwalk

Shadow Step reaches 30 feet. When you use it, you may treat the movement as a short teleport to a visible legal unoccupied space within range instead of normal movement. It cannot cross a barrier that explicitly prevents teleportation or Aetheric movement, and it cannot place you into an unseen or illegal space. Aether Trace improves to 20 feet for non-Nightstalker targets. Sneak Attack becomes 7d6.

Level 15 — Relentless Execution

Relentless Execution

When a melee attack that consumes at least one Rune Mark reduces its target to 0 HP, choose another hostile creature within 30 feet. If you consumed 1 or 2 Marks, place 1 Mark on the new creature; if you consumed all 3, place 2 Marks. You may then use an additional Shadow Step/Ghostwalk movement toward the new target if you can end closer to it. This transfer cannot recursively trigger itself from the same attack. Sneak Attack becomes 8d6.

Ascendant Death's Eclipse

Your Death's Eclipse now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 17 — Apex Rune Execution and Perfect Marking

Apex Rune Mastery

Your Rune Die becomes d10, your marked-target limit increases to 3, and Sneak Attack becomes 9d6.

Apex Executions

Oblivion adds 6 Rune Dice when consuming all 3 Marks. Binding Execution adds 5 Rune Dice and forces a CON save; failure sets Speed to 0, prevents Reactions, and prevents becoming Invisible, teleporting, or using Aetheric-displacement movement until the start of your next turn. Null Aether adds 4 Rune Dice and forces a CON save; failure ends Concentration and prevents spellcasting until the start of your next turn. These effects remain one-turn, save-dependent apex control rather than indefinite lockdown.

Perfect Marking

When your qualifying Sneak Attack and Assassination Sequence both hit the same creature, place 2 additional Rune Marks after the follow-up resolves instead of the 1 additional Mark from Masterful Carving. Combined with normal Rune Carving, a clean two-hit sequence can prepare all 3 Marks, but those Marks still cannot be consumed by either hit that created them.

Level 18 — Unseen Predator

Unseen Predator

Aether Trace range becomes 30 feet against ordinary targets. An unseen creature cannot gain Advantage on attacks against you solely because it is unseen. You may attempt to Hide while only lightly obscured. If you begin your turn Hidden or Invisible, your first eligible melee attack that turn has Advantage. Nightstalker retains its larger quarry-specific Aether Trace distance.

Level 19 — Empyrean Boon

Boon and Sneak Attack

Gain the universal Empyrean Boon. Sneak Attack reaches its final 10d6.

Level 20 — Perfect Assassin

Perfect Preparation

When you roll Initiative for genuine combat, choose up to three hostile creatures you can perceive within 60 feet. Place 1 Rune Mark on each. This cannot exceed your marked-target limit.

Flawless Setup

The first time on each of your turns that a qualifying Sneak Attack hits a creature bearing none of your Rune Marks before the hit, normal Rune Carving places 2 Marks instead of 1 after the hit.

Perfect Transition

After you consume all 3 Marks with a Greater or Apex Execution, choose one hostile creature within 30 feet of you or the executed target. If the executed target survives, place 1 Mark on the chosen creature; if it is reduced to 0 HP, place 2 instead. You may then Shadow Step/Ghostwalk toward the new target if legal. This does not recursively create another execution.

Apex Death's Eclipse

Your Death's Eclipse uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Example Combat Sequences

  1. Level 5: land Sneak Attack, carve 1 Mark, immediately make Assassination Sequence; if the follow-up also hits the same target, the two-hit alternative can establish the next Mark on later levels. Shadow Step can reposition after the qualifying Sneak hit.
  2. Level 9: Sneak Attack + Sequence both hit the same target. Normal Rune Carving places 1 Mark after the Sneak hit, then Masterful Carving places another after the follow-up, preparing 2 Marks in one turn.
  3. Level 11: if that sequence brings the creature to 3 Marks, spend your Bonus Action on Execution Strike and consume the prepared pattern with a Greater Execution.
  4. Executioner: delay the three-Mark cash-out until the target is wounded, then combine Deep Carving, the expanded crit range, and Greater/Apex Execution dice.
  5. Phantom: Sneak Attack -> Shadow Step -> Hide through Vanishing Step -> become Invisible through Veiled Passage -> choose when to re-engage.
  6. Nightstalker: place a Mark to establish quarry, use Aether Trace/tracking to deny concealment, and save your Reaction for Relentless Quarry or No Escape rather than an ordinary Opportunity Attack.
