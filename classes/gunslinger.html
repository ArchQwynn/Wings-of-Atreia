Class Identity

Gunslingers are technist marksmen who turn Aether-assisted firearms into speed, precision, and controlled ranged pressure. Pistols support rapid, mobile sequences through Quick Shot and Akimbo, while Muskets provide deliberate two-handed fire that concentrates Tempo into stronger individual hits through Deliberate Shot. Tempo represents the Gunslinger’s ability to build combat rhythm through successful action rather than treating every shot as an isolated event.

Tradition & Outlook

Gunslingers belong to Atreia’s newer technological traditions. The wider Aion tradition describes early firearm research being dismissed as technist tinkering until battlefield success forced major institutions to recognize the discipline. Wings of Atreia keeps that identity of innovation earning legitimacy through results while using Pistols and Muskets as the class’s only firearm categories. Gunslingers often value nerve, technical familiarity, accuracy, independence, and adaptability.

Appearance & Adventuring

Gunslingers favor mobile equipment, ammunition management, firearm maintenance, and a practical awareness of range and firing position. Their weapons can make them look strikingly modern beside older martial traditions, but their discipline remains Aetheric and distinctly Atreian. They adventure to test new techniques, serve as ranged specialists, hunt dangerous targets, protect expeditions from positions others cannot exploit, or demonstrate that innovation has earned its place in the war for Atreia.

Core Engine — Tempo, Firearms, and Reload

The Gunslinger is a mobile firearm martial whose power comes from maintaining Tempo through successful firearm play. Firearms are class-supported Gunslinger weapons; carrying two firearms does not itself grant extra attacks.

Pistols

Ranged Gunslinger One-handed Weapon Attack. Range 30/90 feet. Attack modifier = PB + DEX. On a hit, deal 1d8 + DEX modifier Piercing damage. A Gunslinger can wield two Pistols, but doing so does not itself grant extra attacks. Pistols are compatible with Quick Shot and Akimbo.

Muskets

Ranged Gunslinger Two-handed Weapon Attack. Range 60/180 feet. Attack modifier = PB + DEX. On a hit, deal 1d12 + DEX modifier Piercing damage. Ammunition capacity is fixed at 2. Muskets cannot use Quick Shot or Akimbo. Gunslinger ammunition-restoration features can restore Musket shots normally, but a Musket can never hold more than 2 shots. Standard one-handed firearm capacity increases do not apply to Muskets.

Reload

Every firearm attack consumes one shot. A firearm with no shots remaining is Empty and cannot make firearm attacks until at least one shot is restored.

  • Normal Bonus Action Reload: an Empty firearm is restored to its full Reload capacity.
  • Ammunition restoration is not the Reload action unless a feature explicitly says so.
  • Ammunition-restoration features can restore shots to either Pistols or Muskets up to that firearm's maximum capacity. A Musket's maximum capacity remains 2.
  • Switching between carried one-handed Gunslinger firearms as part of a firearm attack requires no separate object interaction.

Standard one-handed firearm capacity progression: Ammo Capacity 4 at Levels 1-4, Ammo Capacity 5 at 5-8, Ammo Capacity 6 at 9-14, and Ammo Capacity 7 at 15-20. Increasing capacity does not automatically reload an Empty firearm.

Tempo

Tempo is a genuine-combat resource representing firing rhythm, accuracy, and momentum. It begins at 0, resets to 0 when combat ends, and artificial initiative cannot generate it.

  • Maximum Tempo: Levels 1-4 = 3; 5-8 = 4; 9-12 = 5; 13-16 = 6; 17-20 = 7.
  • Clean Hit: after you hit a hostile creature with a Gunslinger firearm attack, gain 1 Tempo, subject to the generation limits below.
  • Tactical Shot: when a Gunslinger class feature tied to a firearm attack meaningfully forces movement, applies a meaningful hostile penalty, or creates at least 5 feet of safe movement, gain 1 Tempo, subject to the generation limits below.
  • A single attack can never generate more than 1 normal Tempo even if it satisfies multiple conditions.
  • Levels 1-4: normal own-turn Tempo generation is capped at 1.
  • From Level 5: up to 2 Tempo on your own turn, but from distinct qualifying events.
  • From Level 17: a third Tempo on your turn is possible only from an explicitly named advanced feature.
  • Outside your turn, gain at most 1 Tempo per round unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.

Tempo never falls below 0. If you end your turn without making a Gunslinger firearm attack or successfully using a Gunslinger firearm technique, lose 1 Tempo. At Level 13 this rule improves through Sustained Tempo.

Tempo Refunds

A Tempo refund is not Tempo generation unless a feature explicitly says otherwise. Refunds therefore do not trigger effects that require gaining Tempo.

Universal Gunslinger Safeguards

  • A secondary Gunslinger effect produced by Tempo gain, Tempo refund, ammunition restoration, movement, specialization riders, or technique riders does not recursively trigger another Gunslinger rider unless explicitly stated.
  • Flat Gunslinger class-feature penalties to the same statistic do not stack unless explicitly stated; use the largest.
  • Flat Gunslinger class-feature bonuses to the same attack roll, AC, saving throw, or similar statistic do not stack unless explicitly stated; use the largest.
  • Free techniques still count as techniques even when their Tempo cost becomes 0.
  • One attack may trigger multiple riders only where those riders are explicitly compatible.
  • No Gunslinger feature grants a second Attack Action.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Quick Shot, Deliberate Shot, Evasive Shot, Disrupting Shot, Fast Hands, Close-Quarters Gunplay, Running Fire, Precision Reload

Quick Shot

Cost: 1 Tempo. Once on each of your turns after making a firearm attack with a Pistol, make one additional Pistol attack as a Bonus Action. Quick Shot consumes ammunition normally, cannot generate Tempo, cannot be used with a Musket, and cannot trigger another Quick Shot.

Deliberate Shot

Cost: 1 Tempo. Once on each of your turns when you hit a hostile creature with a Musket, you may enhance that hit to deal +1d8 weapon damage. Deliberate Shot is a Gunslinger firearm technique, not another attack. It expends no additional ammunition, cannot generate Tempo, and cannot trigger itself. The added die is part of the triggering attack and is doubled on a critical hit.

Evasive Shot

Cost: 1 Tempo. Reaction when a hostile creature you can see makes an attack roll against you. Before the attack resolves, move 5 feet without provoking from that creature. If that movement makes you an illegal target for the triggering attack, the attack misses; otherwise it resolves normally. You must be able to move. Evasive Shot does not trigger against area effects.

Disrupting Shot

Cost: 1 Tempo. When a firearm attack hits, choose one effect for that attack: Hamstring (Speed -10 feet until the start of your next turn), Break Aim (-1 to the creature's next attack roll before your next turn), or Drive Back (push 5 feet if the target is no more than one size larger).

Fast Hands

Once on each of your turns after making a firearm attack or using Evasive Shot, restore 1 shot to one Gunslinger firearm.

Close-Quarters Gunplay

You do not suffer Disadvantage on a Gunslinger firearm attack merely because a hostile creature is adjacent to you. Other sources of Disadvantage still apply.

Running Fire

Once on each of your turns after hitting with a firearm attack, move 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature you hit, provided you can move.

Precision Reload

When you use the normal Bonus Action Reload on an Empty firearm, choose one: Ready Aim (+1 to your next firearm attack before end of next turn), Quick Step (move 5 feet safely from one creature), or Brace (gain PB temporary HP until start of your next turn). This feature is replaced by Tactical Reload at Level 2.

Level 2 — Gunplay Style, Tactical Reload

Gunplay Style

  • Akimbo Style: when wielding two one-handed Gunslinger firearms, handling and switching between them is seamless. Quick Shot may freely use the other firearm.
  • Marksman Style: against targets at least 30 feet away, ignore Half Cover. If you moved no more than 5 feet before the attack, increase that attack's normal range by 20 feet.
  • Skirmisher Style: once on each of your turns after making a firearm attack, move 5 feet safely from one creature affected by that attack. Combined movement from this style and Running Fire is capped at 10 feet from that creature.

Tactical Reload

Tactical Reload replaces Precision Reload. Whenever you use the normal Bonus Action Reload on an Empty firearm, choose one: Tactical Step (move 5 feet safely from one creature), Tactical Aim (+1 to your next firearm attack before end of next turn), or Tactical Guard (gain PB + INT temporary HP until start of your next turn).

Level 3 — Gunslinger Specialization

Choose Pistolero, Deadeye, or Saboteur. Specialization features are gained at Levels 3, 6, 10, and 14.

Pistoleros are the highest-volume Gunslingers, specializing in alternating firearms, sustained aggression, and Quick Shot sequences.

Pistolero — Twin Gun Rhythm

Once per turn, after hitting with one one-handed firearm, your next hit that turn with a different one-handed firearm deals +PB damage.

Pistolero — Rapid Draw

Once on each of your turns when one firearm becomes Empty, immediately switch to another loaded firearm you carry. If that firearm has not been fired this turn, its first attack gains +1 to the attack roll.

Deadeyes specialize in careful distance, precision, and critical pressure rather than firing volume.

Deadeye — Precision Window

Once on each of your turns, if you moved no more than 5 feet before attacking and the target is at least 30 feet away, your first firearm hit that turn deals +1d6 weapon damage.

Deadeye — Steady Sight

Against targets at least 30 feet away, you ignore Half Cover. When you make a firearm attack against such a target after moving no more than 5 feet this turn, increase that weapon’s Maximum Range by 20 feet for that attack, thereby negating the auto-miss feature of the ranged weapon for targets farther than the normal weapon range.

Saboteurs use modified ammunition and technical interference to disrupt hostile movement, accuracy, and reactions.

Saboteur — Modified Ammunition

Once on each of your turns when a firearm attack hits, choose one: Shock Round (target cannot make Opportunity Attacks until start of your next turn), Crippling Round (Speed -10 feet), or Impact Round (push 5 feet if eligible).

Saboteur — Technical Ballistics

Saboteur firearm-system save DC = 8 + PB + INT modifier.

Feat / ASI

Level 5 — Extra Attack, Tempo Mastery, Quick Shot Improvement, Deliberate Shot Improvement, Reload 5

Extra Attack

When you take the Attack Action, make two firearm attacks instead of one. This is the Gunslinger's only normal Extra Attack progression.

Tempo Mastery

Maximum Tempo becomes 4. You may gain up to 2 Tempo on your own turn, but only from two distinct qualifying events. A single attack never generates more than 1 normal Tempo.

Quick Shot Improvement

After using Quick Shot, move 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature targeted by Quick Shot.

Deliberate Shot Improvement

After using Deliberate Shot, you may move 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature damaged by the triggering Musket hit.

Reload 5

Your one-handed Gunslinger firearms now have Reload 5.

Level 6 — Specialization Advancement

Pistolero — Crossfire

Once on each of your turns when you hit the same creature with two different firearms, deal +1d8 weapon damage after the second hit. This is bonus damage, not another attack.

Pistolero — Tempo Barrage

When you use Quick Shot, you may spend 1 additional Tempo to add +1d8 damage on a hit. Tempo Barrage cannot stack with itself.

Deadeye — Critical Focus

Your firearm attacks score a critical hit on 19-20 while the target is at least 30 feet away and you moved no more than 5 feet before the attack. Expanded critical ranges do not stack; use the widest applicable range.

Deadeye — Precision Reposition

After Precision Window triggers, move up to 5 feet without provoking from the damaged creature.

Saboteur — Specialized Payload

When Modified Ammunition triggers, you may spend 1 Tempo to improve it: Shock Round also gives -1 to the target's next attack roll; Crippling Round becomes Speed -20 feet; Impact Round pushes 10 feet.

Saboteur — Disruption Recovery

Once per round when one of your Saboteur effects actually changes an outcome - causing a miss, contributing to a failed save, or preventing a Reaction that otherwise would have occurred - gain 1 Tempo.

Level 7 — Flowing Reload, Evasive Gunplay

Flowing Reload

Once on each of your turns when a firearm becomes Empty, restore 1 shot to it without using an Action or Bonus Action. Fast Hands and Flowing Reload cannot both restore ammunition from the same triggering attack.

Evasive Gunplay

Evasive Shot movement becomes 10 feet. If the triggering attack remains legal after the movement, gain +1 AC against that attack.

Level 8 — Feat / ASI

Feat / ASI

Gain the standard Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 9 — Greater Tempo, Steady Rhythm, Reload 6

Greater Tempo

Maximum Tempo becomes 5. Once on each of your turns when you gain Tempo while already at 3 or more Tempo, choose one: Flow Step (move 5 feet safely from one creature), Steady Hand (+1 damage on your next firearm hit this turn), or Loaded Rhythm (restore 1 shot to one firearm).

Steady Rhythm

Once on each of your turns when both firearm attacks from your Attack Action hit, gain 1 additional Tempo after both attacks resolve. This still respects the normal 2-Tempo own-turn ceiling.

Reload 6

Your one-handed Gunslinger firearms now have Reload 6.

Level 10 — Specialization Advancement

Final Chamber — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, choose the option matching a Pistol or Musket you are wielding. Final Chamber requires no loaded shot, expends no ammunition, and ignores ordinary Reload and firing-cadence limits. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Pistol - Tempest Barrage. Make one Ultimate Pistol attack against a creature within the Pistol's normal range. On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Piercing damage of 100 at Levels 10-14, 120 at Levels 15-19, or 140 at Level 20. Then choose up to two other hostile creatures within 15 feet of the primary target. Each makes a DEX save against DC 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your DEX modifier, taking fixed Piercing damage of 40 at Levels 10-14, 50 at Levels 15-19, or 60 at Level 20 on a failure, or half that amount on a success.

Musket - Final Shot. Make one Ultimate Musket attack against a creature within the Musket's normal range. On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Piercing damage of 140 at Levels 10-14, 160 at Levels 15-19, or 180 at Level 20. Final Shot ignores Half Cover; Full Cover remains absolute. It affects no secondary creatures.

Pistolero — Bullet Dance

Once on each of your turns after making attacks with two different firearms, move up to 10 feet without provoking from one creature hit by either attack.

Pistolero — Relentless Barrage

Once per round, if both firearm attacks from your Attack Action hit and you then use Quick Shot, regain 1 Tempo after Quick Shot resolves. This is a refund, not Tempo generation.

Deadeye — Piercing Shot

Precision Window attacks ignore Half Cover. Full Cover remains absolute.

Deadeye — Lethal Calibration

Once per turn when you score a firearm critical hit, deal additional damage equal to PB after critical damage resolves.

Saboteur — Disabling Round

Once on each of your turns when a firearm attack hits, spend 1 Tempo and force a CON save. On failure, choose one until start of your next turn: Jammed Movement (Speed halved), Broken Aim (-2 to next attack roll), or Suppressed Reaction (cannot take Reactions).

Saboteur — Improved Disruption Recovery

Disruption Recovery can also trigger when a hostile creature fails its save against Disabling Round. It remains once per round.

Level 11 — Rapid Sequence, Greater Quick Shot, Greater Deliberate Shot, Greater Evasive Shot, Greater Disrupting Shot

Rapid Sequence

Once on each of your turns after taking the Attack Action and making at least one firearm attack, choose one: Reload Sequence (reload one firearm without using your Bonus Action), Movement Sequence (move 10 feet safely from one creature you hit this turn), or Technique Sequence (apply Disrupting Shot to one qualifying hit this turn for 0 Tempo). Only one option per turn.

Greater Quick Shot

Quick Shot still costs 1 Tempo. On a hit, it deals additional damage equal to your INT modifier, minimum 1.

Greater Deliberate Shot

Deliberate Shot still costs 1 Tempo. Its triggering Musket hit also deals additional damage equal to your INT modifier, minimum 1.

Greater Evasive Shot

Evasive Shot movement becomes 15 feet. If the triggering attack remains legal, gain +2 AC against that attack.

Greater Disrupting Shot

  • Hamstring: Speed -15 feet.
  • Break Aim: -2 to the next attack roll.
  • Drive Back: push 10 feet if eligible.

Level 12 — Feat / ASI

Feat / ASI

Gain the standard Feat / Ability Score Improvement.

Level 13 — Sustained Tempo, Reactive Reload, Tempo Conversion

Sustained Tempo

Maximum Tempo becomes 6. You now lose 1 Tempo at end of turn only if you did not make a Gunslinger firearm attack, use a Gunslinger firearm technique, or use the normal Reload action on a firearm that was Empty.

Reactive Reload

Once per round when one of your firearms becomes Empty outside your turn, restore 1 shot to it. This is ammunition restoration, not a Reload action. A Musket can benefit from this restoration but can never exceed its fixed 2-shot maximum.

Tempo Conversion

Once per round when you would genuinely gain Tempo while already at maximum Tempo, choose one: Loaded Tempo (restore 1 shot), Moving Tempo (move 5 feet safely), or Guarded Tempo (gain PB temporary HP until start of your next turn). It does not trigger if no Tempo would actually have been gained.

Level 14 — Final Specialization Advancement

Pistolero — Grand Barrage

Once on each of your turns, when the same hostile creature is hit by both Attack Action attacks and Quick Shot, deal +1d8 weapon damage to that creature after the third hit.

Pistolero — Perfect Akimbo

When wielding two loaded one-handed firearms, the first time each turn you switch away from one firearm, restore 1 shot to that firearm. Only one firearm can gain this restoration per turn.

Deadeye — Execution Window

Once on each of your turns when Precision Window damages a creature at or below half-maximum HP, its bonus damage becomes 2d6 instead of 1d6.

Deadeye — Perfect Sight

Your Steady Sight reaches its peak. When you make a firearm attack against a target at least 30 feet away after moving no more than 5 feet this turn, increase that firearm’s Maximum Range by 40 feet instead of 20 feet. In addition, while Steady Sight applies, treat the firearm’s Minimum Range as 30 feet if it would normally be higher. Full Cover still blocks the attack normally.

Saboteur — Grand Sabotage

Once on each of your turns when a hostile creature fails against Disabling Round, choose one additional effect: Overloaded (no Opportunity Attacks), Destabilized (-1 AC / -2 AC if hit by a Musket), or Staggered (move 5 feet horizontally if eligible) until start of your next turn.

Saboteur — Feeding Disruption

Once per round when a hostile creature under one of your Saboteur effects misses, fails a save, or loses a Reaction opportunity because of your effect, choose to gain 1 Tempo or restore 1 shot to one firearm. Feeding Disruption cannot trigger from the same event that already triggered Disruption Recovery.

Level 15 — Perfect Gunplay, Momentum Reload, Reload 7

Perfect Gunplay

Once on each of your turns after making a firearm attack, choose one: Perfect Reload (restore 1 shot), Perfect Step (move 5 feet safely from one creature hit this turn), or Perfect Aim (+1 to your next firearm attack roll this turn).

Momentum Reload

Once per turn, if a firearm was Empty at the start of your turn and you use the normal Bonus Action Reload on it, regain 1 Tempo if you currently have at least 1 Tempo.

Reload 7

Your one-handed Gunslinger firearms now have Reload 7.

Ascendant Final Chamber

Your Final Chamber now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 17 — Apex Tempo, Apex Quick Shot, Apex Deliberate Shot, Apex Evasive Shot, Apex Disrupting Shot, Perfect Sequence

Apex Tempo

Maximum Tempo becomes 7. Your own-turn Tempo ceiling becomes 3, but the third Tempo must come from an explicitly named advanced feature. Normal Clean Hit and Tactical Shot generation still cannot produce more than 2 Tempo by themselves.

Apex Quick Shot

Quick Shot still costs 1 Tempo. On a hit, it deals +1d8 damage. This replaces Greater Quick Shot's INT bonus.

Apex Deliberate Shot

Deliberate Shot still costs 1 Tempo. Its bonus becomes +2d8 weapon damage. This replaces Greater Deliberate Shot's INT bonus. These dice remain part of the triggering Musket attack for critical-hit purposes.

Apex Evasive Shot

Evasive Shot movement becomes 20 feet. If the triggering attack remains legal, gain +2 AC against it. After the attack resolves, restore 1 shot to one firearm.

Apex Disrupting Shot

  • Crippling Shot: Speed -20 feet.
  • Shattered Aim: -2 to the next attack roll, and that attack cannot gain Advantage from flanking, elevation, or positional effects.
  • Driving Shot: push 15 feet if eligible.

Perfect Sequence

Once on each of your turns after you make at least one firearm attack, use one firearm technique, and perform one Reload or ammunition-restoration effect, choose one: Recover Tempo (+1 Tempo), Combat Step (move 10 feet safely), or Ready Chamber (restore 1 shot). Perfect Sequence cannot trigger itself and can grant at most 1 Tempo per turn.

Level 18 — Perfect Firearm Mastery, Relentless Rhythm, Master Gunplay Style

Perfect Firearm Mastery

Once per round, the first time a firearm becomes Empty, immediately restore 1 shot. This is ammunition restoration, not Reload. It can restore a Musket shot normally, subject to the Musket's fixed 2-shot maximum.

Relentless Rhythm

Once per round, after spending 2 or more Tempo during the same turn, if at least one of those techniques successfully affects a hostile creature, regain 1 Tempo. Evasive Shot qualifies only if it meaningfully changes the attack, such as causing it to miss.

Master Gunplay Style

  • Akimbo Mastery: once per turn, the first hit with a firearm different from the one used for your previous firearm attack deals +INT modifier damage.
  • Marksman Mastery: at 40+ feet, your first firearm hit each turn deals +PB damage if you moved no more than 5 feet before the attack.
  • Skirmisher Mastery: once on each of your turns after hitting with a firearm, move 10 feet safely from that creature. This replaces the style's earlier 5-foot movement.

Level 19 — Empyrean Boon

Empyrean Boon

Gain the universal Empyrean Boon.

Level 20 — Gunslinger Ascendant

Perfect Tempo

When you roll Initiative for genuine combat, you gain 3 Tempo. Artificial Initiative cannot trigger this.

Endless Magazine

The first time each round one firearm becomes Empty, restore 2 shots instead of Perfect Firearm Mastery's 1 shot.

Masterful Rhythm

Once on each of your turns after three distinct events occur - a firearm hit, Tempo spent on a technique, and a separate Reload or ammunition-restoration event - choose one: regain 1 Tempo, move 10 feet safely from one creature hit this turn, restore 2 shots to one firearm, or gain +2 to your next firearm attack roll before end of turn. Tempo recovered this way cannot trigger Tempo-gain riders.

Bullet Time

Once per Long Rest as a Bonus Action, enter Bullet Time for 1 minute. It ends early if you become Incapacitated. Once-per-round benefits refresh at the start of your turn.

  • Accelerated Tempo: the first time you generate Tempo on your own turn each round, gain 1 additional Tempo, without exceeding your maximum. A single event still cannot create more than 2 Tempo.
  • Tempo Technique Refund: the first Quick Shot or Deliberate Shot you successfully resolve each round refunds 1 Tempo after it resolves. Quick Shot must hit to qualify; Deliberate Shot is declared only on a hit.
  • Endless Motion: after each firearm attack, move 5 feet safely from the creature targeted by that attack, to a maximum of 15 feet per turn from this feature.
  • Perfect Reload: the first firearm you Reload each round reloads without requiring a Bonus Action.
  • Deadly Rhythm: once per round when you hit the same hostile creature with two firearm attacks during the same turn, deal +1d8 weapon damage after the second hit.
  • Climactic Shot: once per round after making at least two firearm attacks and spending Tempo during the turn, choose one creature you hit. Choose one: Piercing Finale (+2d8 weapon damage), Breaking Finale (-2 to its next attack roll before your next turn), Driving Finale (push 15 feet if eligible), or Vanishing Finale (move yourself 15 feet safely from that creature).

Deadly Rhythm and Climactic Shot may both occur in the same round. Neither is another attack.

Flight, Critical Hits, and Range

  • Gunslinger firearms are physical ranged weapons and use the universal altitude-based physical ranged range increase.
  • Apply class-feature firearm range modifications first, then apply the universal altitude increase.
  • Running Fire, Evasive Shot, Bullet Time movement, and similar effects grant ordinary movement only and do not bypass universal altitude-band change rules.
  • Close-Quarters Gunplay functions while airborne.
  • Firearm critical hits use the universal Wings of Atreia critical rule: double only rolled damage dice belonging to the attack; flat modifiers are not doubled.
  • Crossfire, Grand Barrage, Lethal Calibration, Deadly Rhythm, Climactic Shot, and similar post-hit riders are not automatically doubled on a critical hit unless explicitly stated.

Final Class Identity

The Gunslinger is strongest when the fight never interrupts: shoot, move, reload, spend Tempo, and keep the sequence alive.

Pistolero is the highest-volume Gunslinger and leads sustained direct firearm pressure. Deadeye sacrifices mobility for distance, critical pressure, and precision. Saboteur gives up direct damage for soft control and enemy-tempo disruption.

The class never gains a second Attack Action. A Pistol Gunslinger's normal high-level attack ceiling remains two Attack Action attacks plus one Quick Shot. A Musket Gunslinger instead makes the two Attack Action attacks and concentrates Tempo into Deliberate Shot rather than gaining a third attack. Class power comes from Tempo economy, reload sequencing, mobility, weapon identity, and specialization riders rather than unconditional extra attacks.

Apex Final Chamber

Your Final Chamber uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.
