Core Mechanics & Resources

Momentum

Generation: Your first melee hit on your own turn grants 1 Momentum. Once on each of your turns, Aggressive Momentum grants 1 additional Momentum when one of the three rules are met:

  • A melee hit follows at least 15 ft of movement toward that target.
  • You scored a critical hit
  • You reduced a hostile creature to 0 HP.

Expenditure: Gained Momentum is used to spend specified Chains Links and Finishers.

Recovery: Later features may refund or grant Momentum after a Link or Finisher resolves or at a stated timing. A refund is not a new Momentum-generation event unless the feature explicitly says otherwise.

Momentum and Chains and Chain Links

Momentum represents the Gladiator forcing the battle forward. It exists only in genuine combat and resets to 0 when combat ends. Normal Momentum does not decay simply because you failed to spend it; later features care about maintaining an active Chain instead.

  • A single attack cannot normally grant more than the Momentum allowed by the generation rules above.
  • A Chain begins when you declare a successful melee hit on your own turn to be an Opener. You can maintain only one Chain at a time. The Chain belongs to you, not to a particulary enemy, so later Links may strike another legal target. A miss does not automatically end the Chain. It lasts until the end of your next turn and you were not able to hit any hostile target, until you use a Finisher, abandon it, or become unable to use it.
  • Chain Links and Finishers normally apply only to attacks made on your own turn.
  • A Finisher ends the Chain after it resolves.
  • Chain Dice added directly to the triggering weapon attack double on a critical hit. Independent secondary damage does not.
  • Chain Links occur when there is at least 1 link after the declared Opener is applied. This further strengthens the Finisher. The more links there are, the stronger the Finisher move is. Waiting for the right moment before cashing the links out with a Finisher rewards the Gladiator with additional damage modifiers after the Finisher move as shown in the Class Progression table under Damage per Chain Link column. Chain Links and damage modifiers of the Chain Links do not double on a critical hit.

Universal Gladiator Chain Techniques

Driving Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Requires a melee hit that can carry a Link. Add 1 Chain Die to the attack. If the target is no more than one size larger, push it 5 ft. Does not double on critical hit.

Sweeping Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Requires a melee hit and can only be done once per turn. You can choose another hostile within your reach and within 5 ft of the original target. It takes 1 Chain Die + STR modifier damage. Does not double on critical hit.

Crushing Finisher. Costs 2 Momentum. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 2 Chain Die to the triggering attack. The Chain ends after resolution. This doubles on a critical hit.

Hamstring Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Level 5 ability. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 1 Chain Die and reduce the target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn. Does not double on a critical hit.

Staggering Strike. Costs 2 Momentum. Level 11 ability. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 1 Chain Die. The target cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn.

Devastating Finisher. Costs 3 Momentum. Level 11 ability. Requires at least 2 prior Links. Add 3 Chain Die. The target makes a STR save against your Class Save DC. On a failure, the target falls prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage on the Save. The Chain ends. This doubles on a critical hit.

Titanic Finisher. Costs 4 Momentum. Level 17 ability. Requires at least 3 prior Links. Add 4 Chain Die. The target makes a STR save against your Class Save DC. On a failure, you can choose the target to become Prone and push 20 ft, Pron only, or push 30 ft and not prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage. The Chain ends. This doubles on a critical hit.
