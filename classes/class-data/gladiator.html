“Nothing can stand in my way!”

The premier sustained melee damage class, built around deliberate attack sequences, Momentum, Chain Links, and Finishers.

The Gladiator is the premier sustained melee damage class. It wins through sequences rather than single isolated attacks: open a Chain, spend Momentum on Links that alter the battlefield, then cash the sequence out with a Finisher. Polearms provide the highest sustained weapon output, while weapon discipline and specialization determine whether the Gladiator emphasizes execution, cleaving pressure, or relentless pursuit.

Class Identity

Gladiators are Daevas who turn martial mastery into relentless battlefield pressure. They are not defined by a single weapon or one decisive blow, but by the ability to seize momentum, force an opening, and keep an enemy under pressure until the fight breaks in their favor. In Wings of Atreia, this identity is expressed through Momentum and Chain Attacks: the Gladiator becomes more dangerous when attacks, movement, and finishers are linked into a deliberate sequence.

Tradition & Outlook

The Gladiator tradition descends from the broad warrior disciplines of Atreia and is associated with soldiers, arena fighters, legion veterans, weapon masters, and Daevas who believe that skill must survive contact with a real battlefield. Gladiators commonly value courage, physical discipline, adaptability, and decisive action. Some fight for glory or personal mastery; others see themselves as the hard edge of a legion, the Daeva who advances when everyone else needs the line to move.

Appearance & Adventuring

Gladiators usually present themselves as practical combatants: heavy armor, battle-worn weapons, and equipment chosen for reach, leverage, or sustained close combat. Polearms and other approved Gladiator weapons may suggest very different fighting schools, but the class is recognized by an aggressive, forward-driving presence. A Gladiator adventures to test mastery, protect comrades through offensive pressure, earn renown, serve a military cause, or become strong enough to face the threats of the Abyss and Balaurea directly.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Momentum

Generation: Your first melee hit on your own turn grants 1 Momentum. Once on each of your turns, Aggressive Momentum grants 1 additional Momentum when one of the three rules are met:

  • A melee hit follows at least 15 ft of movement toward that target.
  • You scored a critical hit
  • You reduced a hostile creature to 0 HP.

Expenditure: Gained Momentum is used to spend specified Chains Links and Finishers.

Recovery: Later features may refund or grant Momentum after a Link or Finisher resolves or at a stated timing. A refund is not a new Momentum-generation event unless the feature explicitly says otherwise.

Momentum and Chains and Chain Links

Momentum represents the Gladiator forcing the battle forward. It exists only in genuine combat and resets to 0 when combat ends. Normal Momentum does not decay simply because you failed to spend it; later features care about maintaining an active Chain instead.

  • A single attack cannot normally grant more than the Momentum allowed by the generation rules above.
  • A Chain begins when you declare a successful melee hit on your own turn to be an Opener. You can maintain only one Chain at a time. The Chain belongs to you, not to a particulary enemy, so later Links may strike another legal target. A miss does not automatically end the Chain. It lasts until the end of your next turn and you were not able to hit any hostile target, until you use a Finisher, abandon it, or become unable to use it.
  • Chain Links and Finishers normally apply only to attacks made on your own turn.
  • A Finisher ends the Chain after it resolves.
  • Chain Dice added directly to the triggering weapon attack double on a critical hit. Independent secondary damage does not.
  • Chain Links occur when there is at least 1 link after the declared Opener is applied. This further strengthens the Finisher. The more links there are, the stronger the Finisher move is. Waiting for the right moment before cashing the links out with a Finisher rewards the Gladiator with additional damage modifiers after the Finisher move as shown in the Class Progression table under Damage per Chain Link column. Chain Links and damage modifiers of the Chain Links do not double on a critical hit.

Universal Gladiator Chain Techniques

Driving Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Requires a melee hit that can carry a Link. Add 1 Chain Die to the attack. If the target is no more than one size larger, push it 5 ft. Does not double on critical hit.

Sweeping Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Requires a melee hit and can only be done once per turn. You can choose another hostile within your reach and within 5 ft of the original target. It takes 1 Chain Die + STR modifier damage. Does not double on critical hit.

Crushing Finisher. Costs 2 Momentum. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 2 Chain Die to the triggering attack. The Chain ends after resolution. This doubles on a critical hit.

Hamstring Strike. Costs 1 Momentum. Level 5 ability. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 1 Chain Die and reduce the target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn. Does not double on a critical hit.

Staggering Strike. Costs 2 Momentum. Level 11 ability. Requires at least 1 prior Link. Add 1 Chain Die. The target cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn.

Devastating Finisher. Costs 3 Momentum. Level 11 ability. Requires at least 2 prior Links. Add 3 Chain Die. The target makes a STR save against your Class Save DC. On a failure, the target falls prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage on the Save. The Chain ends. This doubles on a critical hit.

Titanic Finisher. Costs 4 Momentum. Level 17 ability. Requires at least 3 prior Links. Add 4 Chain Die. The target makes a STR save against your Class Save DC. On a failure, you can choose the target to become Prone and push 20 ft, Pron only, or push 30 ft and not prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage. The Chain ends. This doubles on a critical hit.

Ultimate Ability

HeavenbreakerUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, move up to your Speed toward a creature you can see without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You must end within your melee reach, then make one Ultimate melee weapon attack against it. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Damage: On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed additional damage of the ability.

Shockwave. On a hit, every other hostile creature within 10 feet of the primary target makes a DEX save against your Gladiator Class Save DC. If the attack misses, every other hostile creature within the specified distance will not receive any damage. In the even that the attack hits, the hostile creatures roll a save. A creature takes fixed Force damage on a failure, or half that amount on a success.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Roll. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. When the Ultimate requires an attack roll, roll 1d20 + the applicable ability modifier + the character's Proficiency Bonus, with Advantage. Advantage and Disadvantage interact normally, and other explicit modifiers apply normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output, Miss Choice, and Critical Hits. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally. On a hit, resolve normal weapon damage when applicable, all fixed Ultimate damage, and the Ultimate's full legal riders, then expend all 100 DP. On a miss, normal weapon damage and full-hit riders do not occur. After a miss is confirmed, the player chooses one resolution:

  • Release the Power: expend all 100 DP and resolve one-half, rounded down, of the Ultimate's fixed primary damage that would have occured on a hit. Area or secondary target damage, forced movement, condition or status effects, and other riders do not occur.
  • Hold the Power: resolve no Ultimate damage or other effect, and retain all 100 DP, but that Ultimate cannot be attempted again until the next in-game dawn. Short Rests, Long Rests, and Aetheric Reconstution do not end this restriction early. A critical hit does not increase fixed Ultimate output; only rolled normal weapon damage dice double under the normal critical-hit rule. Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability, and damage reduction apply normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

DESTROYER

The Destroyer specializes in devastating single-target attacks, turning carefully built Chains into increasingly lethal finishing blows. Its abilities reward targeting weakened enemies, making it ideal for players who enjoy setting up powerful executions and delivering decisive damage.

TEMPEST

The Tempest expands the Gladiator's Chain Attacks into powerful multi-target assaults. It excels at pressuring groups of nearby enemies, striking multiple opponents with increasingly destructive sweeping attacks and controlling the flow of melee combat.

WARPATH

The Warpath is a relentless frontline pursuer that thrives on movement and aggressive engagement. It improves the Gladiator's ability to charge, break through enemy positions, and maintain pressure while moving from one opponent to another.

Initial Choice begins at Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replaceit with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Momentum, Chain Techniques, Aggressive Momentum, Crushing Finisher, Driving Strike, Sweeping Strike

    Momentum

    Your maximum Momentum is 3. Your first melee hit on your turn grants 1 Momentum.

    Chain Techniques

    You gain the Chain rules and the Chain techniques Driving Strike, Sweeping Strike, and Crushing Finisher. At this level your Chain Die is d6 and a Chain may contain one or more Links before the Finisher, subject to available attacks and Momentum. Each Chain Link would get a +1 to your damage roll.

    Aggressive Momentum

    Moving at least 15 ft toward a creature regardless whether ground or aerial — on the same altitude or not — before hitting it with a melee weapon attack will grant you 1 additional Momentum. This is done only once on your turn. The movement must be actual movement through legal spaces; forced movement does not count.

  2. Level 2: Combat Discipline

    Choose one discipline. You may retrain this choice when you gain a class Feat/ASI

    • Heavy Weapon lets you reroll one weapon damage die showing 1 or 2 once per attack if you are equipped with either a Polearm or a Longsword. This does not reroll a Chain Die.
    • Polearm Discipline increases the reach of your polearm melee attacks by 5 feet during your own turn.
    • Twin Blade adds your STR modifier to the damage of your additional weapon attack; two Shortswords are explicitly a valid Twin Blade setup. Valid equipments are: Dagger, Shortsword, Longsword or Spear.
    • Defensive Weapon Discipline grants +1 AC while wielding a melee weapon and not using a shield.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    DESTROYER — Relentless Finisher

    Once on each of your turns, when Crushing Finisher hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, regain 1 Momentum after the Finisher resolves.

    TEMPEST — Widening Sweep

    When you use Sweeping Strike, choose one additional eligible secondary creature bringing to a total of 2 targets of the Sweeping Strike. That second creature takes one Chain Die without adding STR.

    WARPATH — Charging Assault

    Aggressive Momentum requires only 10 feet of movement toward the target. After the qualifying hit resolves, you may move up to 10 feet more without provoking an Opportunity Attack from that creature; you must end closer to another hostile creature you can perceive. Maximum movement speed still applies.

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  5. Level 5: Extra Attack, Improved Momentum, Hamstring Strike

    Extra Attack

    When you take the Attack action on your turn, make two attacks instead of one. This is the Gladiator's standard attack ceiling: the class does not later gain a third ordinary Attack-action attack. This extra attack does not grant an additional Momentum if the first attack hits. If Gladiator receives Advantage to the attack roll, these two attacks will get the benefit.

    Improved Momentum and Chain Die

    Maximum Momentum increases to 4 and your Chain Die becomes d8. You can now more reliably create an Opener with the first attack and advance the Chain with the second.

    Hamstring Strike

    You unlock Hamstring Strike for 1 Momentum. On a qualifying melee hit, add 1d8 Chain damage and reduce Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. The Speed reduction follows the class non-stacking rule.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Continue your current Specialization or reassign under the universal rule, then gain the chosen path’s Level 6 advancement.

    DESTROYER — Executioner's Weight

    When Crushing Finisher hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, add 1 additional current Chain Die to the attack.

    TEMPEST — Storm of Steel

    Every secondary creature damaged by Sweeping Strike now takes one current Chain Die + your STR modifier, including the additional creature from Widening Sweep.

    WARPATH — Relentless Charge

    When moving toward a creature for Aggressive Momentum, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain. Opportunity Attacks made against you during that qualifying approach have Disadvantage.

  7. Level 7: Relentless Advance

    When a hostile creature within your melee reach willingly moves away from you, use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed. You must end closer to that creature than where you started and cannot end farther from it. This movement does not itself provoke an Opportunity Attack from the triggering creature and grants no attack.

    Each Chain Link would get a +2 to your damage roll.

  8. Level 8: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  9. Level 9: Momentum Mastery

    Greater Momentum

    Your maximum Momentum increases to 5. Once on each of your turns when you spend Momentum on a Chain Link that advances an already active Chain, regain 1 Momentum after that Link resolves. The refund cannot exceed your maximum and cannot recursively trigger another Gladiator rider.

  10. Level 10: Specialization Decision / Advancement, Heavenbreaker

    Continue your current Specialization or reassign under the universal rule, then gain the chosen path’s Level 10 advancement.

    DESTROYER — Execution Window

    When a Finisher attack hits a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, that Finisher scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

    TEMPEST — Cyclone Sweep

    Sweeping Strike secondary targets no longer need to be within 5 feet of the original target; each only needs to be within your melee reach.

    WARPATH — Overrun

    When you make at least 20 ft movement toward your target and make an attack using Aggressive Momentum, the target makes a STR save. On a failure, choose to push it 10 feet or knock it Prone. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage on the save.

    Heavenbreaker

    Your Gladiator makes an ultimate move. The Primary Damage is a fixed amount of 100 on a hit. If you choose to Release the Power, damage is only 50. Secondary damage is fixed Force damage of 40 if every other hostile creature within 10 ft of the primary target fails a DEX save against your Gladiator Class Save DC. Success only suffers 20. There is no Secondary damage if the attack roll is a miss.

  11. Level 11: Greater Chains, Staggering Strike, Devastating Finisher

    Greater Chain Die

    Your Chain Die becomes d10.

    Staggering Strike

    Spend 1 Momentum on a qualifying Link to add 1d10 and prevent the target from taking Reactions until the start of your next turn.

    Devastating Finisher

    After at least two prior successful Links in the current Chain, spend 3 Momentum on a melee hit to add 3d10. The target makes a STR save; on a failure it falls Prone. A creature more than one size larger has Advantage. The Chain then ends.

  12. Level 12: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  13. Level 13: Unbroken Momentum

    Greater Momentum

    Your Maximum Momentum increases to 6.

    Unbroken Momentum

    A successful Chain Link refreshes the duration of your active Chain. In addition, if you begin your turn with an active Chain and 0 Momentum, gain 1 Momentum. This start-turn recovery can occur only once per turn and cannot exceed your maximum.

  14. Level 14: Final Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Continue your current Specialization or reassign under the universal rule, then gain the chosen path’s Level 14 advancement. This is the final normal Specialization decision.

    DESTROYER — Perfect Execution

    Executioner’s Weight is replaced by Perfect Execution. Any Finisher that hits a creature at half maximum HP or fewer adds 1 current Chain Die. If that Finisher reduces the target to 0 HP, regain 2 Momentum instead of the 1 Momentum refund from Relentless Finisher, once per turn.

    TEMPEST — Maelstrom Sweep

    Sweeping Strike may now damage up to three eligible secondary creatures, each for one current Chain Die + STR. If at least two secondary creatures actually take damage, regain 1 Momentum after the Sweep resolves, once per turn.

    WARPATH — Breakthrough

    After a creature fails the save against Overrun, you may move up to 10 feet. You may pass through the space of the affected creature if it is no more than one size larger, but cannot end there. This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks from that creature and should end closer to another hostile creature when one is reasonably reachable.

  15. Level 15: Endless Assault

    Endless Assault

    Once on each of your turns after a Finisher hits and fully resolves, you may spend 1 Momentum to make one melee weapon attack as a Bonus Action. This attack cannot carry a Chain Link or Finisher, but it may be declared the Opener of a new Chain after the previous Chain has ended.

    Each Chain Link would get a +3 to your damage roll. This is the highest ceiling for extra damage modifiers for your Chain links.

    Ascendant Heavenbreaker

    Your Heavenbreaker now deals 120 fixed damage on a successful attack hit to the Primary target. If you choose to Release the Power, damage is only 60. Radius of Secondary damage is now at 15 ft of the primary target with a fixed Force damage of 50 if hostile creatures within this area fail a DEX save against your Gladiator Class Save DC. Success only suffers 20. There is no Secondary damage if the attack roll is a miss.

  16. Level 16: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline. This is the last level you can change your Combat Discipline.

  17. Level 17: Titanic Finisher, Perfect Chain Sequence

    Apex Momentum and Chain Die

    Maximum Momentum increases to 7 and your Chain Die becomes d12. Your active Chain may contain up to four successful Links before you use a Finisher.

    Each Chain Link would get a +3 to your damage roll. This is the highest ceiling for extra damage modifiers for your Chain links.

    Perfect Chain Sequence

    After you successfully resolve three different Chain Links within the same Chain, reduce the Momentum cost of the next Finisher in that Chain by 1, minimum 1. The reduction is consumed when that Finisher is used or the Chain ends.

    Titanic Finisher

    After at least three prior Links, spend 4 Momentum on a melee hit to add 4d12. The target makes a STR save. On a failure choose: knock it Prone and push it 20 feet; knock it Prone without pushing; or push it 30 feet. Creatures more than one size larger have Advantage. The Chain ends after resolution.

  18. Level 18: Master of the Frontline, Second Reaction

    Master of the Frontline

    You have Advantage on checks and saving throws made to resist being knocked Prone, shoved, or forcibly moved.

    Second Reaction

    You gain one additional Reaction each round. This extra Reaction can be used only for an Opportunity Attack or Relentless Advance. You cannot spend both Reactions on the same triggering event, and both refresh at the start of your turn.

  19. Level 19: Empyrean Boon

    Gain the universal Empyrean Boon available at Level 19.

  20. Level 20: Perfect Gladiator

    Battle Ready Momentum

    When you roll Initiative for genuine combat, you gain 3 Momentum.

    Perfect Finisher Flow

    Once on each of your turns after a Finisher successfully hits and resolves, regain 2 Momentum. This is a refund, not Momentum generation, and cannot recursively trigger itself.

    Perfect Endless Assault

    Endless Assault no longer costs Momentum. It still grants only one Bonus Action melee attack, still cannot carry a Link or Finisher, and still does not grant another Attack action.

    Your Flight Slots increases to 8
