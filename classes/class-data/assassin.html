“You'll know I'm there when you're dead.”

The Assassin is a precision melee striker that builds target-bound Rune Marks, converts them into burst damage or execution riders, and uses Shadow Step to remain difficult to pin down. Unlike a conventional Extra Attack martial, its Level 5 Assassination Sequence is a conditional follow-up attack tied to a successful Sneak Attack.

Class Identity

p>Assassins are precision predators who win through positioning, timing, concealment, and carefully prepared execution. Their art is not simply “attacking from stealth.” An Assassin studies a target, creates the right opening, carves Rune Marks, and converts preparation into a sudden lethal advantage. Their greatest strength is the ability to decide where and when a close-range confrontation becomes dangerous.

Tradition & Outlook

The Assassin tradition descends from Atreia’s scout disciplines and is practiced by infiltrators, covert operatives, hunters of dangerous individuals, intelligence agents, and Daevas who favor certainty over spectacle. Assassins often prize patience, awareness, self-control, and economy of motion. Their motives vary widely: service to a faction, revenge, professional duty, protection through preemptive action, or the pursuit of perfect technique.

Appearance & Adventuring

Assassins favor equipment that permits mobility and unobstructed movement, with Shortswords and Daggers forming the class’s approved weapon identity in Wings of Atreia. Their appearance may be subdued or ceremonial, but practical Assassins avoid anything that compromises movement or control. They adventure when a threat must be found before it strikes, a route must be infiltrated, an enemy leader must be neutralized, or a mystery demands someone comfortable operating unseen.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Rune Marks

Rune Mark Mechanics: While every Assassin tracks their own individual limit, a single creature can never hold more than the specified number of Rune Marks in total, even if multiple Assassins are applying them. Additionally, you can trigger your own specific Rune Burst using the marks left by another Assassin to finish off the target.

Rune Mark limit: A target creature can only carry up to 3 of your Rune Marks.

Marks disappear when consumed, when the marked creature is defeated, or when the encounter ends.

Sneak Attack and Rune Mark Carving and Bursting

Sneak Attack: A Sneak Attack applies once on each of your own turns. Sneak Attack damage scales per level. To trigger a Sneak Attack, you must satisfy one of the following two conditions on your attack:

  • You have advantage on the attack roll. Any effect, spell, or abilities that granted you advantage on your attack roll qualifies. Attacking while Invisible counts as granting Advantage to the attack roll.
  • An ally or another enemy of the target is within 5 ft of the target of your attack, and that creature is not incapacitated. The other creature does not need to be within your melee reach.

Rune Carving: Once on your turn, a qualifying melee hit that actually deals Sneak Attack places 1 Rune Mark after that hit resolves. In the event that you are able to attack and hit twice, only place the Rune Mark after the second hit resolves.

Rune Mark Capacity: Whenever a feature would place Rune Marks on a creature that already has 3 Rune Marks, place only enough Marks to reach 3; any excess Marks are lost. This limit applies regardless of how the Marks are generated, including Rune Carving, Masterful Carving, Perfect Marking, Relentless Execution, Perfect Preparation, Perfect Transition, and any Specialization feature.

Newly created Rune Marks cannot be consumed by the same hit that created them. You will have to wait for the next turn to Burst the Rune Mark.

If you exceed your current marked-target limit, choose one previously marked creature and remove all of your Marks from it. If you did not hit that creature to remove the Rune Marks, it does not deal any Rune Burst damage.

You can also detonate a Rune Mark immediately after landing a hit on a marked target. This does not require a Sneak Attack; instead, you can spend your Bonus Action to expend your chosen Marks and roll the corresponding Rune Dice for extra damage.

Missing, merely targeting, off-turn staged attacks, or hitting a creature without satisfying Rune Carving do not place Marks unless an explicit Assassin feature says otherwise.

Rune Burst and Critical Hits When you hit a creature bearing your Rune Marks, you may use your Bonus Action to consume 1, 2, or 3 Marks to add the same number of Rune Dice to that weapon attack. Rune Dice added directly to the attack double on a critical hit. Secondary status riders of an Execution are not doubled. A Rune Burst resolves only the Marks placed on the previous turns, not on the same turn this ability is executed.

Ultimate Ability

Death's Eclipse Ultimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, choose one creature you can see within 60 ft and teleport to a legal unoccupied space within 5 ft of it. If no such space exists, you cannot choose that creature. Make one Ultimate attack against it with your melee weapon. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Execution Damage: The attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed additional damage of the ability.

Rune Mark Interaction:When Death’s Eclipse hits a creature bearing your Rune Marks, you may choose to consume Marks on that hit using Rune Burst or an applicable Greater or Apex Execution. All normal requirements, Rune Dice, and additional effects of the chosen option apply. You may also choose not to consume any Marks.

Eclipsed Response. The target cannot take Reactions until the end of your next turn. Death's Eclipse has no area-damage or allied-support component; its power is concentrated into the primary target.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

EXECUTIONER

The Executioner focuses on converting accumulated Rune Marks into devastating finishing attacks. It strengthens full-mark executions, improves critical-hit opportunities, and becomes especially dangerous against weakened targets.

PHANTOM

The Phantom specializes in disappearing, repositioning, and striking from advantageous positions. Its improved Shadow Step and concealment abilities allow it to evade retaliation, manipulate its positioning, and create opportunities for repeated precision attacks.

NIGHTSTALKER

The Nightstalker is an expert hunter who identifies a target and relentlessly pursues it. Its abilities improve tracking, expose concealed opponents, and make escaping its pursuit increasingly difficult, making it particularly effective against priority targets.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Sneak Attack, Rune Carving, Rune Burst, Shadow Step

    Sneak Attack

    Once on each of your own turns, add 1d6 damage to your melee weapon hit.

    Rune Carving and Rune Burst

    Your Rune Die is d4 and you may maintain Marks on one creature. After a qualifying Sneak Attack hit, place 1 Rune Mark.

    Rune Burst

    You can activate the Rune Burst effect as a Bonus Action to detonate your active Rune Marks for damage. For every mark you consume in this process, you also add an equal number of d4 Rune Dice to the attack.

    Shadow Step

    Once per turn, after landing a qualifying Sneak Attack, you can move up to 10 feet without triggering Opportunity Attacks from the target you struck. Because this is standard movement rather than teleportation, and it does not grant extra speed, you must have at least 10 feet of movement left on your turn to use it.

  2. Level 2: Predator's Senses, Blinding Feint

    Predator's Senses

    You sense an Invisible creature within 10 feet if it is not behind Full Cover. You know the space it occupies, it cannot become Hidden from you solely because it is Invisible, and you do not suffer Disadvantage on attacks against it solely from that invisibility. When you make a Perception or Investigation check specifically to find Hidden creatures, tracks, ambushes, or deliberate concealment, add your PB if you are not already proficient in the check. This does not stack with proficiency.

    Blinding Feint

    As a Bonus Action, choose one creature within 10 feet that can see you and has not suffered any damage during the current encounter. It must make a Wisdom saving throw against your Assassin Class Save DC. On a failure, it cannot see you until the start of your next turn, and any attack roll it makes against you automatically misses. It can still see and interact with other creatures and objects normally. On a success, it resists the feint and can still see you. You may use this feature repeatedly against the same creature as long as it continues to meet the requirements, even after it succeeds on a save. This does not apply the Blinded condition.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    EXECUTIONER — Deep Carving

    When you consume all 3 Rune Marks through a full three-Mark Rune Burst, add 1 additional current Rune Die to the triggering attack’s damage. At Level 3 this is an extra d4; it scales automatically when your Rune Die improves.

    PHANTOM — Vanishing Step

    Shadow Step becomes 15 feet. Once on each of your turns after using Shadow Step, if you end somewhere you could normally Hide, you may take the Hide action as part of Shadow Step with no additional action. Normal hiding requirements still apply.

    NIGHTSTALKER — Hunter’s Rune

    Marked Quarry: Whenever you place at least one Rune Mark on a creature, it becomes your Marked Quarry while it retains at least one of your Rune Marks. Within 60 feet and not behind Full Cover, you know its direction, whether it is moving, and whether it remains in range. Aether Trace becomes 30 feet against it. If it attempts to Hide, resolve its Stealth against your Passive Perception + its current Rune Marks.

    Hunter’s Pressure: Once on each of your turns, when you hit your Marked Quarry with a melee weapon attack, choose one:

    • Predator’s Strike adds damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus to the triggering attack;
    • Hamstringing Strike reduces its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

      • This applies to ordinary melee attacks and attacks that trigger Sneak Attack. It requires no Rune Mark expenditure or additional action.

    Your Sneak Die becomes a 2d6.

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  5. Level 5: Assassination Sequence, Improved Runes

    Assassination Sequence

    Once on each of your turns, immediately after a melee weapon hit that actually deals Sneak Attack damage, make one additional eligible melee attack. It requires no Action or Bonus Action. This follow-up attack cannot deal Sneak Attack and cannot trigger another Assassination Sequence.

    Improved Runes

    Your Rune Die becomes d6 and Sneak Attack becomes 3d6. Because the Sequence creates a second melee hit, you now have a reliable route to the two-hit Rune Carving condition on the same target. Only one Rune Mark must still be placed on the target creature in this turn.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 6 feature.

    EXECUTIONER — Execution Window

    When a melee weapon attack against a creature bearing 3 Rune Marks consumes all 3 Marks, that execution attack scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

    PHANTOM — Fleeting Shadow

    When Assassination Sequence triggers, you may use Shadow Step before or after the follow-up attack. This does not grant a second Shadow Step. If you successfully Hid with Vanishing Step that turn, Shadow Step movement does not automatically end Hidden unless it places you where you can plainly be seen.

    NIGHTSTALKER — Relentless Quarry

    When your Marked Quarry that you can perceive willingly moves, you may use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed toward it, ending closer to it. The quarry cannot make Opportunity Attacks against you during this movement. If you end within melee reach, you may make one melee weapon attack against it as part of the same Reaction. This additional attack cannot trigger Sneak Attack, Assassination Sequence, or Rune Marks through Rune Carving. It cannot trigger itself and can be used only once per round.

  7. Level 7: Improved Shadow Step, Tactical Disengagement

    Improved Shadow Step

    Base Shadow Step becomes 20 feet instead of 15 ft. Sneak Attack becomes 4d6.

    Tactical Disengagement

    When you use Shadow Step, you do not provoke Opportunity Attacks from any creature whose reach you leave during that Shadow Step movement. This protection applies only to the movement made as part of Shadow Step; other movement can still provoke Opportunity Attacks normally. It does not prevent other Reactions or attacks.

  8. Level 8: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  9. Level 9: Rune Mastery

    Rune Mastery

    Your marked-target limit increases from 1 to 2. Sneak Attack becomes 5d6.

    When the Sneak Attack hit and the Assassination Sequence follow-up both hit the same creature during your turn, place one additional Rune Mark on that creature after the follow-up resolves. Combined with normal Rune Carving, this can place up to 2 Marks from the sequence. Newly placed Marks still cannot be consumed by the hit that created them; subject to maximum Rune Mark limit per creature.

  10. Level 10: Specialization Decision / Advancement, Death's Eclipse

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 10 feature.

    EXECUTIONER — Lingering Ruin

    Once on each of your turns when you consume all 3 Rune Marks from a creature and it remains alive after the attack resolves, place 1 Rune Mark on it after all execution effects resolve.

    PHANTOM — Veiled Passage

    Whenever you successfully Hide using Vanishing Step, become Invisible until the earliest of the start of your next turn, making an attack, forcing a saving throw, or dealing damage. Invisible does not by itself make you Hidden.

    NIGHTSTALKER — True Quarry

    Your Hunter’s Rune directional tracking range becomes 120 feet and Aether Trace against your Marked Quarry becomes 60 feet. Your Marked Quarry cannot gain Advantage on attacks against you solely because it is unseen while within your tracking range. Your directional tracking can persist through Full Cover, but it does not reveal the quarry’s exact occupied space through Full Cover. In addition, once on each of your turns when you hit your Marked Quarry with a melee weapon attack, Hunter’s Pressure gains a third option:

    Suppressing Strike.

    The quarry cannot take Reactions until the start of your next turn. You may still choose only one Hunter’s Pressure option on that turn.

    Death's Eclipse

    Your Assassin makes an ultimate move. The attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed 130 damage of the weapon's type.

  11. Level 11: Greater Rune Execution, Execution Strike

    Greater Executions

    Your Rune Die becomes d8 and Sneak Attack becomes 6d6. When you consume all 3 Marks on a melee hit, you may choose a Greater Execution instead of ordinary Rune Burst:

    • Rupture adds 4 Rune Dice;
    • Crippling adds 3 Rune Dice and forces a CON save, with failure setting Speed to 0 and preventing Reactions until the start of your next turn;
    • Aether Severance adds 2 Rune Dice and forces a CON save, with failure preventing spellcasting until the start of your next turn.

    Save DC is your Assassin Class Save DC. These hard-edged effects are brief and save-dependent.

  12. Level 12: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  13. Level 13: Ghostwalk

    Shadow Step reaches 30 feet. When you use it, you may treat the movement as a short teleport to a visible legal unoccupied space within range instead of normal movement. It cannot cross a barrier that explicitly prevents teleportation or Aetheric movement, and it cannot place you into an unseen or illegal space. Aether Trace improves to 20 feet for non-Nightstalker targets. Sneak Attack becomes 7d6.

  14. Level 14: Final Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 14 feature.

    EXECUTIONER — Death Sentence

    When an execution attack consumes all 3 Rune Marks from a creature that had half its maximum HP or fewer before the attack, the attack’s critical range becomes 18–20, replacing the 19–20 range from Execution Window.

    PHANTOM — Perfect Vanishing

    Once on each of your turns when you consume all 3 Rune Marks with Rune Burst or an Execution, immediately become Invisible until the same ending conditions as Veiled Passage. You do not need to Hide first, but Invisible still does not automatically mean Hidden.

    NIGHTSTALKER — No Escape

    Greater Executions

    When your Marked Quarry, which you can perceive or track, willingly moves or teleports while within 60 feet of you, you may use your Reaction to choose one option.

    • Relentless Pursuit: Move up to half your Speed toward the quarry; it cannot make Opportunity Attacks against you during this movement. If you end within melee reach, you may make one melee weapon attack against it.;
    • Ghost Pursuit Teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the quarry, provided you can perceive or determine its occupied space. You and the quarry must be on the same plane, and the quarry must be within 60 feet when it triggers this feature. If you end within melee reach, you may make one melee weapon attack against it.;

    Either additional attack cannot trigger Sneak Attack, Assassination Sequence, or Rune Marks through Rune Carving; it cannot trigger itself. No Escape can be used only once per round and requires your Reaction. Ghost Pursuit does not consume your normal Shadow Step use and cannot cross an effect or barrier that prevents teleportation.

  15. Level 15: Relentless Execution, Defensive Reflexes

    Relentless Execution

    When a melee attack that consumes at least one Rune Mark reduces its target to 0 HP, choose another hostile creature within 30 ft of you. You can place 1 Mark on the new creature. As a free action, you may use Shadow Step/Ghostwalk movement for 15 ft toward the new target. This must let you end closer to the new target, not away. Sneak Attack becomes 8d6.

    Defensive Reflexes

    When you take damage from an attack roll, you may use your Reaction after the damage is determined but before it is applied to reduce that damage by an amount equal to your total Dexterity score. This applies to melee and ranged attack rolls, including weapon and spell attacks, but not to damage from saving throws or effects that do not use attack rolls. The damage cannot be reduced below 0. You must have your Reaction available to use this feature.

    Death's Eclipse

    Your Death's Eclipse now deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed 150 damage of the weapon's type.
