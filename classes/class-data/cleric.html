“Protect me and you shall never fall.”

The durable restorative and protective frontline tank-support spellcaster.

The Cleric is a durable restorative and protective frontline tank-support spellcaster. It is deliberately more durable than the d6 full casters and is rewarded for meaningful intervention: healing an ally in real danger, actually preventing damage, or removing a significant hostile effect. Divine Grace converts that successful support into further emergency power, allowing the Cleric to hold the line while sustaining and protecting the party.

Class Identity

Clerics are Atreia’s durable frontline restorative and protective spellcasters. They combine personal durability with healing, protection, and purification, preserving the party through recovery, cleansing, and timely intervention while remaining capable of fighting at the front. Divine Grace represents the sacred and restorative reserve through which a Cleric answers moments of genuine need.

Tradition & Outlook

The Cleric tradition grows from Atreia’s priestly disciplines and is associated with healers, battlefield ministers, temple servants, protectors of communities, and Daevas who understand survival as a collective responsibility. Clerics often value service, perseverance, mercy, duty, or faith, but they need not share one personality or theology. A militant Cleric and a gentle healer may practice the same class through very different convictions.

Appearance & Adventuring

Clerics commonly combine durable armor with practical healing or ritual equipment. In Wings of Atreia they may use Maces, Hammers, Staffs, Quarterstaffs, and shields from Round Shields to Medium Shields according to their approved access. They adventure to protect lives, confront corruption or supernatural danger, recover sacred knowledge, serve a cause, or ensure that an expedition can survive places where retreat and ordinary medicine are not enough.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Divine Grace

Generation: Once per round, when you meaningfully support yourself or an ally with a Cleric spell or feature, you regain 1 Grace. Your once-per-round resource refreshes at the start of your turn. This lets you use it to protect yourself during an enemy's turn without accidentally getting to use it twice in the same round.

Maximum: Your Maximum Divine Grace is equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier, minimum of 2.

Recovery: Long Rest restores all Divine Grace. Short Rest only restores equal to your Proficiency Bonus up to maximum.

Meaningful Support and Divine Grace Expenditure

The Cleric, along with the Chanter, are the only two classes that do not need to equip with an implement like the other spellcasters. They, however, need to equip with a weapon in order to do their melee attack. They can still cast their spells without being equipped with any weapon or implements.

Meaningful Support means one of the following actually occurs:

  • restore HP while the creature was below half maximum HP;
  • prevent or reduce damage by at least equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus; or
  • suppress/remove a mechanically significant hostile condition or effect.

Doing these mechanics do not qualify in restoring Divine Grace:

  • Healing a full-HP creature;
  • Granting Temporary HP is not considered healing and cannot restore a creature from 0 HP. Temporary HP follow the Core Rulebook: they do not add together (i.e. when a creature would gain temporary HP while it already has some, it chooses which pool to keep.)
  • Preventing 0 damage;
  • Refreshing temporary HP without increasing it;
  • Passive aura / AC alone;
  • Cosmetic effects, or intentionally manufactured harmless damage

A secondary Cleric rider created by another Cleric feature does not recursively trigger Twin Devotion, Sacred Intervention, Graceful Channeling, Empoowered Restoration, or another support sequence unless explicitly stated by the mechanics.

Flat Cleric save penalties do not stack; use the largest.

Divine Grace is used to activate core Cleric abilities:

Graceful Restoration – Costs 1 Grace and spent when a Cleric spell heals; no separate action. It applies to one creature and one healing instance. It heals additional HP dependent on Cleric's Wisdom Modifier.

  • Note: Graceful Restoration applies to only one creature and one individual healing instance when you spend its 1 Grace; it does not automatically enhance every creature healed by the same spell. A separately resolved healing instance for another creature can be considered separately if you can pay the cost and the feature’s timing permits it.

Protective Grace – Costs 1 Grace. It is done as a Reaction when a visible creature within 30 ft takes damage. It reduces damage taken by that creature or yourself.

Cleansing Grace – Costs 1 Grace and spent when a Cleric spell heals; no separate action. It suppresses conditions that progresses at certain levels with higher status effects cleansed at later levels.

  • Note: Cleansing Grace never normally suppresses death, Unconsciousness, Petrification, permanent transformation, statistic replacement, or no-save complete domination unless a specific later rule explicitly overrides that boundary.

Purifier Absolute Purification – Costs 1 Grace and is a Specialization-specific ability; no separate action.

Divine Presence – Costs 2 Grace as a reaction when an ally failed the save vs. Fear, Charm, Poison, Forced Movement or Speed 0. The ally will reroll and must take the new roll.

Ultimate Ability

Empyrean JudgmentUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn..

Primary Damage: The target takes fixed Radiant damage.

Mass Restoration. Choose yourself and up to a number of allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP at certain levels.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

HEALER

The Healer focuses on restoring the life and vitality of wounded allies through increasingly powerful healing abilities. It improves direct restoration, healing overflow, and emergency recovery, making it the Cleric's dedicated path for keeping companions alive.

PROTECTOR

The Protector turns divine power toward preventing harm rather than simply repairing it afterward. It specializes in damage mitigation, interception, protective enhancements, and maintaining the safety of allies under sustained enemy pressure.

PURIFIER

The Purifier specializes in identifying and removing hostile conditions, debilitating effects, and supernatural afflictions. It is particularly effective against enemies that rely on curses, corruption, and other harmful effects to weaken the party.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Divine Grace, Grace Techniques, Healing Doctrine, Sacred Presence, Divine Resilience

    Spellcasting

    You gain spellcasting and 4 additional spells:

    • Healing Light. Action. At-Will. Healing.
    • Purifying Spark. Action. At-Will. Support / Minor Purification.
    • Radiant Bolt. Action. At-Will. Damage.
    • Sacred Ward. Reaction. At-Will. Protection.

    Graceful Restoration

    Your Graceful Restoration allows you to spend your 1 Grace for additional healing when you cast your healing spells on any creature. One Grace is spent per creature that you wish to heal. Healing is added by 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier.

    Protective Grace

    Your Protective Grace lets you reduce damage received by you or your ally for 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier.

    Cleansing Grace

    Beginning at Level 1, when you heal a creature, you can also suppress Fear, Poison, Speed Reduction, Nonmagical bleed until target next turn. No action required. Range of this is equivalent to the Healing spell you used on the creature.

    Healing Doctrine

    When you are healing a creature that is at or below half HP, you can, at once per turn, add additional HP healed equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus. This is a flat bonus and is not counted as a rolled-healing class rider.

    Sacred Presence

    You have an aura of 5 ft and any allies within the aura will get a +1 modifier on Death Saves and a Temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus once/turn when you heal them within your aura.

    Divine Resilience

    Once per Long Rest you have the ability to defy death that would leave you at 1 HP instead of 0.

  2. Level 2: Purifying Touch, Graceful Channeling

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 6.

    Purifying Touch

    As an action, you can move to within 5 ft of the target and touch to do a Purification Check which is d20 + your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier against the effect DC to suppress Fear, Poison, diseas, nonmagical bleed/wound, or hostile Speed reduction until target next turn. This is equivalent to Cleansing Grace except that this does not cost 1 Divine Grace and must be within melee reach of the Cleric. You can use this a number of times equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

    Graceful Channeling

    Once per turn and after a support spell such as healing, etc, you can choose:

    • add an extra healing equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus;
    • +1 AC until the start of the target's next turn; or
    • one normal repeat save roll against an ongoing effect.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    HEALER — Mercy Overflow

    Once per turn when healing a creature that was at or below half its maximum HP, add 1d6 to one healing instance. If that healing exceeds the creature’s missing HP, convert up to your Proficiency Bonus of the excess into temporary HP lasting until your next turn; this conversion occurs at most once per turn per creature.

    PROTECTOR — Sacred Intercession

    Once per turn when you reduce damage to yourself or an ally through a Cleric spell or feature, that creature gains temporary HP after the damage resolves. This temporary HP is equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus. Once per round, when an ally within 5 feet is hit, use your Reaction to reduce the triggering damage equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier with no Grace cost; this cannot combine with Protective Grace on the same damage instance.

    PURIFIER — Purifying Insight

    Once per turn when your Cleric spell or feature meaningfully removes or suppresses a hostile condition/effect, you may choose one of the following benefits for that creature without needing to spend Grace:

    • Restorative Grace lets the creature regain HP equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1);
    • Protective Grace lets the creature gain temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus until the start of your next turn; or
    • Resolute Grace lets the creature gain a +1 bonus to its next saving throw against a hostile effect before the end of its next turn.

    Purifying Insight lets you identify the nature of a mechanically significant hostile effect you successfully examine or suppress under the class’s purification rules. You will be able to identify the nature (whether effect is a condition, disease, poison, curse, or another hostile effect), origin (whether it is magical, nonmagical, or supernatural and whether the source is identifiable), persistence (whether it is ongoing, requires maintenance or has a continuing duration), and purification (whether this can be removed or suppressed using your current Cleric purification abilities)

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 3, and 3 additional spell:

    • Greater Restoration Light. Action. Cooldown 2. Healing.
    • Guardian Halo. Action. Cooldown 2. Area Protection.
    • Judgment Beam. Action. Cooldown 2. Damage.

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  5. Level 5: Greater Grace, Empowered Restoration, Emergency Grace Refund

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, and 1 additional spell:

    • Cleanse Affliction].Action. Short Rest 1. Purification

    Greater Grace

    Your Grace techniques are improved. Your Graceful Restoration and Protective Grace now use 1d8+WIS instead of the original 1d6+WIS. Your Cleansing Grace now also adds Blinded, Deafened, magical ongoing damage, hostile attack/save penalty.

    Empowered Restoration

    Once per turn, after either healing or preventing damage of at least equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier, you can choose:

    • Renew to add +1d6 to the healing;
    • Fortify to add +2 AC against the next attack; or
    • Steady to have Advantage on the next save.

    Emergency Grace Refund

    Once per round, when you spent 1 Divine Grace that directly prevents an ally from reaching 0 HP, you will get refunded 1 Divine Grace after resolution.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Decision / Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 4, and 1 additional spell:

    • Prayer Resolve. Action. Short Rest 1. Support.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 6 feature.

    HEALER — Deep Restoration

    Once/turn when healing a creature at/below half HP choose:

    • +WIS to that healing;
    • Delayed PB healing at start next turn if alive; or
    • PB healing to a second creature within 15 ft.

    PROTECTOR — Intercepting Grace

    Before you end your current turn, you can choose either self/ally within 10 ft of your Cleric to add +1 AC until the start of your next turn. Non-self target also gains Advantage vs Shove/forced movement while within this distance from your Cleric. Protective Grace may target an ally within 10 ft even without seeing damage source if you perceive ally.

    PURIFIER — Greater Purification

    Successful Purifying Touch can fully end listed nonmagical/ordinary afflictions, grant an immediate Advantage save vs repeat-save magic, or grant +2 next save vs same source. Diagnosis reveals broad category, ordinary-save ending, and whether effect exceeds current cleansing power.
