“Protect me and you shall never fall.”

The durable restorative and protective frontline tank-support spellcaster.

The Cleric is a durable restorative and protective frontline tank-support spellcaster. It is deliberately more durable than the d6 full casters and is rewarded for meaningful intervention: healing an ally in real danger, actually preventing damage, or removing a significant hostile effect. Divine Grace converts that successful support into further emergency power, allowing the Cleric to hold the line while sustaining and protecting the party.

Class Identity

Clerics are Atreia’s durable frontline restorative and protective spellcasters. They combine personal durability with healing, protection, and purification, preserving the party through recovery, cleansing, and timely intervention while remaining capable of fighting at the front. Divine Grace represents the sacred and restorative reserve through which a Cleric answers moments of genuine need.

Tradition & Outlook

The Cleric tradition grows from Atreia’s priestly disciplines and is associated with healers, battlefield ministers, temple servants, protectors of communities, and Daevas who understand survival as a collective responsibility. Clerics often value service, perseverance, mercy, duty, or faith, but they need not share one personality or theology. A militant Cleric and a gentle healer may practice the same class through very different convictions.

Appearance & Adventuring

Clerics commonly combine durable armor with practical healing or ritual equipment. In Wings of Atreia they may use Maces, Hammers, Staffs, Quarterstaffs, and shields from Round Shields to Medium Shields according to their approved access. They adventure to protect lives, confront corruption or supernatural danger, recover sacred knowledge, serve a cause, or ensure that an expedition can survive places where retreat and ordinary medicine are not enough.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Divine Grace

Generation: Once per round, when you meaningfully support yourself or an ally with a Cleric spell or feature, you regain 1 Grace. Your once-per-round resource refreshes at the start of your turn. This lets you use it to protect yourself during an enemy's turn without accidentally getting to use it twice in the same round.

Maximum: Your Maximum Divine Grace is equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier, minimum of 2.

Recovery: Long Rest restores all Divine Grace. Short Rest only restores equal to your Proficiency Bonus up to maximum.

Meaningful Support and Divine Grace Expenditure

The Cleric, along with the Chanter, are the only two classes that do not need to equip with an implement like the other spellcasters. They, however, need to equip with a weapon in order to do their melee attack. They can still cast their spells without being equipped with any weapon or implements.

Meaningful Support means one of the following actually occurs:

  • restore HP while the creature was below half maximum HP;
  • prevent or reduce damage by at least equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus; or
  • suppress/remove a mechanically significant hostile condition or effect.

Doing these mechanics do not qualify in restoring Divine Grace:

  • Healing a full-HP creature;
  • Granting Temporary HP is not considered healing and cannot restore a creature from 0 HP. Temporary HP follow the Core Rulebook: they do not add together (i.e. when a creature would gain temporary HP while it already has some, it chooses which pool to keep.)
  • Preventing 0 damage;
  • Refreshing temporary HP without increasing it;
  • Passive aura / AC alone;
  • Cosmetic effects, or intentionally manufactured harmless damage

A secondary Cleric rider created by another Cleric feature does not recursively trigger Twin Devotion, Sacred Intervention, Graceful Channeling, Empoowered Restoration, or another support sequence unless explicitly stated by the mechanics.

Flat Cleric save penalties do not stack; use the largest.

Divine Grace is used to activate core Cleric abilities:

Graceful Restoration – Costs 1 Grace and spent when a Cleric spell heals; no separate action. It applies to one creature and one healing instance. It heals additional HP dependent on Cleric's Wisdom Modifier.

  • Note: Graceful Restoration applies to only one creature and one individual healing instance when you spend its 1 Grace; it does not automatically enhance every creature healed by the same spell. A separately resolved healing instance for another creature can be considered separately if you can pay the cost and the feature’s timing permits it.

Protective Grace – Costs 1 Grace. It is done as a Reaction when a visible creature within 30 ft takes damage. It reduces damage taken by that creature or yourself.

Cleansing Grace – Costs 1 Grace and spent when a Cleric spell heals; no separate action. It suppresses conditions that progresses at certain levels with higher status effects cleansed at later levels.

  • Note: Cleansing Grace never normally suppresses death, Unconsciousness, Petrification, permanent transformation, statistic replacement, or no-save complete domination unless a specific later rule explicitly overrides that boundary.

Purifier Absolute Purification – Costs 1 Grace and is a Specialization-specific ability; no separate action.

Divine Presence – Costs 2 Grace as a reaction when an ally failed the save vs. Fear, Charm, Poison, Forced Movement or Speed 0. The ally will reroll and must take the new roll.

Ultimate Ability

Empyrean JudgmentUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn..

Primary Damage: The target takes fixed Radiant damage.

Mass Restoration. Choose yourself and up to a number of allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP at certain levels.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

HEALER

The Healer focuses on restoring the life and vitality of wounded allies through increasingly powerful healing abilities. It improves direct restoration, healing overflow, and emergency recovery, making it the Cleric's dedicated path for keeping companions alive.

PROTECTOR

The Protector turns divine power toward preventing harm rather than simply repairing it afterward. It specializes in damage mitigation, interception, protective enhancements, and maintaining the safety of allies under sustained enemy pressure.

PURIFIER

The Purifier specializes in identifying and removing hostile conditions, debilitating effects, and supernatural afflictions. It is particularly effective against enemies that rely on curses, corruption, and other harmful effects to weaken the party.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Divine Grace, Grace Techniques, Healing Doctrine, Sacred Presence, Divine Resilience

    Spellcasting

    You gain spellcasting and 4 additional spells:

    • Healing Light. Action. At-Will. Healing.
    • Purifying Spark. Action. At-Will. Support / Minor Purification.
    • Radiant Bolt. Action. At-Will. Damage.
    • Sacred Ward. Reaction. At-Will. Protection.

    Graceful Restoration

    Your Graceful Restoration allows you to spend your 1 Grace for additional healing when you cast your healing spells on any creature. One Grace is spent per creature that you wish to heal. Healing is added by 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier.

    Protective Grace

    Your Protective Grace lets you reduce damage received by you or your ally for 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier.

    Cleansing Grace

    Beginning at Level 1, when you heal a creature, you can also suppress Fear, Poison, Speed Reduction, Nonmagical bleed until target next turn. No action required. Range of this is equivalent to the Healing spell you used on the creature.

    Healing Doctrine

    When you are healing a creature that is at or below half HP, you can, at once per turn, add additional HP healed equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus. This is a flat bonus and is not counted as a rolled-healing class rider.

    Sacred Presence

    You have an aura of 5 ft and any allies within the aura will get a +1 modifier on Death Saves and a Temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus once/turn when you heal them within your aura.

    Divine Resilience

    Once per Long Rest you have the ability to defy death that would leave you at 1 HP instead of 0.

  2. Level 2: Purifying Touch, Graceful Channeling

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 6.

    Purifying Touch

    As an action, you can move to within 5 ft of the target and touch to do a Purification Check which is d20 + your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier against the effect DC to suppress Fear, Poison, diseas, nonmagical bleed/wound, or hostile Speed reduction until target next turn. This is equivalent to Cleansing Grace except that this does not cost 1 Divine Grace and must be within melee reach of the Cleric. You can use this a number of times equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.

    Graceful Channeling

    Once per turn and after a support spell such as healing, etc, you can choose:

    • add an extra healing equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus;
    • +1 AC until the start of the target's next turn; or
    • one normal repeat save roll against an ongoing effect.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    HEALER — Mercy Overflow

    Once per turn when healing a creature that was at or below half its maximum HP, add 1d6 to one healing instance. If that healing exceeds the creature’s missing HP, convert up to your Proficiency Bonus of the excess into temporary HP lasting until your next turn; this conversion occurs at most once per turn per creature.

    PROTECTOR — Sacred Intercession

    Once per turn when you reduce damage to yourself or an ally through a Cleric spell or feature, that creature gains temporary HP after the damage resolves. This temporary HP is equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus. Once per round, when an ally within 5 feet is hit, use your Reaction to reduce the triggering damage equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier with no Grace cost; this cannot combine with Protective Grace on the same damage instance.

    PURIFIER — Purifying Insight

    Once per turn when your Cleric spell or feature meaningfully removes or suppresses a hostile condition/effect, you may choose one of the following benefits for that creature without needing to spend Grace:

    • Restorative Grace lets the creature regain HP equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1);
    • Protective Grace lets the creature gain temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus until the start of your next turn; or
    • Resolute Grace lets the creature gain a +1 bonus to its next saving throw against a hostile effect before the end of its next turn.

    Purifying Insight lets you identify the nature of a mechanically significant hostile effect you successfully examine or suppress under the class’s purification rules. You will be able to identify the nature (whether effect is a condition, disease, poison, curse, or another hostile effect), origin (whether it is magical, nonmagical, or supernatural and whether the source is identifiable), persistence (whether it is ongoing, requires maintenance or has a continuing duration), and purification (whether this can be removed or suppressed using your current Cleric purification abilities)

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 3, and 3 additional spell:

    • Greater Restoration Light. Action. Cooldown 2. Healing.
    • Guardian Halo. Action. Cooldown 2. Area Protection.
    • Judgment Beam. Action. Cooldown 2. Damage.

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  5. Level 5: Greater Grace, Empowered Restoration, Emergency Grace Refund

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, and 1 additional spell:

    • Cleanse Affliction].Action. Short Rest 1. Purification

    Greater Grace

    Your Grace techniques are improved. Your Graceful Restoration and Protective Grace now use 1d8+WIS instead of the original 1d6+WIS. Your Cleansing Grace now also adds Blinded, Deafened, magical ongoing damage, hostile attack/save penalty.

    Empowered Restoration

    Once per turn, after either healing or preventing damage of at least equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier, you can choose:

    • Renew to add +1d6 to the healing;
    • Fortify to add +2 AC against the next attack; or
    • Steady to have Advantage on the next save.

    Emergency Grace Refund

    Once per round, when you spent 1 Divine Grace that directly prevents an ally from reaching 0 HP, you will get refunded 1 Divine Grace after resolution.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Decision / Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 4, and 1 additional spell:

    • Prayer Resolve. Action. Short Rest 1. Support.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 6 feature.

    HEALER — Deep Restoration

    Once/turn when healing a creature at/below half HP choose:

    • +WIS to that healing;
    • Delayed PB healing at start next turn if alive; or
    • PB healing to a second creature within 15 ft.

    PROTECTOR — Intercepting Grace

    Before you end your current turn, you can choose either self/ally within 10 ft of your Cleric to add +1 AC until the start of your next turn. Non-self target also gains Advantage vs Shove/forced movement while within this distance from your Cleric. Protective Grace may target an ally within 10 ft even without seeing damage source if you perceive ally.

    PURIFIER — Greater Purification

    Successful Purifying Touch can fully end listed nonmagical/ordinary afflictions, grant an immediate Advantage save vs repeat-save magic, or grant +2 next save vs same source. Diagnosis reveals broad category, ordinary-save ending, and whether effect exceeds current cleansing power.

  7. Level 7: Divine Recovery, Restorative Momentum

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, and 4 additional spells:

    • Aegis Field. Action. Cooldown 2. Area Protection.
    • Cleansing Grace. Action. Cooldown 2. Purification.
    • Judgment Seal. Action. Cooldown 2. Damage / Debuff.
    • Restoring Wave. Action. Cooldown 2. Area Healing.

    Divine Recovery

    Your Divine Recovery upgrades Divine Resilience. After you survive 1 HP instead of falling to 0, you can choose:

    • Heal yourself 2d8+WIS modifier;
    • Regain 2 Grace; or
    • give an ally within 30 ft temporary HP equivalent to your Cleric level + WIS modifier.

    Restorative Momentum

    Restorative Momentum lets a creature restored from 0 HP once/round to stand for free, then choose either to move 10 ft safely, or gain Temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus.

  8. Level 8: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 5, and 1 additional spell:

    • Circle of Purity. Action. Short Rest 1. Area Purification.

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  9. Level 9: Abundant Grace, Sacred Intervention

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Abundant Grace

    At once/round, convert a valid Divine Grace gain while already at max into healing equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus, Temporary HP equivalent to Proficiency Bonus, or +1 to the next save for an affected creature.

    Sacred Intervention

    Once per round, after supporting an ally, you can choose to apply to yourself:

    • +2 to your next attack against a visible hostile; or
    • add an additional Radiant damage equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus on your next Radiant damage spell.

    This only lasts until the start of your next turn.

  10. Level 10: Specialization Decision / Advancement, Empyrean Judgment

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 6, and 1 additional spell:

    • Consecrated Ground. Action. Short Rest 1. Zone / Support.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 10 feature.

    HEALER — Restorative Conversion

    Once/turn when Cleric spell heals, as a Bonus Action, choose one healed creature: if it is still below half its maximum HP, add +1d8 healing; otherwise another conscious creature within 15 ft of your Cleric heals for an amount equivalent to your Wisdom modifier.

    PROTECTOR — Anchoring Presence

    As a Bonus Action, choose either self or an ally within 10 ft. You or the ally within 10 ft gets +1 AC, +1 saves, first damage reduced equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus until start of your next turn. Hostiles treat spaces within 5 ft of you as difficult terrain when moving toward an ally adjacent to you.

    PURIFIER — Greater Diagnosis

    As a Bonus Action, after suppressing/removing a hostile condition, choose:

    • The same target will have Advantage to next save;
    • The hostile creature creating making that hostile status suffer -2 on its next save vs one of your Cleric spell; or
    • The target heals HP equivalent to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.

    Diagnosis also reveals maintained status, broad remaining duration, and whether suppression would allow return.

    Empyrean Judgment

    Your Cleric casts an ultimate spell move or makes a weapon attack move. Resolve the weapon or spell damage first, then the target suffers a fixed amount of 80 Radiant damage. Once resolved, choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 ft of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP: 30 + your Cleric Level.

  11. Level 11: Twin Devotion, Enhanced Greater Grace, Divine Grace Efficiency

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Twin Devotion

    Once per turn after an Action Cleric spell supports an ally, include one Devotional Act in the same Action:

    • A free spell attack within 60 ft of you for 1d8 Radiant damage;
    • Temporary HP equivalent to Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier to another creature within 30 ft of you and the allied creature which you healed earlier; or
    • 10 ft safe movement for you/affected ally.

    Enhanced Greater Grace

    Your Grace techniques are enhanced further. Your Graceful Restoration and Protective Grace now use 2d8 + your Wisdom modifier instead of the 1d8 + Wisdom modifier from Greater Grace. Your Cleansing Grace now also adds Restrained, no-Reactions, no-HP recovery, hostile AC reduction.

    Divine Grace Efficiency

    Once per turn as a free action, after spending Grace on a core technique, roll 1d6. You will get a refund of 1 Grace if you roll a 6.

  12. Level 12: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 7.

  13. Level 13: Sacred Reach, Critical Restoration

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Sacred Reach

    Once per turn, your Support spells that has an eligible 30 ft range increases this by 10 ft, or your damaging spells with a defined measured area increases dimension by 5 ft.

    Critical Restoration

    Once per round, when you heal a creature that is at or below one-quarter HP, grant an extra temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier. Target also can make a 10 ft safe movement.

    Once per round, when restoring a creature from 0 HP, you will also be refunded 1 Divine Grace, in addition to the normal Meaningfu Support Grace gain if available.

  14. Level 14: Final Specialization Decision / Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 8.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 14 feature.

    HEALER — Miracle of Life

    Once/turn when healing a creature that was at/below half HP choose:

    • +1d10 immediate healing, delayed 1d8 + WIS at target next turn if alive; or
    • second ally within 20 ft heals 1d8 + WIS.

    PROTECTOR — Unyielding Sanctuary

    While conscious project a 10-ft zone: allies gain +1 AC (this AC bonus does not stack with Anchoring Presence) and once/round reduce one ally’s damage equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus with no action. This damage reduction from Unyielding Sanctuary stacks with the Proficiency Bonus damage reduction from Anchoring Presence when both independently apply to the same damage instance.

    You gain Advantage vs shove/Prone/forced movement away from an adjacent ally. If you get a successful save, gives an ally within 5 ft temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus once/round.

    PURIFIER — Purifying Aegis

    When using Purifying Touch or a Cleric spell to cleanse, spend 1 Grace to:

    • Strengthen into an Advantage save vs control,
    • Remove one hostile magical penalty to AC/attacks/saves/movement/HP recovery, or
    • End ordinary disease/poison/bleed/curse-like weakening within capability.

    After successful removal, cleaned target gains resistance to one observed source damage type until the start of your next turn.

  15. Level 15: Sustained Grace

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 9, and 4 additional spells:

    • Divine Bastion. Action. Short Rest 1. Apex Protection.
    • Heavenly Verdict. Action. Short Rest 1. Apex Damage / Control.
    • Miracle of Life. Action. Long Rest 1. Apex Healing.
    • Sanctified Restoration. Action. Short Rest 1. Apex Purification.

    Sustained Grace

    Sustained Grace introduces Grace Momentum 0–3: gain 1 Grace once per round from Meaningful Support, lose 1 at end of turn if no meaningful support since previous turn. This is separate tracking and depending on how many Grace Momentum you have by the start of your turn, you can do the following:

    • At 1, the first Grace spend each turn gives +1 to your next Cleric spell attack or +1 to an ally’s next save;
    • At 2, a meaningful support spell enables 10-ft safe movement; or
    • At 3, the first Grace technique each turn costs 1 less, minimum 0.

    Empyrean Judgment

    Your Cleric casts an ultimate spell move or makes a weapon attack move. Resolve the weapon or spell damage first, then the target suffers a fixed amount of 100 Radiant damage. Once resolved, choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus within 40 ft of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP: 40 + your Cleric Level + Proficiency Bonus.

  16. Level 16: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain +2 Spell Advancements, and universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  17. Level 17: Apex Grace, Greater Twin Devotion

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 9.

    Apex Grace

    Your Grace techniques are now at the apex level. Your Graceful Restoration and Protective Grace now use 3d8 + your Wisdom modifier instead of the 2d8 + Wisdom modifier from the previous tier. Your Cleansing Grace now also adds Charmed, no voluntary movement, no Bonus Actions, hostile Disadvantage on attacks/saves.

    Greater Twin Devotion

    Your Twin Devotion is now enhanced with more options:

    • Additional healing of 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier; or
    • Choose yourself or one creature affected by the triggering Cleric spell within 30 ft of you. Until the start of your next turn, the first attack roll made against that creature has Disadvantage, the effect ends after that attack resolves.

  18. Level 18: Divine Presence, Unbroken Grace, Grace Momentum Mastery

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, and 1 additional spell:

    • Divine Intervention. Reaction. Long Rest 1. Apex Support.

    Divine Presence

    Divine Presence replaces Sacred Presence with a 15-ft aura: you and allies gain Advantage on Death Saves; the first healing each round received by each individual ally grants Temporary HP equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus.

    You and allies within this aura gain Advantage vs Frightened/forced movement.

    Unbroken Grace

    As a Reaction, you regain 1 Grace when a visible ally within 60 ft falls to 0 HP.

    Grace Momentum Mastery

    Your Grace momentum only decays if you neither meaningfully supported since prior turn nor spent Grace this turn.

  19. Level 19: Empyrean Boon

    Gain +2 Spell Advancements and the universal Empyrean Boon.

  20. Level 20: Perfect Cleric

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements.

    Perfect Grace

    You now start genuine combat with Divine Grace equivalent to the higher of its current value or 3, capped at maximum..

    Perfect Sustained Grace

    You also begin combat with 1 Grace Momentum. At At 3 Momentum, the first paid Grace technique each turn triggers two independent Divine Grace Efficiency rolls; each roll showing a 6 on a d6 refunds 1 Grace, and both rolls can refund Grace.

    Miracle Intervention

    Once per Long Rest, as a Reaction, you can keep an ally within 60 ft at 1 HP instead of 0, heals that ally 4d8 + your Wisdom modifier. After healing the allied creature or target, you can select to either have the target stand freely or a temporary HP equivalent to your Cleric level.

    Divine Presence

    Your Divine Presence expands to 20 ft and as a Reaction may spend 1 Grace to reroll an ally’s failed save vs Fear, Charm, Poison, forced movement, or Speed 0

    Empyrean Judgment

    Your Cleric casts an ultimate spell move or makes a weapon attack move. Resolve the weapon or spell damage first, then the target suffers a fixed amount of 120 Radiant damage. Once resolved, choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus within 60 ft of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP: 50 + your Cleric Level + your current Wisdom Ability Score.

How to Play Cleric

The Cleric is a durable frontline support spellcaster. Your job is not simply to restore HP after damage has happened: you prevent dangerous damage, restore allies when their HP is genuinely low, suppress hostile effects, and keep the party functioning under pressure. Divine Grace rewards meaningful intervention, so focus on support that changes the outcome of an encounter.

1. Your Role and Gameplay Loop

Your Role as a Cleric

You are a frontline tank-support caster with three central responsibilities: keep allies alive, reduce or prevent incoming harm, and remove hostile effects that interfere with the party. Your d8 Hit Die and additional +2 HP per Cleric level after 1 make you more durable than the d6 full casters, allowing you to operate closer to the front when the situation requires it.

Your primary objectives are to:

  • Identify which ally is in genuine danger rather than automatically healing whoever has taken any damage.
  • Prevent significant damage before it lands whenever a Reaction or protective feature can do so.
  • Use healing spells to restore injured allies and combine them with Grace techniques when the extra benefit matters.
  • Suppress or remove important hostile conditions and effects.
  • Maintain enough Divine Grace for an emergency instead of spending it whenever possible.

Your Core Gameplay Loop

  1. Assess: Check allies’ HP, active conditions, and immediate threats.
  2. Intervene: Heal a seriously injured ally, prevent meaningful damage, or suppress a significant hostile effect.
  3. Generate: When your intervention qualifies as Meaningful Support, regain 1 Divine Grace once per round.
  4. Enhance: Spend Grace on Restoration, Protection, or Cleansing when the additional effect is worth its cost.
  5. Stabilize: Use your aura, specialization, and follow-up features to protect the party after the initial intervention.
  6. Adapt: Return to damage, control, or positioning when the party is stable, then respond to the next genuine threat.
Your JobFrontline healing, damage prevention, purification, and emergency protection.
Your ResourceDivine Grace, generated by Meaningful Support and spent on enhanced healing, damage reduction, cleansing, and other Cleric features.
Your PositionStay close enough to support allies and protect the frontline, but avoid exposing yourself to threats that would force you to spend every resource on self-preservation.

2. Divine Grace and Meaningful Support

Generating Divine Grace

Your Divine Grace maximum equals your Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier, with a minimum maximum of 2. Once per round, when you meaningfully support yourself or an ally with a Cleric spell or feature, regain 1 Grace. The once-per-round refresh occurs at the start of your turn, allowing a qualifying Reaction during an enemy’s turn without giving you two gains in the same round.

Meaningful Support requires an actual result:

  • Restore HP to a creature that was below half its maximum HP.
  • Prevent or reduce damage by at least an amount equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • Suppress or remove a mechanically significant hostile condition or effect.

Healing a full-HP creature, granting Temporary HP by itself, preventing 0 damage, refreshing an unchanged Temporary HP pool, or providing only a passive aura or AC bonus does not qualify. Temporary HP are not healing and cannot restore a creature from 0 HP. A secondary rider created by another Cleric feature does not recursively trigger another support sequence unless its rule explicitly says so.

Spending Grace at the Right Time

  • Graceful Restoration: Spend 1 Grace when a Cleric spell heals to add healing to one creature and one healing instance. It does not automatically enhance every target of a multi-target spell.
  • Protective Grace: Spend 1 Grace as a Reaction when a visible creature within 30 feet takes damage to reduce that damage.
  • Cleansing Grace: Spend 1 Grace when a Cleric spell heals to suppress eligible hostile conditions or effects. Its eligible list expands as you level.

Do not spend all your Grace on routine improvements. Keep enough available to respond to a dangerous hit or a debilitating condition. A Long Rest restores all Grace; a Short Rest restores an amount equal to your Proficiency Bonus, up to your maximum.

3. Healing, Protection, and Purification

Heal When It Changes the Situation

Your strongest support turns usually begin when an ally is below half HP, is at risk of being reduced to 0 HP, or needs to recover from a dangerous condition. Healing Doctrine adds your Proficiency Bonus to qualifying healing once per turn. Graceful Restoration adds further healing to one selected healing instance, while Healer specialization features improve healing and can convert eligible excess healing into Temporary HP.

Restoring a creature from 0 HP is an emergency intervention, not a substitute for preventing the knockout in the first place. At higher levels, Restorative Momentum and Critical Restoration improve the follow-up after a creature is brought back.

Prevent Damage Before It Happens

Protective Grace is a Reaction, so you can use it when an ally is hit outside your turn. Protector features add more ways to reduce damage, intercept threats, improve AC, and keep nearby allies safe. Check whether another feature already reduces the same damage instance: for example, Sacred Intercession cannot combine with Protective Grace on that same instance.

Remove the Effect, Not Just Its Consequence

Use Cleansing Grace when a healing spell can also suppress an eligible hostile effect. Purifying Touch gives you an Action-based purification option without spending Grace, but it requires you to be within 5 feet and uses a Purification Check against the effect DC. The Purifier specialization adds diagnosis and stronger cleansing options. Remember that suppression is not automatically permanent removal; follow the specific feature and the hostile effect’s rules.

Graceful Channeling, Empowered Restoration, Twin Devotion, and later support features give you additional choices after a support spell. Choose the benefit that answers the current threat rather than selecting the same option every time.

4. Positioning, Flight, and Aerial Combat

Hold a Useful Frontline Position

You can fight with a Mace, Hammer, Staff, or Quarterstaff and use your approved shield access. You do not need to equip an implement to cast Cleric spells. Position yourself so that allies are within the ranges of your healing, protection, and aura features, while keeping a route to safety if enemies focus on you. Your Reaction is valuable: decide whether a likely incoming attack, an Opportunity Attack, or another reaction-based feature deserves it.

Manage Combat Flight Slots

Flight uses the universal Combat Flight rules, not the Exploration Flight time table. In Stable Aether, a stable airborne Daeva spends 1 Flight Slot at the end of each turn. A grounded Daeva recovers 1 Flight Slot at the end of each grounded turn. A Short Rest restores half your maximum Flight Slots, rounded down; a Long Rest restores all. Your Cleric progression lists your Flight Slot maximum by level.

  • Levels 1–3: 2 Flight Slots.
  • Levels 4–6: 3 Flight Slots.
  • Levels 7–9: 4 Flight Slots.
  • Levels 10–12: 5 Flight Slots.
  • Levels 13–16: 6 Flight Slots.
  • Levels 17–19: 7 Flight Slots.
  • Level 20: 8 Flight Slots.

When your Flight Slots are depleted while airborne, you immediately fall. You may use your Reaction for a DC 12 Dexterity save; on a success, halve the fall damage. A feature such as Controlled Descent may change the result. Depleted Aether also prevents Aether-supported flight, while Saturated Aether changes the normal Flight Slot expenditure rules; use the current Flight rules for those state transitions.

Use Altitude to Protect and Support

Combat altitude is divided into low, middle, and high tiers, separated by 30 feet. Melee attacks require the attacker and target to be within 5 feet. Descending at least 25 feet before an attack grants Advantage under the aerial combat rules. Physical ranged attacks made from a higher altitude increase their maximum range by half the vertical distance. A lower-altitude physical ranged attacker has Advantage against a higher-altitude winged airborne target because its wings are exposed; being higher than a target does not itself grant Advantage.

As a Cleric, altitude is a positioning tool, not a reason to abandon your support role. Stay within the actual range and line-of-effect requirements of the spell or feature you intend to use. A higher position can help you reach or observe allies, but it does not automatically extend a spell’s range. Keep track of vertical distance when measuring ranges and when deciding whether an ally is still within an aura or Reaction feature.

Gliding Is an Exploration Rule

Exploration Glide is separate from Combat Flight Slots. It requires at least 30 feet of descent, functioning open wings, and an Aether State that permits flight. It travels 3 feet horizontally for each 1 foot descended, consumes neither Exploration Flight Time nor Combat Flight Slots, always loses altitude, and has a 1-hour cooldown. Do not treat gliding as a way to remain airborne indefinitely during combat.

5. Choose and Develop Your Specialization

Your Specialization determines which part of the Cleric role receives the strongest improvements. You choose a path at Level 3 and gain further specialization features at Levels 6, 10, and 14. At the later decision levels, you may continue your current path or reassign according to the class’s specialization rules.

  • Healer: Choose this path if you want to maximize direct healing, improve recovery for badly injured allies, and make better use of healing overflow.
  • Protector: Choose this path if you want to prevent damage, intercept attacks, strengthen nearby allies’ defenses, and hold a safer frontline.
  • Purifier: Choose this path if you want to identify hostile effects, suppress debilitating conditions, and remove afflictions that ordinary healing does not solve.

Build your spell choices and tactical habits around your specialization, but do not neglect the other parts of the Cleric role. A Healer still benefits from damage prevention; a Protector still needs to restore HP; and a Purifier still needs to keep allies alive while identifying what must be cleansed.

6. Advance Your Support and Use Your Ultimate Wisely

Grow Into a More Efficient Support Caster

Your class progression adds more than larger healing numbers. Greater Grace and Apex Grace improve your core techniques; Divine Grace Efficiency can refund spent Grace; Abundant Grace converts an otherwise wasted Grace gain; Sacred Reach extends eligible support or spell areas; and Sustained Grace adds a separate Grace Momentum track that rewards continued meaningful support.

At higher levels, Divine Presence strengthens your aura, Unbroken Grace helps you recover resource when an ally falls, and Perfect Cleric improves your opening resource state and emergency intervention. Track each feature’s timing separately. Once-per-turn, once-per-round, Reaction, and Long Rest limits are not interchangeable.

Empyrean Judgment

Empyrean Judgment costs 100 Daeva Points and consumes both your Action and Bonus Action. It makes one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet, then applies its fixed Radiant damage and restores HP to you and selected allies within its listed range. Its damage, healing, and range improve at the specified levels. It is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, additional-action effects, Reactions, or similar features. After it resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Use Empyrean Judgment when its combined offensive and restorative impact can meaningfully change the encounter. Before activating it, confirm that both your Action and Bonus Action are available and that the intended target and allies are within the appropriate ranges.

A successful Cleric does more than keep the party’s HP totals high. You recognize the moment that needs intervention, choose the right form of support, preserve Divine Grace for the next crisis, and maintain a position from which your allies can continue fighting.
