“I am never alone. I have my Spirit with me.”

The premier sustained melee damage class, built around deliberate attack sequences, Momentum, Chain Links, and Finishers.

The Spiritmaster is a two-body tactical caster. One active elemental spirit shares the Spiritmaster’s turn and action economy; together they equal one complete player character. The class establishes two battlefield positions, casts through the spirit, and earns Spirit Bond only through meaningful master-spirit coordination.

Class Identity

Spiritmasters are binders and commanders of elemental spirits who fight as a partnership rather than as a solitary caster. Their strength comes from coordinating their own spellcraft with a Greater Spirit, applying pressure, disruption, and control across more than one position on the battlefield. Spirit Bond represents the relationship and command structure that makes this divided presence function as one class.

Tradition & Outlook

The Spiritmaster tradition develops from Atreia’s mage disciplines but turns outward toward spirits, elemental forces, and the difficult art of directing entities shaped by Aether. Spiritmasters are often patient observers, researchers, summoners, or specialists in threats that cannot be understood through ordinary physical study. Their beliefs about spirits differ: some emphasize command, others partnership, responsibility, curiosity, or respect for elemental power.

Appearance & Adventuring

Spiritmasters use cloth armor and magical implements, while the visible presence of a summoned spirit often defines their silhouette more strongly than clothing does. Their equipment may include ritual, scholarly, or elemental motifs without creating additional mechanics. They adventure to understand unusual spirits, recover lost summoning knowledge, counter supernatural threats, serve as versatile magical operatives, or deepen the bond between Daeva and elemental ally.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Spirit Bond

Generation: Once on each of your turns, gain 1 Spirit Bond if both you and your active spirit successfully affect hostile creatures during your turn. This includes a hit, actual damage, a failed save, meaningful hostil condition/penalty, forced movement, or another real combat effects. Merely targeting a hostile or applying an effect to which it is immune does not. Normal Bond generation is once on each of your turns. The master and spirit do not need to affect the same hostile unless a specific features says so.

Starting State and Reset: Spirit Bond begins at 0 when genuine combat starts and resets to 0 when combat ends. Artifical combat and initiatives cannot preload it.

Bond Techniques

  • Bonded Assault – Costs 1 Spirit Bond and done on Spirit Hit. Add 1d6 damage of that spirit's Basic Attack damage type; d8 beginning Level 2, d10 beginning Level 5, and 2d10 beginning at Level 17. Independent damage; not doubled on a critical hit. No Action required.
  • Protective Bond – Costs 1 Spirit Bond. When you or your spirit takes attack damage while within 30 ft of the other, use your Reaction to reduce the total damage from that triggering attack by 1d6 + INT; d8 + INT at L5; 2d8 + INT at L17. Apply the reduction once to the attack’s combined damage total, including all damage types and components.
  • Spirit Recall – Costs 1 Spirit Bond. As a Bonus Action, teleport spirit to within 5 ft of you, or if already within 5 ft to a visible space within 30 ft; 40 ft at L5. L17 may exchange positions within 60 ft if perceived through Spirit Sight. This teleportation and position exchange do not provoke Opportunity Attacks; destinations must be legal, unoccupied spaces.

Summon Spirits and Elemental Spirits

A Spiritmaster spirit is a Large creature that occupies a 4-grid space. All spirits hover and remain displaced above the ground. They do not need to land. They can move vertically using their available speed. Fire, Water, and Earth spirits have a Speed of 30 ft; Wind has a Speed of 40 ft. Hovering and vertical movement do not by themselves prevent Opportunity Attacks; a spirit can provoke normally when it voluntarily moves out of an enemy's reach. Teleportation and movement that explicitly avoids Opportunity Attacks remain exceptions.

  • The Spiritmaster and spirit share one Reaction. If either uses it, neither can use another Reaction until the start of the Spiritmaster's next turn.
  • You know all four fundamental spirits—Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind—and choose among them tactically. Only one Spiritmaster spirit may be summoned at a time. Summoning another spirit will disperse the other one that is currently active. Summoning uses an Action and places the spirit in a visible unoccupied space within 15 ft.
  • A defeated spirit disperses into Aether and makes no death saves. Recovery depends on your level, and you may resummon it at certain HP levels depending on your Spiritmaster Level. Only a spirit actually reduced to 0 HP qualifies for reconstitution. Voluntary dismissal or replacement never qualifies for defeat recovery. Outside combat a newly summoned spirit appears at full HP; voluntary replacement during combat preserves the dismissed spirit's current HP percentage rather than healing by switching forms.
  • The spirit shares your Initiative and acts during your turn. It moves normally during your turn without consuming your action.
  • You normally spend your Bonus Action to command its basic Spirit Action. If not commanded, it may move normally, Dodge, or remain in place; it may move and Dodge during the same turn. It does not need a Bonus Action to move or Dodge when commanded.
  • A spirit normally makes only one basic Spirit Attack per turn.
  • Spirit attacks are class features, not spells. Spirit AC never inherits your armor or personal AC bonuses.
  • The spirit has its own designated Chassis separate from your own as described below.

Ultimate Ability

Primal ConvergenceUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Convergent Damage: The attack deals the normal spell damage plus fixed additional damage of the ability.

Primal Suppression. The target's Speed becomes 0, it cannot take Reactions, and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of your next turn. At Level 15, the suppression instead lasts until the end of your next turn. At Level 20, the target also cannot benefit from increases to its Speed or teleport while suppressed. This is a bespoke restriction, not Stunned or Incapacitated.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

SPIRIT CALLER

The Spirit Caller places its spirit companion at the center of its combat strategy, strengthening its attacks, persistence, and eventual ascendance. It is suited to players who want to command a powerful elemental companion and make their spirit a major source of battlefield damage.

HEX BINDER

The Hexbinder weakens opponents through persistent curses and debilitating magical effects. Its abilities deepen the impact of hostile hexes, allowing it to undermine enemy effectiveness and make important targets increasingly vulnerable.

BONDWEAVER

The Bondweaver strengthens the connection between Spiritmaster and spirit, improving resource efficiency, shared protection, and overall resilience. Rather than focusing solely on the spirit's offensive power, this path develops a more durable and adaptable partnership.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Spellcasting, Summon Spirit, Spirit Bond, Bond Techniques, Shared Aether

    Spellcasting

    Maximum Spell Level 1 and you gain Spiritmaster spellcasting and 6 spells:

    • Gale Sigil. Action. At-Will. Damage / Positioning
    • Spirit Bolt. Action. At-Will. Damage
    • Spirit Flame. Action. At-Will. Damage / Bond Setup
    • Spirit Mend. Action. At-Will. Repair / Healing
    • Stone Mark. Action. At-Will. Damage / Protection Setup
    • Water Sigil. Action. At-Will. Debuff

    Spirit Bond and Bond Techniques

    Your maximum Spirit Bond is 3 and you can use them on Bond Techniques according to their designated timing rules.

    Shared Aether

    Once on each of your turns when you cast a Spiritmaster spell that affects a hostile creature within 10 feet of your spirit, choose one:

    • Channel Through Spirit. makes your spell to come from your spirit's location instead of yours. Measure the spell's range from where the spirit is standing, even though you are still the one casting it.
    • Resonant Target. gives +1 to the spell attack or +1 to the first save DC against one creature adjacent to your spirit.

    Only AOE spells that has defined range and area like Cone, Line, Sphere, are considered eligible spells.

  2. Level 2: Spirit Link, Aetheric Command

    You now begin to gain +2 Spell Advancements every time you level up.

    Your Maximum Spell Level is 2. Use the gained Spell Advancements to increase the level of spells up to the spell level maximum. You can also bank in your spell advancements to increase the spell level of a certain spell when you obtain them.

    Your Spirit Bond maximum becomes 4.

    Spirit Link

    As a Bonus Action, while your spirit is within 60 ft, you may perceive through the spirit. Until the start of your next turn, attack rolls against you or your spirit have Disadvantage as long as your spirit is within 30 ft from you.

    Aetheric Command

    Once each turn, when you command your active spirit to take its Spirit Action, choose one command mode:

    • Aggressive – When the spirit's basic Spirit Attack hits, it deals additional damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. This does not double on a critical hit.
    • Guarding – The spirit uses Dodge and protects an adjacent creature; while guarding, it may use its Reaction to impose Disadvantage on an attack targeting that protected creature.
    • Mobile – The spirit may move up to half its Speed as part of the Spirit Action. This move is added on top of the current Speed of the spirit or whether it has already repositioned or not.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, and 1 additional spell:

    • Hex of Frailty. Action. Cooldown 2. Debuff

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    SPIRIT CALLER — Empowered Spirit

    Once per turn on a basic Spirit Attack hit, add one attack die: Fire +1d8; Water, Earth, or Wind +1d6, doubled on a critical hit. When spending Bonded Assault, the spirit may move 5 feet without provoking an Opportunity Attack from the creature hit.

    HEXBINDER — Cursed ConduitOnce per turn after a creature fails a Spiritmaster spell save, choose one until your next turn:

    • Enfeeble reduces its next damage by PB (minimum 1);
    • Hinder reduces its Speed by 10 feet, or
    • Expose gives the next Spirit Attack against it +2.

    When Shared Aether originates a save spell from the spirit, one creature within 5 feet of the spirit takes -1 on that save.

    BONDWEAVER — Efficient Bond

    Increase maximum Spirit Bond by 1. Once per turn when you spend Bond, roll 1d6; on a 6, regain 1 Bond after the feature resolves. Protective Bond used on a creature within 15 feet reduces the triggering damage by an additional PB.

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 3, universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level.

  5. Level 5: Coordinated Manifestation

    Gain +2 Spell Advancements. You also gained 1 additional spell:

    • Shared Aether Pulse. Action. Short Rest 1. Dual-Origin Support.

    Coordinated Manifestation

    When both you and your active spirit successfully affect hostile creatures during your turn, choose its listed benefit:

    • 1d8 additional Force damage (does not double on critical);
    • 5 ft Speed reduction;
    • Temporary HP to you or your active spirit equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + INT modifier.

    When you perform a qualifying action on your turn, both Coordinated Manifestation and Perfect Coordination (level 15) activate at the same time as separate abilities. You can only trigger each of these features once per turn, and you apply their bonuses individually rather than combining them into a single effect. Additionally, any extra actions or bonus effects created by one of these abilities cannot be used to set off the other one in a chain reaction.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Advancement

    You gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 4 and 1 additional spell:

    • Spirit Erosion. Action. Cooldown 2. Damage / Debuff.

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 6 feature.

    SPIRIT CALLER — Relentless Spirit

    Once/turn on basic hit:

    • Fire deals INT Fire at start target next turn;
    • Water adds another -5 Speed;
    • Earth gives Disadvantage on target next attack;
    • Wind moves spirit 10 ft safely.

    It cannot stack immediate damage with later Ascendance unless one effect is delayed/control/movement.

    HEXBINDER — Malignant Echo

    Once/turn after a failed Spiritmaster save, choose a creature within 5 ft of spirit:

    • -2 next save vs your Spiritmaster spell,
    • -2 modifier to its next dealt damage,
    • or no Reactions until your next turn.

    BONDWEAVER — Resonant Bond

    Once/turn after normal Bond generation choose:

    • you/spirit heal equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus;
    • both move 5 ft safely;
    • or next Protective Bond before your turn reduces +PB.
