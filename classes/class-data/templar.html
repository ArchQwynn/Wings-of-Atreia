Core Mechanics & Resources

Resolve

Generation: You gain Resolve whenever the following instances occur even outside your own turn as long as within genuine combat:

  • Hold the Line: gain 1 Resolve when a hostile creature you can see hits you with a weapon or spell attack. AOE weapon or spell attacks are included.
  • Protect the Vulnerable: gain 1 Resolve when a Templar class feature successfully reduces, prevents, redirects, or intercepts damage that would otherwise be dealt to another creature, or when a feature explicitly says the protection qualifies.
  • Defy the Templar: gain 1 Resolve when a creature Challenged by you attacks another creature while you were a valid target for that attack. Ranged and spell attacks are included.
  • You can normally gain no more than 1 Resolve during any hostile creature’s turn. One event grants Resolve only once even if it satisfies multiple triggers.
  • Note: Inflicting self-damage or damage from allies will not generate Resolve for your Templar.

Expenditure: Gained Resolve is used to spend specified Templar abilities that can affect or change the outcome of the hostile creature's attack as listed under Signature Defensive Mechanics.

Resolve Timing and Signature Defensive Mechanics

Resolve represents the Templar converting battlefield pressure into defensive certainty. Unless a later feature states otherwise, Resolve becomes 0 when Initiative is rolled, becomes 0 when combat ends, and cannot be gained while you are Incapacitated.

  • Resolve generated by Hold the Line can be spent immediately on Resolute Guard to mitigate the triggering attack. When used this way, you only reduce the damage by half of your total Guard die rounded up + CON modifier roll.
  • Resolve generated by a successful Guardian's Intercession is gained before redirected damage is applied, so it may be used against the redirected attack if otherwise legal.
  • Resolve generated because Guardian's Shelter changed a save from failure to success may immediately pay for Resolute Shelter at Level 14.
  • Challenge may remain on a creature while its penalty is temporarily suppressed because you are out of range, cannot be perceived, or are not a legal target. Explicit rules apply.

Resolve Expenditure

Resolute Guard. Costs 1 Resolve. When you take damage from an attack, spend 1 Resolve with no action required to reduce that damage by 1 Guard Die + your CON modifier, minimum 0. You normally use Resolute Guard only once per turn unless a Templar feature explicitly says otherwise.

Shield Bash. Costs 1 Resolve. As a Bonus Action, bash and push the target that is within your melee range. Target must roll a STR saving throw and beat your Class Save DC. Upon failure, target gets pushed 5 ft and would trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. You will not get an OA when the target was moved away from your reach using this ability. Success will not force move the target. Targets that are at least 1 size larger than you will have Advantage.

Guardian's Intercession. Costs 2 Resolve. Before an enemy makes an attack roll against an ally within 5 feet of you, you may expend your Reaction and the requisite Resolve to intercept the attack, becoming the new target. Resolve the attack roll against your own Armor Class (AC); on a miss, the attack has no effect, and on a hit, you take half as much damage (rounded up). Because preventing your ally from taking damage satisfies the conditions of Protect the Vulnerable, you regain 1 Resolve regardless of whether the attack hits or misses, though you do not gain an additional Resolve from sustaining the damage yourself.

Improved Shield Bash. Costs 2 Resolve. Level 7 ability. As a Bonus Action, you can bash and push the target that is within your melee range. Target rolls a STR saving throw and beat your Class Save DC. Upon failure, target is pushed 10 ft and would trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. You will not get an OA when the target was moved away from your reach using this ability. Success will only push target 5 ft but will not trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. Targets that are at least 1 size larger than you will have Advantage.

Seismic Slam. Costs 4 Resolve. Level 18 ability. As a bonus action, you bash a target within your melee reach with a forceful push. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Class Save DC or be pushed 10 feet away, which provokes opportunity attacks from your allies. You immediately move into the horizontal space the target vacated, and this forced movement does not provoke an opportunity attack from you. Additionally, any hostile creatures adjacent to the target when you activate this ability must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Class Save DC, taking damage equal to half the damage dealt by this ability (rounded down) on a failed save. Success will only force move the target by 5 ft and cannot trigger OA. Targets that are at least 2 sizes larger than you have Advantage. Secondary targets do not have Advantage or Disadvantage to the saving throw regardless of their size.

Ultimate Ability

Empyrean BulwarkUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate melee weapon attack against a creature within your reach. After this Ultimate resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Damage: The attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Radiant damage of the ability.

Empyrean Bulwark. Choose yourself and a number of allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Each gains Resistance to all damage and cannot be moved against its will until the start of your next turn.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output, Miss Choice, and Critical Hits. Damage or healing printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

Bulwark

The Bulwark is dedicated to protecting allies by placing itself between them and incoming danger. It strengthens Guardian's Intercession, improves defensive resource usage, and gains increasingly powerful ways to withstand attacks intended for its companions.

Justicar

The Justicar punishes enemies that ignore its Challenge and threaten its allies. Through retaliatory attacks, movement restrictions, and increasingly severe punishments, it turns enemy aggression into an opportunity to control and weaken opponents.

Sentinel

The Sentinel controls the battlefield by making the space around it dangerous for hostile creatures to cross. It excels at defending chokepoints, restricting enemy movement, and preventing opponents from simply passing through its reach to attack vulnerable allies.
