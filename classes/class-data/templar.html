“You're only as good as your armor.”

The premier martial defender, turning incoming pressure into protection, damage reduction, retaliation, and formation control.

The Templar is the system’s premier martial defender. It does not force enemies to attack it through mind control; instead, Challenge makes ignoring the Templar tactically dangerous while Resolve turns incoming pressure into protection, damage reduction, retaliation, and formation control.

Class Identity

Templars are guardian-warriors whose strength is measured by what they can endure and whom they can keep standing. They occupy the most dangerous space in a battle, challenge enemies that would otherwise overwhelm vulnerable allies, and turn personal resilience into protection for the group. Resolve is the mechanical expression of this identity: a Templar grows most meaningful when danger is real and protection requires commitment.

Tradition & Outlook

The Templar tradition grows from Atreia’s warrior disciplines but places duty, discipline, guardianship, and control above personal glory. Templars are found among legion defenders, fortress guardians, sworn protectors, escorts, and Daevas who believe power carries an obligation to stand between danger and others. Individual Templars may be stern, compassionate, devout, pragmatic, or fiercely patriotic, but the class ideal is constancy under pressure.

Appearance & Adventuring

Templars are commonly associated with plate armor, shields, and the unmistakable silhouette of a heavily protected frontline Daeva. Their equipment tends to emphasize readiness and endurance rather than ornament alone. Templars adventure because someone must hold the line: to defend a community, fulfill an oath, guard companions, reclaim threatened territory, or confront enemies that cannot safely be allowed to reach the rest of the party.
