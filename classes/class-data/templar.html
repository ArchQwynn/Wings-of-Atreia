Core Mechanics & Resources

Resolve

Generation: You gain Resolve whenever the following instances occur even outside your own turn as long as within genuine combat:

  • Hold the Line: gain 1 Resolve when a hostile creature you can see hits you with a weapon or spell attack. AOE weapon or spell attacks are included.
  • Protect the Vulnerable: gain 1 Resolve when a Templar class feature successfully reduces, prevents, redirects, or intercepts damage that would otherwise be dealt to another creature, or when a feature explicitly says the protection qualifies.
  • Defy the Templar: gain 1 Resolve when a creature Challenged by you attacks another creature while you were a valid target for that attack. Ranged and spell attacks are included.
  • You can normally gain no more than 1 Resolve during any hostile creature’s turn. One event grants Resolve only once even if it satisfies multiple triggers.
  • Note: Inflicting self-damage or damage from allies will not generate Resolve for your Templar.

Expenditure: Gained Resolve is used to spend specified Templar abilities that can affect or change the outcome of the hostile creature's attack as listed under Signature Defensive Mechanics.

Resolve Timing and Signature Defensive Mechanics

Resolve represents the Templar converting battlefield pressure into defensive certainty. Unless a later feature states otherwise, Resolve becomes 0 when Initiative is rolled, becomes 0 when combat ends, and cannot be gained while you are Incapacitated.

  • Resolve generated by Hold the Line can be spent immediately on Resolute Guard to mitigate the triggering attack. When used this way, you only reduce the damage by half of your total Guard die rounded up + CON modifier roll.
  • Resolve generated by a successful Guardian's Intercession is gained before redirected damage is applied, so it may be used against the redirected attack if otherwise legal.
  • Resolve generated because Guardian's Shelter changed a save from failure to success may immediately pay for Resolute Shelter at Level 13.
  • Challenge may remain on a creature while its penalty is temporarily suppressed because you are out of range, cannot be perceived, or are not a legal target. Explicit rules apply.

Resolve Expenditure

Resolute Guard. Costs 1 Resolve. When you take damage from an attack, spend 1 Resolve with no action required to reduce that damage by 1 Guard Die + your CON modifier, minimum 0. You normally use Resolute Guard only once per turn unless a Templar feature explicitly says otherwise.

Shield Bash. Costs 1 Resolve. As a Bonus Action, bash and push the target that is within your melee range. Target must roll a STR saving throw and beat your Class Save DC. Upon failure, target gets pushed 5 ft and would trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. You will not get an OA when the target was moved away from your reach using this ability. Success will not force move the target. Targets that are at least 1 size larger than you will have Advantage.

Guardian's Intercession. Costs 2 Resolve. Before an enemy makes an attack roll against an ally within 5 feet of you, you may expend your Reaction and the requisite Resolve to intercept the attack, becoming the new target. Resolve the attack roll against your own Armor Class (AC); on a miss, the attack has no effect, and on a hit, you take half as much damage (rounded up). Because preventing your ally from taking damage satisfies the conditions of Protect the Vulnerable, you regain 1 Resolve regardless of whether the attack hits or misses, though you do not gain an additional Resolve from sustaining the damage yourself.

Improved Shield Bash. Costs 2 Resolve. Level 7 ability. As a Bonus Action, you can bash and push the target that is within your melee range. Target rolls a STR saving throw and beat your Class Save DC. Upon failure, target is pushed 10 ft and would trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. You will not get an OA when the target was moved away from your reach using this ability. Success will only push target 5 ft but will not trigger Opportunity Attack from your allies. Targets that are at least 1 size larger than you will have Advantage.

Guardian's Rally. Costs 1 Resolve. Level 13 ability. As a Bonus Action, spend 1 Resolve and choose yourself or an ally within 10 ft. Roll your current Guard Die; the creature gains temporary HP equal to the result + CON modifier until the start of your next turn. Guardian’s Rally does not itself generate Resolve or trigger Protect the Vulnerable when those temporary HP absorb damage.

Seismic Slam. Costs 4 Resolve. Level 18 ability. As a bonus action, you bash a target within your melee reach with a forceful push. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your Class Save DC or be pushed 10 feet away, which provokes opportunity attacks from your allies except you. You immediately move into the horizontal space the target vacated, and this movement does not provoke an opportunity attack towards you. Additionally, any hostile creatures adjacent to the target when you activate this ability must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Class Save DC, taking damage equal to half the damage dealt by this ability (rounded down) on a failed save. Success will only force move the target by 5 ft and cannot trigger OA. Targets that are at least 2 sizes larger than you have Advantage. Secondary targets do not have Advantage or Disadvantage to the saving throw regardless of their size.

Refuse to Fall. Costs 3 Resolve. Level 20 ability. Once per Long Rest, when damage would reduce you to 0 HP, you will survive and will only drop to 2 HP instead. No action required. This does not grant you any Resolve.

Ultimate Ability

Empyrean AegisUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate melee weapon attack against a creature within your reach. After this Ultimate resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Damage: The attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Radiant damage of the ability.

Empyrean Aegis. Choose yourself and a number of allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Each gains Resistance to all damage and cannot be moved against its will until the start of your next turn.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output, Miss Choice, and Critical Hits. Damage or healing printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

BULWARK

The Bulwark is dedicated to protecting allies by placing itself between them and incoming danger. It strengthens Guardian's Intercession, improves defensive resource usage, and gains increasingly powerful ways to withstand attacks intended for its companions.

JUSTICAR

The Justicar punishes enemies that ignore its Challenge and threaten its allies. Through retaliatory attacks, movement restrictions, and increasingly severe punishments, it turns enemy aggression into an opportunity to control and weaken opponents.

SENTINEL

The Sentinel controls the battlefield by making the space around it dangerous for hostile creatures to cross. It excels at defending chokepoints, restricting enemy movement, and preventing opponents from simply passing through its reach to attack vulnerable allies.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Level Details

  1. Level 1: Resolve, Challenge, Resolute Guard, Guardian's Intercession, Shield Bash

    Resolve

    Your maximum Resolve is 3. You gain 1 Resolve as indicated in your Resolve mechanics.

    Challenge

    As a Bonus Action, you can designate one hostile creature you can see within 30 feet as Challenged until the start of your next turn, though you can initially only maintain one Challenge at a time. While the target can perceive you, stays within 30 feet, and is able to target you with at least one of its attacks, it suffers Disadvantage on attack rolls made against anyone other than you. This effect is nonmagical, requires no saving throw, and does not count as a Charm or Fear effect, nor does it end if the target becomes Incapacitated. The Challenge ultimately expires after 10 rounds or immediately if you issue a new Challenge that exceeds your active limit.

    Guardian's Intercession

    You gain the Guardian's Intercession technique as described in your Resolve Expenditure section. Your Guard Die is d6.

    Shield Bash

    Spend the required Resolve to deal damage and push the target as described in the Shield Bash mechanics. Your damage is 1d4 + STR modifier.

  2. Level 2: Combat Discipline, Protective Reflexes

    Choose one discipline. You may retrain this choice when you gain a class Feat/ASI

    • Shield Discipline: while wielding a shield, the first time each round an attack redirected through Guardian’s Intercession hits you, reduce its damage by PB before Resolute Guard.
    • Weapon Discipline: once on each of your turns when you hit with a one-handed melee weapon, add PB to that attack’s damage.
    • Fortress Discipline: while wearing Plate, forced movement against you is reduced by 5 ft, minimum 0, and you have Advantage on checks and saves to resist Prone.
    • Vanguard Discipline: once each turn after moving at least 10 ft toward a hostile and ending within melee reach, you may move through hostile spaces during that movement; you cannot end occupied and OAs against you during it have Disadvantage. This is the only time that hostile spaces are not considered as difficult terrain.

    Protective Reflexes

    After successful Guardian's Intercession, the protected ally may move up to 5 ft without provoking from the creature whose attack you intercepted, if it can legally move.

  3. Level 3: Specialization Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 3 feature.

    BULWARK — Unyielding Intercession

    Guardian's Intercession range becomes 10 ft when the ally is more than 5 ft away, you may move up to 5 ft toward it as part of the reaction without provoking Opportunity Attacks against you, but you must end within 5 ft to your ally.

    JUSTICAR — Retributive Challenge

    when a Challenged creature triggers Defy the Templar, after its attack resolves you may use your Reaction to make one melee weapon attack against it if it is in reach.

    SENTINEL — Hold the Ground

    while you are not incapacitated, spaces within 5 ft of you are difficult terrain for hostile creatures; creatures you permit are unaffected.

  4. Level 4: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  5. Level 5: Extra Attack, Improved Resolve and Relentless Challenge

    Extra Attack

    You can attack twice when taking the Attack action.

    Improved Resolve

    Maximum Resolve increases 4. Your Guard die becomes d8.

    Relentless Challenge

    Can be maintained without requiring a Bonus Action so long as the target you can see is within 30 ft of you. The target must still be able to perceive you and that you are a valid target for at least one attack it could make.

  6. Level 6: Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 6 feature.

    BULWARK — Shared Resolve

    Once per round, when an attack redirected to you by Guardian’s Intercession hits you, spend 1 Resolve to use Resolute Guard against that attack even if you already used Resolute Guard during that turn. The same damage instance can benefit from Resolute Guard only once.

    JUSTICAR — Punishing Rebuke

    When you hit with the melee weapon attack granted by Retributive Challenge, the creature’s Speed becomes 0 until the end of the current turn.

    SENTINEL — Unyielding Ground

    When a hostile creature within 5 feet of you willingly moves to a space farther away from you, reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the end of its turn. This can occur once per creature per turn and never triggers from forced movement.

  7. Level 7: Guardian's Shelter, Improved Shield Bash

    Guardian's Shelter

    When another creature you can see within 5 ft makes a save, use your Reaction before the roll to add your CON modifier, minimum +1. If this changes failure to success, Protect the Vulnerable triggers.

    Improved Shield Bash

    Spend the required Resolve to deal damage and push the target as described in the Shield Bash mechanics. Your damage is 1d8 + STR modifier.

  8. Level 8: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  9. Level 9: Greater Resolve, Steadfast Resolve

    Greater Resolve and Steadfast Resolve

    Maximum Resolve becomes 5 and Guard Die becomes d10. When you roll Initiative for a genuine combat encounter, begin with 1 Resolve instead of 0.

  10. Level 10: Specialization Decision / Advancement, Empyrean Aegis, Commanding Challenge

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 10 feature.

    BULWARK — No One Falls

    Once per round, when an attack hits a creature within your Guardian’s Intercession range and the attack’s damage would reduce that creature to 0 HP, you may use Guardian’s Intercession immediately before the damage is applied even if you already used your Reaction this round. Resolve the redirection normally. Afterward, you cannot use Guardian’s Intercession again until the start of your next turn.

    JUSTICAR — Judgment of Defiance

    When your Retributive Challenge attack hits, it deals one additional current Guard Die of damage. Punishing Rebuke continues to apply.

    SENTINEL — Impassable Line

    A hostile creature provokes an Opportunity Attack from you when it moves 5 feet or more while within your reach, even if that movement does not leave your reach. A creature can provoke this special Opportunity Attack only once per turn. Disengage prevents it.

    Empyrean Aegis

    Your Templar makes an ultimate move. The target suffers a fixed amount of 80 Radiant damage. Once resolved, choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus within 20 ft. Each gains Resistance to all damage and cannot be moved against its will until the start of your next turn.

    Commanding Challenge

    Challenge up to two visible hostiles within 30 ft and maintain up to two simultaneously. Relentless Challenge can maintain both when each qualifies.

  11. Level 11: Unbreakable Resolve

    Unbreakable Resolve

    Whenever Resolute Guard reduces damage, choose one:

    • Stand Firm grants Advantage until your next turn on checks and saves to resist Grappled, Prone, or forced movement;
    • Reposition lets you move 5 ft without provoking;
    • Press the Threat makes your next melee hit against the source before the end of your next turn deal one additional Guard Die, usable once on each of your turns.

  12. Level 12: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  13. Level 13: Guardian's Rally, Resolute Shelter

    Guardian's Rally

    As a Bonus Action, spend 1 Resolve and choose yourself or an ally within 10 ft. Roll your current Guard Die; the creature gains temporary HP equal to the result + CON modifier until the start of your next turn. Guardian’s Rally does not itself generate Resolve or trigger Protect the Vulnerable when those temporary HP absorb damage.

    Resolute Shelter

    Guardian’s Shelter range becomes 10 ft. If the protected creature succeeds and a successful save would normally deal half damage, spend 1 Resolve to make that damage 0. Resolve generated by Shelter changing failure to success may immediately pay this cost.

  14. Level 14: Final Specialization Decision / Advancement

    Choose one specialization path to view its Level 14 feature.

    BULWARK — Last Wall

    Once per round, when an attack redirected to you through Guardian’s Intercession hits you, spend 1 Resolve to gain resistance to all damage dealt by that attack. Apply resistance before Resolute Guard. Last Wall applies only to attacks intercepted for another creature, not attacks that originally targeted you.

    JUSTICAR — Inescapable Judgment

    When a Challenged creature triggers Defy the Templar, your Retributive Challenge attack against it has Advantage. If the attack hits, it also cannot take Reactions until the start of its next turn.

    SENTINEL — No Passage

    When you hit a hostile creature with an Opportunity Attack, its Speed becomes 0 for the remainder of the current turn. Teleportation and forced movement do not trigger Opportunity Attacks from this feature.

  15. Level 15: Unshaken Resolve

    Unshaken Resolve

    Maximum Resolve becomes 6 and Guard Die becomes d12; neither increases further through normal progression. On Initiative, begin with 2 Resolve instead of 1. If you roll Initiative at 0 HP but are not dead, gain no starting Resolve until you regain at least 1 HP.

    Empyrean Aegis

    Your Empyrean Aegis now deals normal weapon damage plus fixed 100 Radiant damage on the primary target. Also choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency bonus within 30 ft. Each gains Resistance to all damage and cannot be moved against its will until the start of your next turn.

  16. Level 16: Feat / Ability Score Improvement

    Gain the universal Feat / Ability Score Improvement for this level. A class retraining opportunity may also be used to change your Combat Discipline.

  17. Level 17: Unyielding Presence, Return Damage, Unceasing Challenge

    Unyielding Presence

    When Resolute Guard is used against attack damage from a creature Challenged by you, increase the damage reduction equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus. The new calculation is now 1 Guard Die + your CON modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.

    Return Damage

    When a creature Challenged by you hits you with a melee attack, your armor will cause some return damage towards the attacking creature equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + your CON Modifier. This does not affect Ranged or Spell or AOE attacks. Resolve generation applies normally.

    Unceasing Challenge

    At the start of each turn, Challenge is now a Free Action. Additionally, you may now maintain this Challenge to up to 3 creatures so long as they still meet the requirements for the Challenge: targets must still be within 30 ft from you, be able to perceive you, and that you are a valid target for at least one attack they could make.

  18. Level 18: Guardian Without Equal, Seismic Slam

    Guardian Without Equal

    You have two Reactions each round. The additional Reaction can be used only for a Templar class feature, not an ordinary Opportunity Attack, feat, item, or non-Templar feature. Both cannot answer the same trigger and both refresh at the start of your turn.

    Seismic Slam

    Spend the required Resolve to deal damage and push the target as described in the Shield Bash mechanics. Your damage is 1d12 + STR modifier.

  19. Level 19: Empyrean Boon

    Gain the universal Empyrean Boon available at Level 19.

  20. Level 20: Perfect Templar

    Battle-Ready Resolve

    When you roll Initiative, begin combat with 3 Resolve instead of 2, up to your maximum.

    Refuse to Fall

    Once per Long Rest, when damage would reduce you to 0 HP, spend 3 Resolve with no action required to drop to 1 HP instead. This does not grant you any Resolve.

    Resolve Through Sacrifice

    When Guardian’s Intercession or Guardian’s Shelter successfully protects another creature and Protect the Vulnerable would grant Resolve, gain 2 instead of 1. Those 2 points count as one generation event and may exceed the normal one-Resolve-per-creature-turn limit. Hold the Line and Defy the Templar remain unchanged.

    Empyrean Aegis

    Your Empyrean Aegis now deals normal weapon damage plus a flat 120 Radiant damage on the primary target. Also choose yourself and a number of allies equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 ft. Each gains Resistance to all damage and cannot be moved against its will until the start of your next turn. Additionally, all allies within the encounter also gain temporary HP equivalent to your Templar level.

    Your Flight Slots increases to 8

How to Play Templar

1. Your Role and Gameplay Loop

Your Role as a Templar

The Templar is a frontline defender who protects allies by drawing enemy attention, intercepting attacks, strengthening saving throws, and controlling the space around the party.

Your job is not simply to absorb damage. You create difficult choices for hostile creatures: attack you and help fuel your Resolve, or attack your allies and risk your Challenge and protective reactions.

Your primary objectives are to:

  • Position yourself where you can protect vulnerable allies.
  • Challenge important hostile creatures and remain a valid target for their attacks.
  • Generate Resolve through Hold the Line, Protect the Vulnerable, and Defy the Templar.
  • Spend Resolve at the right moment to reduce, redirect, or prevent harm.
  • Use your control abilities to disrupt enemy movement and protect important areas.

A Templar is most effective when close enough to intervene, while remaining aware of which allies and enemies are within the ranges of your features.

Your Core Gameplay Loop

  1. Position: Move where you can threaten enemies and remain close to allies who may need protection.
  2. Challenge: Use your Bonus Action to Challenge a priority hostile creature.
  3. Invite Pressure: Remain a valid target so Challenge can discourage attacks against your allies and create opportunities to gain Resolve.
  4. Protect: Use Resolute Guard, Guardian's Intercession, or Guardian's Shelter when their triggers occur.
  5. Respond: Spend Resolve on Shield Bash, Guardian's Rally, or another available technique when it provides a meaningful benefit.
  6. Reposition: Adjust your position to continue protecting allies, controlling enemy movement, and maintaining your Challenge.

You do not need to spend Resolve every time you gain it. Keep enough available for the defensive reaction or technique that may matter most on the next hostile turn.

2. Resolve and Challenge Management

Understanding Resolve

Resolve is your class resource. You gain it from three main triggers during genuine combat:

  • Hold the Line: Gain 1 Resolve when a hostile creature you can see hits you with a weapon or spell attack. Area-of-effect attacks qualify.
  • Protect the Vulnerable: Gain 1 Resolve when a Templar feature successfully reduces, prevents, redirects, or intercepts damage that would otherwise be dealt to another creature, or when a feature explicitly qualifies.
  • Defy the Templar: Gain 1 Resolve when a creature Challenged by you attacks another creature while you were a valid target for that attack. Ranged and spell attacks qualify.

You normally gain no more than 1 Resolve during any hostile creature's turn. One event grants Resolve only once, even if it meets more than one trigger. Self-inflicted damage and damage from allies do not generate Resolve.

Resolve resets to 0 when Initiative is rolled and when combat ends, unless a later feature changes your starting amount. You cannot gain Resolve while Incapacitated.

Managing Your Resolve

Your maximum Resolve increases as you advance: 3 at Levels 1–4, 4 at Levels 5–8, 5 at Levels 9–14, and 6 from Level 15 onward.

Practical tips:

  • Track both your current Resolve and your maximum.
  • Remember that Resolve can be gained outside your own turn when its triggers occur.
  • Consider whether you may need Resolve for Resolute Guard or Guardian's Intercession before spending it on another technique.
  • Taking damage does not always generate Resolve; Hold the Line requires a hit from a hostile creature you can see.
  • Resolve gained from a qualifying protection feature may sometimes be spent immediately when the timing rules allow it.

Using Challenge

Challenge is one of your main tools for protecting the party. As a Bonus Action, designate a hostile creature you can see within 30 feet. While it can perceive you, remains within 30 feet, and can target you with at least one of its attacks, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you.

Challenge is nonmagical, requires no saving throw, and is not a Charm or Fear effect. Initially, you can maintain one Challenge at a time. It lasts until the start of your next turn; the class rules also state that it expires after 10 rounds or when you issue a new Challenge beyond your active limit.

Practical tips:

  • Challenge an enemy whose attacks would be especially dangerous to your allies.
  • Remain within 30 feet and perceptible to the Challenged creature.
  • Stay a valid target for at least one of its attacks; otherwise, the Challenge penalty is suppressed.
  • Challenge does not force an enemy to attack you. It makes attacking others more difficult while its conditions are met.
  • At Level 5, Relentless Challenge lets you maintain a qualifying Challenge without spending a Bonus Action. At Level 10, Commanding Challenge allows two targets; at Level 17, Unceasing Challenge allows up to three and makes Challenge a Free Action at the start of each of your turns.

3. Protecting Allies and Using Defensive Techniques

Resolute Guard

Resolute Guard costs 1 Resolve and requires no action. When you take damage from an attack, reduce that damage by 1 Guard Die + your CON modifier, minimum 0. You normally use it only once per turn unless a Templar feature says otherwise.

When to use it: Use Resolute Guard when reducing the damage will meaningfully preserve your HP, especially when an attack would leave you badly injured or threaten to take you out of the fight.

Guardian's Intercession

Guardian's Intercession costs 2 Resolve and uses your Reaction. Before an attack roll is made against an ally within 5 feet, you can intercept it and become the new target. The attack is resolved against your AC. On a miss, it has no effect; on a hit, you take half the damage, rounded up.

Because you prevented the ally from taking the attack's damage, you regain 1 Resolve through Protect the Vulnerable whether the redirected attack hits or misses. You do not gain another Resolve from taking the redirected damage yourself.

When to use it: Intercede when an ally within range is threatened by an attack that you can afford to take on their behalf. You must have your Reaction and the required Resolve available when the attack is about to be made.

Guardian's Shelter and Resolute Shelter

At Level 7, Guardian's Shelter lets you use your Reaction before a visible creature within 5 feet makes a saving throw, adding your CON modifier to that save, minimum +1. If your intervention changes a failed save into a success, Protect the Vulnerable triggers.

At Level 13, Resolute Shelter increases the range to 10 feet. If the protected creature succeeds on a save that would normally deal half damage, you may spend 1 Resolve to reduce that damage to 0. Resolve gained because Guardian's Shelter changed the save from failure to success may immediately pay this cost.

When to use it: Watch for allies about to make important saving throws, especially when success would prevent a dangerous effect or when Resolute Shelter can eliminate the remaining damage.

Guardian's Rally

At Level 13, Guardian's Rally lets you spend 1 Resolve as a Bonus Action to grant yourself or an ally within 10 feet temporary HP equal to your current Guard Die roll + your CON modifier. These temporary HP last until the start of your next turn.

Guardian's Rally does not itself generate Resolve or trigger Protect the Vulnerable when its temporary HP absorb damage.

When to use it: Use the Rally when a short-lived buffer can help a creature withstand incoming pressure before your next turn.

4. Positioning, Control, and Offensive Techniques

Protective Positioning

Your protective features have specific ranges. Guardian's Intercession begins at 5 feet, Guardian's Shelter begins at 5 feet, and Guardian's Rally can reach an ally within 10 feet. Resolute Shelter later extends Guardian's Shelter to 10 feet.

  • Before moving, consider which ally is most likely to need your Reaction.
  • Stay close enough to allies for Intercession and Shelter when those features are important.
  • Keep Challenged creatures within 30 feet and remain a valid target for their attacks.
  • Do not spend your Reaction casually if an ally may need protection before your next turn.

Shield Bash and Improved Shield Bash

Shield Bash costs 1 Resolve and uses your Bonus Action. A target within your melee reach makes a Strength saving throw against your Class Save DC. On a failure, it is pushed 5 feet; on a success, it is not pushed. A target at least one size larger than you has Advantage on the save. At Level 1, Shield Bash deals 1d4 + your STR modifier.

At Level 7, Improved Shield Bash costs 2 Resolve and deals 1d8 + your STR modifier. On a failed save, the target is pushed 10 feet; on a successful save, it is pushed 5 feet. The successful-save push does not trigger your allies' Opportunity Attacks.

When to use it: Use these techniques when forced movement can disrupt an enemy's position or create an opening for your allies. Consider the target's size and where the push will place it.

Seismic Slam

At Level 18, Seismic Slam costs 4 Resolve and uses your Bonus Action. A target within your melee reach makes a Strength save against your Class Save DC. On a failure, it is pushed 10 feet, provoking Opportunity Attacks from your allies except you; you then move into the horizontal space it vacated without provoking an Opportunity Attack. Adjacent hostile creatures also make Dexterity saves, taking damage equal to half the damage dealt by the ability, rounded down, on a failure.

On a successful Strength save, the primary target is pushed 5 feet and does not trigger Opportunity Attacks. A target at least two sizes larger has Advantage on its save; secondary targets have neither Advantage nor Disadvantage based on size. Seismic Slam deals 1d12 + STR modifier damage.

When to use it: Use Seismic Slam when you can disrupt a central enemy and affect nearby hostiles at the same time. Check the positions of your allies before choosing the direction of the push.

5. Higher-Level Features and Specializations

Combat Discipline — Level 2

Choose one discipline and retrain it when you gain a class Feat/ASI:

  • Shield Discipline: While wielding a shield, reduce the first redirected attack that hits you each round by your Proficiency Bonus before Resolute Guard.
  • Weapon Discipline: Once on each of your turns, add your Proficiency Bonus to the damage of a hit with a one-handed melee weapon.
  • Fortress Discipline: While wearing Plate, reduce forced movement against you by 5 feet, minimum 0, and gain Advantage on checks and saves to resist Prone.
  • Vanguard Discipline: After moving at least 10 feet toward a hostile creature and ending within melee reach, you may move through hostile spaces during that movement; you cannot end in an occupied space, and Opportunity Attacks against you during it have Disadvantage.

Level 5 — Improved Resolve and Relentless Challenge

Your maximum Resolve increases to 4 and your Guard Die becomes d8. Relentless Challenge lets you maintain a qualifying Challenge without spending a Bonus Action.

Level 9 — Greater Resolve and Steadfast Resolve

Your maximum Resolve increases to 5 and your Guard Die becomes d10. When you roll Initiative for a genuine combat encounter, you begin with 1 Resolve instead of 0.

Level 11 — Unbreakable Resolve

Whenever Resolute Guard reduces damage, choose one additional benefit:

  • Stand Firm: Gain Advantage until your next turn on checks and saves to resist Grappled, Prone, or forced movement.
  • Reposition: Move 5 feet without provoking.
  • Press the Threat: Your next melee hit against the source before the end of your next turn deals one additional Guard Die. This can be used once on each of your turns.

Level 15 — Unshaken Resolve

Your maximum Resolve becomes 6 and your Guard Die becomes d12. When you roll Initiative, begin with 2 Resolve instead of 1. If you roll Initiative at 0 HP but are not dead, you gain no starting Resolve until you regain at least 1 HP.

Level 17 — Unyielding Presence, Return Damage, and Unceasing Challenge

Unyielding Presence increases Resolute Guard's damage reduction by your Proficiency Bonus when used against attack damage from a creature Challenged by you. Return Damage harms a Challenged creature that hits you with a melee attack, dealing damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus + CON modifier. Unceasing Challenge lets you maintain up to three qualifying Challenges and makes Challenge a Free Action at the start of each of your turns.

Level 18 — Guardian Without Equal

You gain two Reactions each round. The additional Reaction can be used only for a Templar class feature, not an ordinary Opportunity Attack, feat, item, or non-Templar feature. Both Reactions refresh at the start of your turn, and they cannot answer the same trigger.

Choosing Your Specialization

Your Specialization determines how you protect the party and pressure hostile creatures:

  • Bulwark — Ally protection: Strengthens Guardian's Intercession and improves your ability to take attacks meant for allies. Choose this path when you want to focus on direct protection and defensive resource use.
  • Justicar — Retaliation and punishment: Turns enemies that ignore your Challenge into opportunities for retaliatory attacks, movement restriction, and additional punishment.
  • Sentinel — Area control: Makes the space around you difficult or dangerous for hostile creatures to cross, helping you defend chokepoints and restrict enemy movement.

You choose your initial Specialization at Level 3. You may continue or reassign it at Levels 6, 10, and 14. If you change, replace the entire previous Specialization package with the new path's complete package through that checkpoint; you cannot mix features from different paths. Reassignment does not restore HP or class resources. The Level 14 decision is your final normal choice through Level 20.

6. Combat Examples and Common Mistakes

Example: Challenging an Enemy Attacking Your Party

  1. Identify a hostile creature whose attacks pose a serious threat to your allies.
  2. Use your Bonus Action to Challenge it, provided it is visible and within 30 feet.
  3. Position yourself so you remain within 30 feet, perceptible, and a valid target for at least one of its attacks.
  4. If it attacks you and hits, Hold the Line can grant 1 Resolve.
  5. If it attacks an ally while you remain a valid target, Defy the Templar can grant 1 Resolve.

Lesson: Challenge works best when you can maintain its conditions. It pressures the enemy but does not force it to attack you.

Example: Intercepting an Attack Against an Ally

  1. An ally within 5 feet is about to be attacked.
  2. If you have your Reaction and 2 Resolve, use Guardian's Intercession before the attack roll.
  3. Become the new target and resolve the attack against your AC.
  4. If the attack hits, you take half damage, rounded up; if it misses, it has no effect.
  5. Gain 1 Resolve through Protect the Vulnerable, regardless of whether the attack hits or misses.

Lesson: Intercession can protect an ally and return some Resolve, but you must be prepared to take the redirected attack if it hits.

Example: Helping an Ally Make a Saving Throw

  1. An ally you can see within Guardian's Shelter range is about to make a saving throw.
  2. Use your Reaction before the roll to add your CON modifier, minimum +1.
  3. If the bonus changes the result from failure to success, Protect the Vulnerable triggers.
  4. At Level 13, if the successful save would normally deal half damage, you may spend 1 Resolve through Resolute Shelter to reduce that damage to 0.

Lesson: Shelter is most valuable when the saving throw matters and your intervention can change the outcome.

Example: Holding a Chokepoint as a Sentinel

  1. Position yourself where hostile creatures must pass near you to reach your allies.
  2. Use Hold the Ground to make spaces within 5 feet of you difficult terrain for hostile creatures while you are not Incapacitated.
  3. Use Unyielding Ground, Impassable Line, and No Passage as you gain them to punish or stop enemy movement.
  4. Keep your Reaction available when an enemy's movement may trigger one of your features.

Lesson: Sentinel is strongest when the location itself matters and enemies must move through the area you control.

Common Templar Mistakes

  1. Challenging an enemy and then leaving its range. Challenge's penalty depends on range, perception, and your being a valid target.
  2. Forgetting that Challenge does not force a target to attack you. It imposes Disadvantage on attacks against others only while its conditions are met.
  3. Spending all your Resolve immediately. You may need it for Resolute Guard, Guardian's Intercession, or another defensive response.
  4. Using your Reaction too early. Intercession and Shelter compete for your available Reaction unless a feature grants another one.
  5. Standing too far from allies. Your protective features have specific ranges; positioning determines whether you can use them.
  6. Assuming every hit generates Resolve. Hold the Line requires a hit from a hostile creature you can see; self-damage and ally damage do not qualify.
  7. Expecting Guardian's Rally to generate Resolve. Its temporary HP absorbing damage does not trigger Protect the Vulnerable.
  8. Forgetting your Specialization's role. Bulwark protects allies directly, Justicar punishes Challenged enemies, and Sentinel controls space.
