“A second of elemental fury. An eternity of silence.”

The Aetheric spellcaster wielding destructive and controlling magic.

The Sorcerer is the raw magical-damage benchmark. It casts through Fire, Ice, Lightning, and Wind spell chains, then spends Aether Focus only when a particular spell needs more damage, area, control pressure, or reliability. Aether Focus enhances spells; it is never the fuel required to cast ordinary Sorcerer spells.

Class Identity

Sorcerers are masters of direct Aetheric spellcraft, combining destructive magic with the ability to control the shape and tempo of a fight. They can punish clustered enemies, deny movement or action, and unleash powerful spells when an opening appears. Aether Focus represents the concentration and arcane discipline required to turn immense magical potential into reliable battlefield power.

Tradition & Outlook

The Sorcerer tradition grows from Atreia’s mage disciplines and is associated with formal magical study, experimentation, academies, battlefield arcanists, and Daevas fascinated by the structure of Aether itself. Sorcerers commonly value knowledge, precision, preparation, and intellectual mastery, although their personalities range from scholarly restraint to spectacular confidence. For many, magic is both a weapon and a language through which Atreia can be understood.

Appearance & Adventuring

Sorcerers favor cloth armor and magical implements such as Orbs and Spellbooks under the Wings of Atreia equipment rules. Their appearance may reflect academic, military, ceremonial, or highly individual magical traditions. They adventure to recover lost knowledge, study Aetheric phenomena, test magical theories, defend their people with overwhelming spellcraft, or confront powers that ordinary weapons cannot adequately answer.

Core Mechanics & Resources

Aether Focus

Maximum: Your Maximum Aether Focus is equivalent to your Proficiency Bonus + your INT modifier, minimum of 2. A Long Rest restores all expended Aether Focus. A Short Rest restores Aether Focus equal to PB, up to your maximum. The resource is an enhancement reserve: casting an ordinary Sorcerer spell never requires Aether Focus unless a spell or class feature explicitly says otherwise. Aether Sage specialization and the Level 20 capstone explicitly increase this maximum.

Starting State: Aether Focus is a rest-based enhancement reserve and does not reset merely because combat begins or ends. After a Long Rest it is normally full.

Recovery: Long Rest restores all expended Aether Focus. Short Rest restores Focus equal to PB, up to maximum; Aether Sage specialization can improve this recovery. Unused Aether Focus persists between encounters until spent or restored.

Base Generation: The base Sorcerer does not generate Aether Focus from ordinary spell hits or damage. Combat refunds exist only where a class or specialization feature explicitly grants them.

Spellcasting Acquisition and Spellcasting

Spells are class-granted abilities. They cannot be bought, sold, looted, copied from spellbooks or scrolls, learned from vendors, or acquired by commerce or exploration. A spell becomes available only when the character reaches its Unlock Level and, when applicable, satisfies the immediately preceding spell in its linear prerequisite chain. Unlocking a later spell never removes the earlier spell. Newly unlocked spells begin at Spell Level 1 unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Spell Advancement raises one eligible unlocked spell by 1 Spell Level, subject to your class’s Maximum Spell Level and listed prerequisites. Unspent Spell Advancements may be banked indefinitely, but banked or newly earned Spell Advancements may be spent only when you gain a level; they cannot normally be spent during an adventure, between encounters, or during a rest. You cannot spend more Spell Advancements than what you have earned.

Spell Retraining. There is no universal spell retraining. Once a Spell Advancement is spent, it is permanent unless a specific class feature, explicit story effect, or GM-approved exceptional circumstance directly permits reassignment.

Spell Shaping

Intensify= Costs 1 Aether Focus. When an eligible Sorcerer spell deals damage, increase one damage resolution by +1d6.

Widen= Costs 1 Aether Focus. Increase an eligible radius or cone by 5 ft; a line by 10 ft; or a cube by 5 ft on each side. The spell must have an area that can legally be widened.

Potent Control= Costs 1 Aether Focus. Choose one creature affected by an eligible spell whose non-damaging control or movement rider requires a save. That creature has Disadvantage on its first save against that rider for this casting.

Spell Shaping – Your Spell Shaping enhances only a single spell. You choose and pay the shaping cost which is 1 of your Aether Focus when the spell is cast, before its affected rolls are fully resolved unless the feature says otherwise.

Spell Shaping is not required by every spells. Spending on Spell Shaping depends entirely on you.

Ultimate Ability

Aetheric CataclysmUltimate

Ultimate Activation: Costs 100 Daeva Points (DP).

As your Action and Bonus Action, make one damaging spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 ft. After this Ultimate resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your turn.

Primary Damage: The attack deals its normal spell damage calculation plus fixed Force damage. This Force damage is independent from the type of damage the triggering spell has.

Cataclysm. Every other hostile creature within a certain distance from the primary target makes a DEX save against your Spell Save DC. Every affected creature takes fixed Force damage on a failure, or half that amount on a success. You may excluse allies from the sphere.

Rules, Recovery and Restrictions:

Ultimate Attack Ability. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. The Ultimate Ability is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output. Damage printed on the corresponding level as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those die. Normal weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Specializations

Three distinct paths, with advancement features at the established specialization levels.

ELEMENTALIST

The Elementalist devotes its spellcraft to one chosen element: Fire, Ice, or Lightning. It progressively strengthens the destructive or controlling properties of that element, making it ideal for players who want a defined magical identity and specialized elemental attacks.

SPELLBINDER

The Spellbinder manipulates the battlefield through magical seals, persistent spell areas, and restrictions on enemy actions. It specializes in controlling enemy positioning and movement while improving its ability to maintain powerful ongoing spells.

AETHER SAGE

The Aether Sage focuses on mastering Aether Focus and expanding the flexibility of the Sorcerer's spell-shaping techniques. It gains greater adaptability, improved resource efficiency, and access to increasingly versatile casting options.
