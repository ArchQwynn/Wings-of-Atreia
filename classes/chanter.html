The Chanter wins by alternating weapon attacks, spells, and active Mantra management. Cadence is earned by changing categories rather than repeating one action, then spent on damage, protection, healing, and tactical movement. It is a secondary healer and hybrid support, not a replacement for the Cleric or a dedicated martial striker.

Class Identity

Chanters are martial support specialists who combine staff technique, enchantment, restorative magic, and Mantras into a continuous rhythm of battle. Rather than solving every problem through one large spell, a Chanter improves the party’s performance over time and responds to changing pressure with Cadence. Their presence is felt in how effectively the entire group moves, strikes, and recovers together.

Tradition & Outlook

The Chanter tradition grows from Atreia’s priestly disciplines but emphasizes disciplined body, voice, repetition, and the shaping of Aether through words and practiced forms. Chanters are often teachers, battle companions, spiritual guides, martial ascetics, or morale-keepers. They tend to value harmony between individual excellence and collective strength: the ideal Chanter does not merely stand beside allies but helps those allies become more capable.

Appearance & Adventuring

Chanters are strongly associated with Staff and Quarterstaff techniques in Wings of Atreia. Their clothing and armor tend to allow movement while retaining the ceremonial or disciplined character of a priestly warrior. They adventure to guide companions, strengthen military or exploratory groups, carry teachings into dangerous places, preserve morale during long campaigns, or prove that disciplined cooperation can overcome enemies stronger than any one Daeva.

Core Engine — Cadence

Cadence is a genuine-combat resource. It begins at 0 when combat starts, resets to 0 when combat ends, and artificial initiative cannot create or restore it. Maximum Cadence is 3 at Levels 1–4, 4 at 5–8, 5 at 9–12, 6 at 13–16, and 7 at 17–20.

CategoryQualifying Event
MartialYou hit a hostile creature with a weapon attack.
SpellA Chanter spell meaningfully affects a creature by hit, damage, failed save, actual healing, meaningful prevention, or another significant effect.
ChantYou activate, replace, or deliberately invoke a Mantra/Chant and it meaningfully changes at least one creature. Passive maintenance never qualifies.

Your previous qualifying category is always the most recent successful qualifying category, whether or not that event generated Cadence. When you successfully complete a qualifying event whose category differs from your previous successful qualifying category, gain 1 Cadence, subject to the normal generation limit. Your first successful qualifying event in a combat establishes your previous category; it does not generate Cadence by itself. Normal Cadence generation is limited to 1 per turn unless a feature explicitly grants more. Secondary Chanter riders do not recursively generate Cadence, Resonant Network triggers, Mantra Weaving, or other support sequences unless explicit.

Mantras

At Level 1 you maintain one active Mantra. Activating or changing it uses a Bonus Action. A Mantra is not a spell, requires no Concentration, affects you and eligible allies within its radius, and ends when dismissed, replaced, or you become Unconscious unless a later feature states otherwise.

MantraLevel 1 Effect
ValorYou and allies within 10 ft gain +1 weapon damage per attack.
ResolveYou and allies within 10 ft gain +1 to saves against Frightened and forced movement.
VitalityFirst actual HP restoration each round to you or an ally within 10 ft gains PB additional healing; no trigger if no HP is restored.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1

Gain spellcasting, Cadence max 3, one active Mantra, Cadence Techniques, Rhythmic Combat, and Martial Casting. Rhythmic Combat once/turn after a weapon hit or hostile-affecting Chanter spell moves you 5 ft without provoking from that creature. Martial Casting lets a Chanter weapon serve as your spellcasting focus. Max Spell Level 1.

Level 2

Gain +1 Spell Advancement. Mantra Weaving triggers whenever you activate/change a Mantra: choose 5-ft safe move, PB+CHA temp HP to an affected ally/self, or -1 to a hostile’s next attack. Rhythmic Recovery once/round refunds 1 Cadence after spending Cadence if your next qualifying action before end next turn is a different category.

Level 3

Gain +1 Spell Advancement, Max Spell Level 2, and choose War Chanter, Harmonic Sage, or Dissonant Voice.

War Chanter — Battle Rhythm / Martial Flow

Battle Rhythm — Once on each of your turns, when you gain Cadence from a Martial qualifying action after your previous qualifying category was Spell or Chant, choose one: Driving Blow adds 1d6 damage to the triggering weapon attack; Rallying Blow gives one ally affected by an active Mantra +1 to its next attack roll before your next turn; Guarded Blow gives you +1 AC until your next turn. Martial Flow — When you use Resonant Strike, the ally who would gain the Speed bonus may instead gain temporary HP equal to PB.

Harmonic Sage — Extended Mantra / Efficient Cadence

Extended Mantra — Your Mantra Radius becomes 15 feet. When you activate or change a Mantra, choose two affected creatures to receive a creature-targeted Mantra Weaving benefit instead of one. Hostile-target weaving effects still target only one hostile creature. Efficient Cadence — Once on each of your turns, after you spend Cadence on Guarding Rhythm, Restorative Verse, or another Chanter support technique, roll a d6. On a 6, regain 1 Cadence. This occurs at most once per turn.

Dissonant Voice — Dissonant Opening / Broken Rhythm

Dissonant Opening — Once on each of your turns, when a hostile creature fails a saving throw against a Chanter spell, choose until the start of your next turn: Falter (-2 to its next attack roll), Hesitate (cannot take Reactions), or Drag (Speed -10 feet). Only one Dissonant Opening from you can affect a creature at a time. Broken Rhythm — When you gain Cadence by successfully affecting a hostile creature with a Spell after your previous category was Martial or Chant, once on your turn you may move it 5 feet horizontally if it is no more than one size larger and fails a Strength save against your Chanter Spell Save DC.

Level 4

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and a Feat / ASI.

Level 5

Gain +1 Spell Advancement. Extra Attack gives two weapon attacks. Max Cadence becomes 4; completing two different qualifying categories on your turn gains 1 additional Cadence once/turn. Techniques become d8 versions. Rhythmic Strike once/turn after an Action Chanter spell makes one weapon attack as a Bonus Action; it cannot recurse.

Level 6

Gain +1 Spell Advancement, Max Spell Level 3, and your Level 6 specialization features.

War Chanter — Relentless Rhythm / Battle Cadence

Relentless Rhythm — Once on each of your turns, when both attacks from your Attack action hit the same hostile creature, choose one: Crushing Rhythm makes the second attack deal +1d8 damage; Rallying Rhythm gives one ally in an active Mantra PB + INT temporary HP; Driving Rhythm pushes the target 5 feet if it is no more than one size larger. Battle Cadence — When you gain Cadence from Martial after Spell or Chant, move 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the creature you hit. You cannot combine this with Rhythmic Combat against the same creature on the same turn.

Harmonic Sage — Harmonized Mantra / Echoing Cadence

Harmonized Mantra — Whenever you activate or change a Mantra, choose one: Protective Harmony gives one affected ally +1 AC until your next turn; Restorative Harmony restores PB + CHA HP to one affected ally; Resolute Harmony gives one affected ally +2 to its next saving throw before your next turn. Restorative Harmony cannot generate Cadence or recursively trigger other healing riders. Echoing Cadence — Once per round, after you perform a qualifying Chant action, if that Mantra meaningfully benefits at least two creatures before the start of your next turn, regain 1 Cadence.

Dissonant Voice — Lingering Dissonance / Resonant Fracture

Lingering Dissonance — Dissonant Opening lasts until the end of your next turn instead of the start. Resonant Fracture — Once on each of your turns, when you successfully affect a hostile creature with a Chanter spell after Martial or Chant, impose -1 AC until the start of your next turn.

Level 7

Gain +1 Spell Advancement. Dual Mantra lets you maintain two different Mantras. Harmonic Transition once/turn when switching one slot chooses affected creature: 5-ft safe move, PB+CHA temp HP, or +1 next attack/save.

Level 8

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and a Feat / ASI.

Level 9

Gain +1 Spell Advancement, Max Spell Level 4, and Cadence max 5. Flowing Cadence improves sustained category sequencing. Greater Mantra increases base radius to 15 ft; Valor becomes +2 weapon damage, Resolve +2 to relevant saves, and Vitality’s first healing increases by PB + 1.

Level 10

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and your Level 10 specialization features.

Ascendant Cadence — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make either one Ultimate melee weapon attack against a creature within your reach or one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 60 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Cadence Damage. On a melee hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Radiant damage of 80 at Levels 10-14, 100 at Levels 15-19, or 120 at Level 20. On a spell hit, the target takes the same fixed Radiant damage.

Perfect Coordination. On a hit, choose yourself and up to a number of willing allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet. Each chosen creature may immediately move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and has Advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. At Level 15, each also gains temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus + your Chanter spellcasting ability modifier. At Level 20, the movement may be up to the creature's full Speed.

War Chanter — War Hymn / Relentless Assault

War Hymn — Once on each of your turns, when you hit a hostile creature with a weapon attack after your previous qualifying category was Spell or Chant, choose one ally affected by an active Mantra: Assault gives +1d6 on its next weapon hit; Advance gives +10 feet Speed; Guard gives +2 AC against the next attack. Each expires at the start of your next turn. Relentless Assault — When you use Rhythmic Strike, the Bonus Action weapon attack gains +PB damage if the triggering spell successfully affected a hostile creature.

Harmonic Sage — Triple Harmony / Harmonic Resonance

Triple Harmony — Once per Short or Long Rest, when you activate or change a Mantra, enter Triple Harmony for 1 minute. During it, you may maintain three different Mantras you know simultaneously. It ends early if you become Unconscious. Harmonic Resonance — At the start of each of your turns, if at least two allies are benefiting from at least two different Mantras you maintain, choose one of them to gain temporary HP equal to your CHA modifier, minimum 1, until the start of your next turn. This does not generate Cadence, count as healing, or recursively trigger support riders.

Dissonant Voice — Fractured Defense / Deep Dissonance

Fractured Defense — Once on each of your turns, when a creature affected by Dissonant Opening is hit by your weapon attack or Chanter spell attack, choose: Expose gives +2 to the next attack against it; Weaken gives -PB to its next damage roll; Stagger prevents Opportunity Attacks. Each lasts until your next turn as applicable. Deep Dissonance — When a hostile creature fails a saving throw against your Chanter spell by 5 or more, gain 1 Cadence, once per round.

Level 11

Gain +1 Spell Advancement. Harmonic Assault: once on each of your turns, when you take the Attack action and hit with at least one weapon attack, you may replace your second attack with one Harmonic Act: Harmonic Spell — cast one Chanter spell with a normal casting time of 1 Action if it is At-Will or has a 1-Round Cooldown and is currently available; Harmonic Verse — use one Cadence Technique that normally requires a Bonus Action, paying its normal Cadence cost; Harmonic Shift — activate or change one Mantra as part of the Attack action. A spell cast through Harmonic Assault cannot trigger Rhythmic Strike. Harmonic Assault never grants an additional attack; it replaces the second attack. Greater Cadence Techniques: Greater Resonant Strike deals 2d8 Force damage and the chosen ally gains +10 feet Speed until the start of your next turn or temporary HP equal to PB + INT; Greater Guarding Rhythm reduces damage by 2d8 + INT; Greater Restorative Verse restores 2d8 + INT HP and still costs 2 Cadence. If a feature reduces a Cadence Technique's cost to 0, using it still counts as using the technique but not as spending Cadence. Max Spell Level remains 4.

Level 12

Gain +1 Spell Advancement, Max Spell Level 5, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 13

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Cadence max 6. Resonant Network: a creature is in your Resonant Network while it is within the Mantra Radius of at least one active Mantra and eligible to benefit from that Mantra. Once on each of your turns, when you successfully affect a creature in your Resonant Network with a Chanter spell or Cadence Technique, choose one additional creature in your Network within 20 feet of the original target. It gains Echoed Guard (PB temporary HP), Echoed Step (5-foot safe movement from one creature), or Echoed Resolve (+1 to its next saving throw before the start of your next turn). A creature can benefit from your Resonant Network only once per turn. Greater Cadence Flow replaces Rhythmic Recovery. Once per round, when you spend Cadence and then successfully perform a qualifying action from a different category before the start of your next turn, choose one: regain 1 Cadence; gain +10 feet Speed until the end of your next turn; or gain temporary HP equal to INT + PB until the start of your next turn.

Level 14

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and your final specialization features.

War Chanter — Battle Chorus / Martial Crescendo

Battle Chorus — Once on each of your turns when your Attack action hits at least one hostile creature, choose up to two allies affected by your active Mantras. Each receives Assault Chorus (+1d8 on next weapon hit), Advance Chorus (+10 feet Speed and immediate 5-foot safe movement), or Guard Chorus (+2 AC against the next attack), until the start of your next turn. Martial Crescendo — When you spend Cadence on Resonant Strike and that attack follows a successful Spell or Chant qualifying action, increase Resonant Strike’s bonus damage by +1d8. With Greater Resonant Strike, this sequence produces 3d8 Force bonus damage.

Harmonic Sage — Grand Harmony / Harmonic Convergence

Grand Harmony — You may permanently maintain three different active Mantras simultaneously. Your Mantra Radius increases by 5 feet, becoming 25 feet. Harmonic Convergence — Once per round, after a successful qualifying Chant action, if at least three allies are affected by two or more of your Mantras, choose one of those allies: Protective Convergence gives +1 AC; Restorative Convergence heals PB + CHA; Resolute Convergence gives Advantage on its next saving throw. Effects last until your next turn as applicable; Restorative Convergence cannot generate Cadence or recursively trigger support riders.

Dissonant Voice — Crushing Dissonance / Fracture Rhythm

Crushing Dissonance — Once on each of your turns, when a hostile creature fails a save against your Chanter spell while already suffering Dissonant Opening, add one until the end of your next turn: Suppress Defense (-2 AC), Suppress Power (-2 attack rolls), or Suppress Movement (Speed halved). Fracture Rhythm — When a hostile creature affected by Dissonant Opening or Crushing Dissonance misses an attack or fails a saving throw, use your Reaction to gain 1 Cadence, once per round.

Level 15

Gain +1 Spell Advancement, Max Spell Level 6. Perfect Rhythm introduces Rhythm Steps 0–3 earned through sustained category variation; higher Steps strengthen movement, support, and Cadence efficiency but end with combat and decay when you stop participating.

Improved Ascendant Cadence

Your Ascendant Cadence now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and a Feat / ASI.

Level 17

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Cadence max 7. Apex Techniques become 3d8. Harmonic Mastery adds Harmonic Recovery (Network ally heals 1d8+INT and moves 5 ft safely) and Harmonic Dissonance (CON save after first weapon hit; fail Speed -10, no Reactions, -1 next save). Perfect Sequence once/turn after completing Martial, Spell, Chant across current/previous turn chooses +1 Cadence, PB+INT temp HP, or Advantage next attack/spell attack.

Level 18

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Max Spell Level 7. Grand Resonance spreads one Resonant Network trigger to up to two other Network creatures with different Echo benefits. Perfect Mantras reach final values; Mantra Stability Mastery keeps Mantras until end of next turn if you become Unconscious and restores them if you wake before then. Perfect Rhythm Mastery reduces decay conditions.

Level 19

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and the universal Empyrean Boon.

Level 20

Gain final +1 Spell Advancement. Eternal Cadence starts genuine combat with 3 Cadence. Eternal Flow at 3 Rhythm Steps turns excess Cadence into PB temp HP, 5-ft safe movement, or +1 next attack/spell attack for a Network creature. Masterful Harmony once/turn after two different categories permits one additional different Harmonic Act that cannot cast another Action-cost spell or make another weapon attack. Resonant Crescendo 1/LR Bonus Action for 1 minute expands Mantra radius +10, makes first Cadence Technique each round free, converts excess Cadence to Network temp HP, and after all three categories once/round grants a Network ally 2d8+INT healing, 15-ft safe move, or +2 AC. Perfect Mantra Switching once/turn activates/replaces a Mantra without using the Bonus Action.

Apex Ascendant Cadence

Your Ascendant Cadence uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Example Rhythm Sequence

  1. Turn A: hit with a weapon attack (Martial), then later cast a Chanter spell that meaningfully affects a creature (Spell). Because the category changed, the second qualifying event can generate Cadence under the normal once-per-turn rule or Improved Cadence at higher level.
  2. At Level 5, cast an Action Chanter spell, then use Rhythmic Strike for one Bonus Action weapon attack. This is not a second Attack action and does not grant a third standard attack.
  3. At Level 7+, change one Mantra slot to create a meaningful Chant event, apply Mantra Weaving/Harmonic Transition, and continue the Martial–Spell–Chant cycle.
  4. At high level, Network/Harmonic riders are secondary effects: they improve allies but do not recursively count as new qualifying Cadence actions unless a feature explicitly says they do.
