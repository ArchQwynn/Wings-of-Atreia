The Chanter wins by alternating weapon attacks, spells, and active Mantra management. Cadence is earned by changing categories rather than repeating one action, then spent on damage, protection, healing, and tactical movement. It is a secondary healer and hybrid support, not a replacement for the Cleric or a dedicated martial striker.

Class Identity

Chanters are martial support specialists who combine staff technique, enchantment, restorative magic, and Mantras into a continuous rhythm of battle. Rather than solving every problem through one large spell, a Chanter improves the party’s performance over time and responds to changing pressure with Cadence. Their presence is felt in how effectively the entire group moves, strikes, and recovers together.

Tradition & Outlook

The Chanter tradition grows from Atreia’s priestly disciplines but emphasizes disciplined body, voice, repetition, and the shaping of Aether through words and practiced forms. Chanters are often teachers, battle companions, spiritual guides, martial ascetics, or morale-keepers. They tend to value harmony between individual excellence and collective strength: the ideal Chanter does not merely stand beside allies but helps those allies become more capable.

Appearance & Adventuring

Chanters are strongly associated with Staff and Quarterstaff techniques in Wings of Atreia. Their clothing and armor tend to allow movement while retaining the ceremonial or disciplined character of a priestly warrior. They adventure to guide companions, strengthen military or exploratory groups, carry teachings into dangerous places, preserve morale during long campaigns, or prove that disciplined cooperation can overcome enemies stronger than any one Daeva.

Core Engine — Cadence

Cadence is a genuine-combat resource. It begins at 0 when combat starts, resets to 0 when combat ends, and artificial initiative cannot create or restore it. Maximum Cadence is 3 at Levels 1–4, 4 at 5–8, 5 at 9–12, 6 at 13–16, and 7 at 17–20.

CategoryQualifying Event
MartialYou hit a hostile creature with a weapon attack.
SpellA Chanter spell meaningfully affects a creature by hit, damage, failed save, actual healing, meaningful prevention, or another significant effect.
ChantYou activate, replace, or deliberately invoke a Mantra/Chant and it meaningfully changes at least one creature. Passive maintenance never qualifies.

Your previous qualifying category is always the most recent successful qualifying category, whether or not that event generated Cadence. When you successfully complete a qualifying event whose category differs from your previous successful qualifying category, gain 1 Cadence, subject to the normal generation limit. Your first successful qualifying event in a combat establishes your previous category; it does not generate Cadence by itself. Normal Cadence generation is limited to 1 per turn unless a feature explicitly grants more. Improved Cadence and Perfect Sequence are explicit once-per-turn exceptions. Echoing Cadence, Deep Dissonance, and Fracture Rhythm retain their independent once-per-round recovery triggers; the same saving throw failure cannot trigger both Deep Dissonance and Fracture Rhythm. Secondary Chanter riders do not recursively generate Cadence, Resonant Network triggers, Mantra Weaving, or other support sequences unless explicit.

Mantras

At Level 1 you maintain one active Mantra. Activating or changing it uses a Bonus Action. A Mantra is not a spell, requires no Concentration, affects you and eligible allies within its radius, and ends when dismissed, replaced, or you become Unconscious unless a later feature states otherwise.

Mantra Placement

Placement Range — 30 feet. When you activate or replace a Mantra, you may place it at a point you can see within 30 feet of yourself. The Mantra is centered on that chosen point and remains there until dismissed, replaced, or ended by another rule. It does not follow the Chanter after placement.

The Chanter does not need to remain within the Mantra's radius to maintain it. The Mantra Radius measures outward from the Mantra's placement point.

The 30-foot placement range and the Mantra Radius are separate values. Increasing a Mantra's Radius does not increase its placement range unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.

When multiple Mantras are maintained, each Mantra has its own placement point. Activating or replacing one Mantra places that Mantra without moving the others.

Example: A Chanter standing behind the frontline can see an ally surrounded by enemies. The Chanter uses a Bonus Action to activate Valor and places the Mantra at a point near that ally. Valor's effects extend from that point while the Chanter remains behind the group.

Swiftness Mantra — Opportunity Attack Immunity

The Speed bonus applies only while you or an eligible ally is within the Swiftness Mantra's radius. At the start of each beneficiary's turn, check whether that creature is within the Mantra's radius. If it is, it gains immunity to Opportunity Attacks for the entire turn. This immunity applies against all enemies, regardless of how many the creature passes during that turn, and remains for the turn even if the creature leaves the Mantra's radius.

If the creature starts its turn outside the Mantra's radius, it does not gain Opportunity Attack immunity that turn, even if it enters the radius during its movement. An enemy being inside the Mantra's radius does not grant immunity to a creature that started its turn outside the radius.

This immunity applies only to Opportunity Attacks. It does not grant immunity to other attacks or reactions.

Specialization Reassignment

Initial choice — Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is the final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Ascendant Cadence — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make either one Ultimate melee weapon attack against a creature within your reach or one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 60 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Cadence Damage. On a melee hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus fixed Radiant damage of 80 at Levels 10-14, 100 at Levels 15-19, or 120 at Level 20. On a spell hit, the target takes the same fixed Radiant damage.

Perfect Coordination. On a hit, choose yourself and up to a number of willing allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet. Each chosen creature may immediately move up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and has Advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. At Level 15, each also gains temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus + your Chanter spellcasting ability modifier. At Level 20, the movement may be up to the creature's full Speed.

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Cadence max 6. Resonant Network: a creature is in your Resonant Network while it is within the Mantra Radius of at least one active Mantra and eligible to benefit from that Mantra. Once on each of your turns, when you successfully affect a creature in your Resonant Network with a Chanter spell or Cadence Technique, choose one additional creature in your Network within 20 feet of the original target. It gains Echoed Guard (PB temporary HP), Echoed Step (5-foot safe movement from one creature), or Echoed Resolve (+1 to its next saving throw before the start of your next turn). A creature can benefit from your Resonant Network only once per turn. Greater Cadence Flow replaces Rhythmic Recovery. Once per round, when you spend Cadence and then successfully perform a qualifying action from a different category before the start of your next turn, choose one: regain 1 Cadence only if that qualifying action did not already generate Cadence through the normal engine; gain +10 feet Speed until the end of your next turn; or gain temporary HP equal to INT + PB until the start of your next turn. The Speed bonus stacks with Swiftness Mantra. This temporary HP is separate from Mantra Weaving, but normal temporary HP rules apply and temporary HP does not stack.

Continue your current Specialization or reassign under the universal rule, then gain the chosen path’s Level 14 advancement. This is the final normal Specialization decision.

Level 15

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Max Spell Level 6. Harmonic Protection: once per turn, when you successfully affect one or more allies with a Chanter spell or Cadence Technique, choose one affected ally. Until the start of your next turn, the next time that creature takes damage, reduce that damage by an amount equal to your Intelligence modifier + your Proficiency Bonus. If it receives Harmonic Protection again while already protected, the new protection replaces the old one and refreshes its duration. Harmonic Protection stacks with Guarding Rhythm; both reductions apply to the same damage instance.

Improved Ascendant Cadence

Your Ascendant Cadence now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and a Feat / ASI.

Level 17

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Cadence max 7. Apex Techniques become 3d8. Harmonic Mastery adds Harmonic Recovery (a Network ally heals 1d8 + INT and moves 5 feet safely) and Harmonic Dissonance (after your first weapon hit, the target makes a CON save; on a failure, its Speed is reduced by 10 feet, it cannot take Reactions, and it takes -1 to its next saving throw). Perfect Sequence triggers once per turn after completing Martial, Spell, and Chant across the current turn and previous turn; choose +1 Cadence, PB + INT temporary HP, or Advantage on your next attack or spell attack. Its +1 Cadence option is an explicit exception to normal once-per-turn Cadence generation and remains subject to your Cadence maximum.

Level 18

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and Max Spell Level 7. Grand Resonance spreads one Resonant Network trigger to up to two other Network creatures, and each recipient must receive a different Echo benefit. Perfect Mantras reach their final values: Valor grants +5 weapon damage per weapon attack, Resolve grants +3 to its relevant saving throws, Vitality grants PB + INT modifier (minimum +1) + 3 additional healing, and Swiftness grants +20 feet Speed. Mantra Stability Mastery keeps Mantras until the end of your next turn if you become Unconscious and restores them if you wake before then. Lingering Harmony: once per turn when you replace an active Mantra, choose one creature that benefited from the replaced Mantra immediately before replacement. That creature retains the entire effect of that Mantra, including numerical bonuses and special effects, until the start of your next turn. It need not remain within the old or new Mantra’s radius. For Swiftness, this includes the Speed bonus and immunity to Opportunity Attacks; that immunity remains limited to Opportunity Attacks. Lingering Harmony works with normal Bonus Action replacement, Perfect Mantra Switching, Harmonic Assault’s Harmonic Shift, and any future feature that explicitly replaces an active Mantra. Its once-per-turn limit applies across all methods. It does not count as an additional active Mantra and does not independently trigger Mantra Weaving, Harmonic Transition, or Cadence generation.

Level 19

Gain +1 Spell Advancement and the universal Empyrean Boon.

Level 20

Gain final +1 Spell Advancement. Eternal Cadence starts genuine combat with 3 Cadence instead of 0; your Cadence maximum remains 7. Eternal Flow triggers whenever you would gain Cadence while already at maximum. Convert each excess Cadence point into one selected benefit for an eligible Resonant Network creature: temporary HP equal to PB; immediate movement up to 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks; or +1 to its next attack roll or spell attack roll. Resolve each excess point separately and choose a benefit and eligible creature for each point; the same creature may receive multiple separate benefits. Identical ongoing bonuses do not stack, while immediate movement and separate benefits can be granted normally. During Resonant Crescendo, its temporary-HP conversion takes precedence over Eternal Flow, and one excess Cadence point cannot produce both conversions. Masterful Harmony triggers once per turn after you successfully perform two different qualifying categories and grants one additional different Harmonic Act without requiring the Attack action. It cannot grant another weapon attack or another Action-cost spell, is separate from Harmonic Assault, and cannot grant another Harmonic Assault use or bypass its once-per-turn limit. Resonant Crescendo is a Bonus Action, once per Long Rest, lasting 1 minute. It increases every active Mantra’s radius by 10 feet, waives the entire printed Cadence cost of the first Cadence Technique you use each round while it is active, converts excess Cadence into Network temporary HP, and grants its once-per-round reward when you complete a new Martial–Spell–Chant sequence. Perfect Mantra Switching allows one Mantra activation or replacement each turn without spending your Bonus Action.

Level 20 Capstone Interactions

Eternal Flow — overflow resolution. Each Cadence point that would exceed your maximum is resolved separately. Choose one benefit and one eligible creature in your Resonant Network for each point: temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus; immediate movement up to 5 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks; or +1 to the creature’s next attack roll or spell attack roll. You may choose different benefits and recipients for different overflow points, including the same creature more than once. Identical ongoing bonuses do not stack. Temporary HP follows the normal temporary HP rules.

Resonant Crescendo — duration and Mantra radius. Activate Resonant Crescendo as a Bonus Action once per Long Rest. It lasts 1 minute and adds 10 feet to the radius of every active Mantra. The normal 15-foot radius becomes 25 feet; a Harmonic Sage with Grand Harmony’s 25-foot radius reaches 35 feet. Mantra placement range remains 30 feet.

Resonant Crescendo — free Cadence Technique. The first Cadence Technique you use each round while Resonant Crescendo is active has its entire printed Cadence cost waived, including a technique whose printed cost is 2 Cadence. The allowance begins when Crescendo is activated and refreshes at the start of each subsequent round. A technique used before activation does not consume that round’s free use. Only techniques used while Crescendo is active qualify. Using the free technique counts as using the technique, but not as spending Cadence: it can trigger effects that require using a technique when their other conditions are met, but it cannot trigger effects that require spending Cadence or generate a refund based on spending Cadence. Normal Action, Bonus Action, and Reaction requirements remain unchanged. When Crescendo expires, no further free uses are available.

Resonant Crescendo — overflow priority. While Crescendo is active, its conversion takes precedence over Eternal Flow. Each excess Cadence point instead grants temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus to one eligible Resonant Network creature, including yourself if eligible. The same excess point cannot also produce an Eternal Flow benefit. Normal temporary HP rules apply.

Resonant Crescendo — sequence reward. Once per round, when you complete a new Martial–Spell–Chant sequence by performing the missing category across the current turn and previous turn, choose yourself if you are eligible for Resonant Network or another eligible Network ally. That creature receives one reward: heal 2d8 + INT; immediately move up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks; or gain +2 AC against the next attack. A sequence still present in the two-turn window cannot trigger another reward by itself. This reward has a limit independent of Perfect Sequence; the same completed sequence can trigger both features. Support effects from these rewards do not recursively count as new qualifying categories.

Perfect Mantra Switching. Once on each of your turns, activate or replace one Mantra without spending your Bonus Action. This waives only the Bonus Action cost. Mantra Weaving still triggers when its conditions are met, and Harmonic Transition still triggers when its conditions are met, subject to its once-per-turn limit. A free switch can qualify as a Chant category if it meaningfully changes effects on at least one creature. At Level 20, you may switch twice on your turn: once through Perfect Mantra Switching and once using your normal Bonus Action. Mantra Weaving may trigger separately for each switch. Repeated Chant events do not count as different categories for Cadence generation. Lingering Harmony can trigger from either replacement, subject to its own once-per-turn limit.

Masterful Harmony. Once per turn, after successfully performing two different qualifying categories, perform one additional Harmonic Act without requiring the Attack action. It may be a different Harmonic Act type from one already used that turn. It cannot grant another weapon attack or another Action-cost spell. Harmonic Verse and Harmonic Shift retain their normal costs and requirements. Masterful Harmony is separate from Harmonic Assault and cannot grant another Harmonic Assault use or bypass Harmonic Assault’s once-per-turn limit.

Apex Ascendant Cadence

Your Ascendant Cadence uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Example Rhythm Sequence

  1. Turn A: hit with a weapon attack (Martial), then later cast a Chanter spell that meaningfully affects a creature (Spell). Because the category changed, the second qualifying event can generate Cadence under the normal once-per-turn rule or Improved Cadence at higher level.
  2. At Level 5, cast an Action Chanter spell, then use Rhythmic Strike for one Bonus Action weapon attack. This is not a second Attack action and does not grant a third standard attack.
  3. At Level 7+, change one Mantra slot to create a meaningful Chant event, apply Mantra Weaving/Harmonic Transition, and continue the Martial–Spell–Chant cycle.
  4. At high level, Network/Harmonic riders are secondary effects: they improve allies but do not recursively count as new qualifying Cadence actions unless a feature explicitly says they do.
