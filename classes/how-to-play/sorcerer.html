How to Play Sorcerer

The Sorcerer is a spell specialist. The class rewards understanding what each spell is trying to accomplish and shaping your spell use around the battlefield rather than simply casting the largest effect available.

Your Core Loop

Read the field → Choose the right spell → Shape it → Resolve the effect → Reassess

Think in Spell Functions

Before casting, decide whether you need damage, control, area denial, defense, or another tactical effect. A smaller spell that solves the immediate problem can be better than a stronger spell used at the wrong time.

Manage Aether Focus

Aether Focus is tied to the Sorcerer's spell-shaping engine. Treat it as a tactical enhancement resource. Preserve it when an unshaped spell already accomplishes your goal, and spend it when shaping meaningfully improves the outcome.

On Your Turn

  1. Identify the battlefield problem you want your spell to solve.
  2. Choose the spell whose function best matches that problem.
  3. Check whether shaping the spell with Aether Focus creates a worthwhile improvement.
  4. Resolve the spell and immediately reassess the field.
  5. Keep your position safe enough to continue influencing the fight.

Common Mistakes

  • Using the most powerful-looking spell instead of the most appropriate one.
  • Spending Aether Focus on effects that do not materially improve the result.
  • Ignoring enemy positioning when selecting area effects.
  • Forgetting that control and positioning can be more valuable than raw damage.

Quick Reference

Identify the problem → Pick the spell → Shape only when worthwhile → Reassess.
