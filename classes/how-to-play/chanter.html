How to Play Chanter

The Chanter is a hybrid support combatant. Cadence rewards varied play: weapon attacks, spells, and active Mantra management all contribute to the class rhythm.

Your Core Loop

Attack → Cast → Maintain Mantra → Alternate → Build Cadence → Spend

Do Not Repeat Yourself

The Chanter's engine rewards alternating categories of action. Instead of mindlessly repeating one option, look for opportunities to weave weapon attacks, spells, and active Mantra management into a useful sequence.

Manage Cadence

Build Cadence through the class's alternating rhythm, then spend it when the payoff matters. Your goal is not simply to reach the maximum as quickly as possible; it is to reach a useful Cadence state while still making good combat decisions.

On Your Turn

  1. Check which action category best serves the current situation.
  2. Look for a useful alternation rather than repeating the same category.
  3. Maintain the Mantra that supports the party's immediate needs.
  4. Build Cadence while contributing meaningful offense or support.
  5. Spend Cadence when its effect changes the outcome of the encounter.

Common Mistakes

  • Repeating one action category when another would advance Cadence.
  • Maintaining the wrong Mantra for the current situation.
  • Building Cadence without considering what you will spend it on.
  • Forgetting that the Chanter is strongest when offense and support work together.

Quick Reference

Alternate your tools → Maintain the right Mantra → Build Cadence → Spend with purpose.
