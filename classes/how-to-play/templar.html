How to Play Templar

The Templar is a protective frontline class. Your strength comes from deliberately becoming the focus of enemy pressure and turning that pressure into Resolve and protection.

Your Core Loop

Challenge → Take pressure → Build Resolve → Protect → Retaliate

Understand Your Job

Your goal is not merely to have high defenses. You want enemy attention to matter. Use Challenge and your defensive tools to control where pressure goes, protect allies, and convert the resulting opportunities into useful Resolve spending.

On Your Turn

  1. Identify which enemy or group threatens your allies most.
  2. Position yourself so you can intercept or influence that threat.
  3. Use Challenge when directing enemy attention is valuable.
  4. Allow your defensive mechanics to generate opportunities rather than avoiding every hit.
  5. Spend Resolve when protection, mitigation, or a decisive defensive response is needed.

Positioning

Stand where enemies must deal with you to reach important allies. Do not wander away from the area you are protecting just to chase damage.

Common Mistakes

  • Playing as a passive damage sponge.
  • Using Challenge without considering where the enemy will be forced to fight.
  • Spending Resolve without a meaningful defensive purpose.
  • Leaving vulnerable allies outside your protective reach.

Quick Reference

Control attention → Absorb pressure → Generate Resolve → Protect the team.
