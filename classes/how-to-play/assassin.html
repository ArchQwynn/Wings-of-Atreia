How to Play Assassin

The Assassin is a precision striker. Your strongest turns come from choosing the right target, establishing Rune Marks, exploiting Sneak Attack, and using superior positioning.

Your Core Loop

Choose → Position → Mark → Burst → Reposition

Choose Your Target

Do not begin every fight by attacking the nearest enemy. Look for a target that is isolated, vulnerable, or strategically important. Your mechanics reward deliberate target selection.

Use Rune Marks Deliberately

Rune Marks are target-bound. Establish them on the enemy you actually intend to pressure, then use your attacks and features to capitalize on that setup. Avoid scattering your attention across several targets without a tactical reason.

On Your Turn

  1. Check whether your intended target is vulnerable to your burst plan.
  2. Use positioning and Sneak Attack opportunities to establish your advantage.
  3. Apply or maintain the Rune Marks your sequence requires.
  4. Deliver your burst while the target is exposed.
  5. Use movement such as Shadow Step to escape danger or reach your next priority target.

Common Mistakes

  • Starting a burst against the wrong target.
  • Ignoring positioning until after attacking.
  • Wasting Rune Mark setup on enemies you will immediately abandon.
  • Staying in danger after the burst is finished.

Quick Reference

Find the opening → Mark the target → Exploit it → Leave before the enemy turns the fight.
