How to Play Assassin

The Assassin is a precision striker. Your strongest play comes from selecting the right target, creating the conditions for Sneak Attack, applying Rune Marks deliberately, delivering your burst, and leaving before the enemy can punish your exposure.

Core Gameplay Loop

Scout → Position → Mark → Exploit → Burst → Reposition

Your Job

Remove or cripple high-value targets through deliberate setup and concentrated damage.

Your Engine

Rune Marks and Sneak Attack create the conditions for your precision burst.

Your Position

Choose angles that let you attack effectively without remaining exposed after the burst.

1. Choose the Target First

Do not begin by attacking whatever is closest. Look for an enemy whose removal, disruption, or weakened state materially helps the party. A target that is isolated or already vulnerable is usually a better investment than a durable enemy with little immediate impact.

2. Build the Opening

Your burst is strongest when the setup is intentional. Use positioning, Sneak Attack opportunities, and Rune Marks to create the conditions your sequence requires. If the opening is not ready, patience can be more valuable than forcing a weak attack.

3. Use Rune Marks Deliberately

Rune Marks are target-bound. Establish them on the enemy you actually intend to pressure and keep track of whether that target is still the correct priority. Spreading your setup across targets without a reason reduces the value of a precision class.

4. What to Do on Your Turn

  1. Check whether your intended target is still the highest-value target.
  2. Evaluate your position and whether Sneak Attack is available or worth setting up.
  3. Apply or maintain the Rune Marks required by your current plan.
  4. Deliver the strongest meaningful burst when the target is exposed.
  5. Use movement such as Shadow Step when it improves your safety, angle, or next target.

5. Decision Guide

  • Opening ready: capitalize rather than adding unnecessary setup.
  • Opening not ready: reposition or establish the conditions for the next exchange.
  • Target nearly defeated: finish it if doing so removes a meaningful threat; otherwise prepare for the next target.
  • Outnumbered: avoid prolonged exposure. Your advantage comes from choosing when and where to commit.
  • Target changes: reassess before carrying a setup sequence onto an enemy that no longer matters.

6. Positioning

Position before you attack whenever possible. Your ideal position gives you access to the target while preserving a route away from retaliation. Mobility is part of your offense because a successful burst is less valuable if it leaves you trapped in the enemy's response.

7. Specialization Play

Your Specialization changes how you convert the Assassin's setup into payoff. Identify what your chosen path rewards most, then build your Rune Mark, positioning, and burst decisions around that emphasis. The specialization should refine your assassination plan rather than replace the need for target selection.

8. Common Mistakes

  • Attacking before establishing the correct opening.
  • Marking a target that is not actually your priority.
  • Ignoring your escape route until after the burst.
  • Spending a strong sequence on a low-value enemy.
  • Confusing mobility with random movement.

9. First Session

Learn three habits: choose the target first, create the opening second, and always know where you will go after the burst. Mastery comes from learning when not to attack yet.

10. Advanced Play

Advanced Assassins plan the entire encounter around target transitions. Before finishing one target, identify the next opening, the safest route, and whether your existing setup can meaningfully carry forward.

Quick Reference

Pick the target → Create the opening → Mark → Burst → Escape or transition → Repeat.
