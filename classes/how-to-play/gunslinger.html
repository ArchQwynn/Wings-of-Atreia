How to Play Gunslinger

The Gunslinger is a mobile ranged precision class. Its gameplay is about maintaining firearm pressure while managing Tempo, ammunition, reload rhythm, and the distinction between Pistols and Muskets.

Core Gameplay Loop

Choose position → Fire → Build Tempo → Plan reload or transition → Fire again → Reposition

Your Job

Apply precise ranged pressure while staying mobile enough to control the engagement distance.

Your Resource

Tempo rewards deliberate sequencing. Your best use of the resource should fit the firearm state and target.

Your Position

Maintain a firing lane and a route away from enemies who can collapse on you.

1. Control the Rhythm

The Gunslinger is strongest when you think one shot ahead. A reload is not an interruption that suddenly appears; it is part of the sequence you should already be preparing for. Plan the end of your current firearm state before it becomes a problem.

2. Use Tempo Deliberately

Tempo should influence which attacks and sequences you choose. Do not spend or chase Tempo at the cost of a poor target, bad position, or badly timed reload. The resource is valuable because it improves a useful firearm sequence.

3. Choose Between Pistols and Muskets

Weapon choice should follow the engagement. Consider distance, mobility, target priority, and how much time you have to establish your next sequence. Do not commit to one firearm approach simply because it worked on the previous turn.

4. What to Do on Your Turn

  1. Check your distance and escape route.
  2. Identify the highest-value target you can affect.
  3. Check your current firearm and ammunition state.
  4. Choose the attack that best advances the current Tempo and firearm sequence.
  5. Plan the reload or weapon transition before the current state becomes a problem.
  6. Reposition when the battlefield demands it.

5. Decision Guide

  • Good firing position: use it, but know where you will move next.
  • Low ammunition: prepare the reload before the enemy forces you to do it at the worst time.
  • Tempo available: look for a meaningful use that fits the current firearm sequence.
  • Enemy closing: move early rather than waiting until you have no safe firing lane.
  • Target changes: reassess firearm choice and range before committing to the next sequence.

6. Positioning

Mobility is not decoration. A Gunslinger should continually maintain enough space to fire while preserving a route away from the most dangerous enemy. A good firing position is one that remains good after the enemy moves.

7. Specialization Play

Your Specialization changes what your firearm rhythm emphasizes. Identify whether your path rewards a particular weapon, tempo pattern, mobility style, or precision role, then make that emphasis part of your normal sequence rather than an occasional trick.

8. Common Mistakes

  • Waiting until ammunition is exhausted before thinking about reloads.
  • Standing still because the current firing position is comfortable.
  • Ignoring Tempo when selecting attacks.
  • Using the same firearm approach at every range.
  • Letting an enemy dictate when and where the next exchange occurs.

9. First Session

Learn the rhythm of your firearm first: fire, watch the ammunition state, and prepare the next sequence early. Then learn how Tempo and weapon choice improve that rhythm.

10. Advanced Play

Advanced Gunslingers treat reloads as planned transitions. They position themselves so a reload or weapon change happens safely, then emerge from that transition already prepared to exploit the next target or firing lane.

Quick Reference

Position → Fire → Build Tempo → Watch ammunition → Reload or transition deliberately → Keep moving.
