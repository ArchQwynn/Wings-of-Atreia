How to Play Gunslinger

The Gunslinger is a mobile ranged precision class. Its gameplay is about maintaining firearm pressure while managing Tempo and the rhythm of your reloads and weapon choice.

Your Core Loop

Acquire position → Fire → Build Tempo → Reload/transition → Fire again

Control the Rhythm

Tempo rewards deliberate sequencing. Think ahead about what your next shot or weapon state needs rather than waiting until you are empty or exposed. Reloads are part of your combat rhythm, not dead time to ignore.

Pistols and Muskets

Choose the firearm style that fits the current engagement. Consider distance, mobility, target priority, and the time available to establish your next sequence.

On Your Turn

  1. Check your distance and escape route.
  2. Choose the target that benefits most from your current firearm and Tempo state.
  3. Attack while preserving useful mobility.
  4. Plan your reload or weapon transition before your current ammunition state becomes a problem.
  5. Keep moving when the battlefield demands it.

Common Mistakes

  • Waiting until the last moment to reload.
  • Standing still because you have a good firing position.
  • Ignoring Tempo when choosing between attacks.
  • Using the same firearm approach regardless of distance or target.

Quick Reference

Position → Fire → Build rhythm → Reload intelligently → Reposition.
