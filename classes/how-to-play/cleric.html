How to Play Cleric

The Cleric is a support specialist whose greatest strength is making the right intervention at the right time. Healing is important, but good Cleric play is about deciding what the group needs before spending your resources.

Your Core Loop

Observe → Prioritize → Support → Build Divine Grace → Intervene

Do Not Heal Automatically

Not every missing hit point needs immediate attention. Consider whether an ally is in real danger, whether prevention or another support effect is better, and whether your action can solve a more urgent problem.

Build and Spend Divine Grace

Divine Grace is generated through meaningful support. Look for genuine opportunities to aid the party, then spend the resource when it produces a meaningful swing in survival, recovery, or support.

On Your Turn

  1. Identify the ally or battlefield problem that matters most right now.
  2. Decide whether prevention, healing, support, or offense is the best response.
  3. Use the appropriate ability or spell without wasting a stronger resource unnecessarily.
  4. Track Divine Grace and prepare for the next critical intervention.
  5. Stay positioned so you can reach the allies who need you.

Common Mistakes

  • Healing the lowest HP target automatically.
  • Ignoring dangerous conditions because HP looks acceptable.
  • Spending Divine Grace without a meaningful payoff.
  • Standing so far from the party that support becomes difficult.

Quick Reference

Read the field → Save lives first → Build Grace → Make the important intervention.
