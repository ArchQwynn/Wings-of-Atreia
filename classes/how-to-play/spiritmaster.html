How to Play Spiritmaster

The Spiritmaster fights through a bonded elemental spirit and a flexible spell toolkit. Your strength comes from coordinating yourself with your spirit rather than treating it as a separate afterthought.

Your Core Loop

Choose your spirit plan → Establish the bond → Direct pressure → Support the spirit → Adapt

Think as a Pair

Your spirit changes how you approach the encounter. Consider what your current elemental companion contributes, then choose spells and positioning that reinforce that contribution. Avoid playing as though your spirit's actions and your own are unrelated.

Manage Spirit Bond

Spirit Bond is part of the class engine. Use bond-based effects when they advance the current plan, and pay attention to the state of your spirit before committing to a sequence that depends on it.

On Your Turn

  1. Check your spirit's position and condition.
  2. Decide whether the encounter calls for pressure, control, defense, or support.
  3. Use your spirit and spells together rather than duplicating the same job unnecessarily.
  4. Spend or exploit Spirit Bond when it produces a meaningful advantage.
  5. Reposition both yourself and your spirit as the battlefield changes.

Common Mistakes

  • Leaving the spirit poorly positioned.
  • Using spells without considering what the spirit is already doing.
  • Treating Spirit Bond as a resource to spend automatically.
  • Ignoring the battlefield because your attention is fixed on your own spell list.

Quick Reference
