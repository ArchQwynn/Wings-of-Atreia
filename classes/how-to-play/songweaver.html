How to Play Songweaver

The Songweaver is a performance-driven class. Your gameplay revolves around choosing a Song Role, performing to create Refrains, changing roles deliberately, and turning those performances into Resonance.

Your Core Loop

Choose a Song Role → Perform → Create Refrains → Change roles deliberately → Build Resonance → Spend

Think in Performances

A Songweaver's strongest play is not simply casting the strongest available song. Decide what role the performance needs to fill, then build toward the Refrains and Resonance that make the next part of the sequence stronger.

Use Song Roles Deliberately

Changing Song Roles is part of the engine. Do not change randomly. Change when the new role answers the battlefield's current need or advances your Resonance plan.

On Your Turn

  1. Identify what the party or battlefield needs from your performance.
  2. Select the appropriate Song Role.
  3. Perform to establish or reinforce useful Refrains.
  4. Plan when a role change will advance your Resonance.
  5. Spend Resonance when it produces a meaningful effect rather than simply because it is available.

Common Mistakes

  • Changing Song Roles without a purpose.
  • Ignoring the value of existing Refrains.
  • Spending Resonance immediately instead of building a stronger sequence.
  • Choosing a performance without considering the party's current need.

Quick Reference

Choose the role → Perform → Build Refrains → Change with purpose → Build and spend Resonance.
