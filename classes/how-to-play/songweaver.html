How to Play Songweaver

The Songweaver is a performance-driven class. Your gameplay revolves around choosing a Song Role, performing to create Refrains, changing roles deliberately, and turning those performances into Resonance.

Core Gameplay Loop

Choose Song Role → Perform → Create Refrains → Change role deliberately → Build Resonance → Spend → Perform again

Your Job

Use performances to fill the party's current need while building a sequence that creates stronger future options.

Your Engine

Song Roles create the structure, Refrains record successful performances, and Resonance rewards deliberate role changes.

Your Position

Stay where your performances can affect the intended targets and where you can safely transition between roles.

1. Choose the Song Role First

Start with the battlefield need. Your Song Role should answer what the party currently needs from you rather than being selected randomly for resource generation. Once the role is chosen, select a performance that advances that role's purpose.

2. Build Refrains

Successful performances create temporary Refrains. Treat them as the memory of your recent performance sequence. Before changing direction, consider which Refrains are currently helping and whether your next performance can build on them.

3. Change Roles With Purpose

Changing Song Roles is part of the engine because deliberate role changes contribute to Resonance. Do not change roles merely because you can. Change when the new role answers the battlefield or advances a sequence worth building.

4. Build and Spend Resonance

Resonance is the reward for successful performance transitions. Its best use is usually tied to a meaningful moment: strengthening an important performance, answering a dangerous turn, or advancing the sequence your current role is creating.

5. What to Do on Your Turn

  1. Identify what the party or battlefield needs.
  2. Choose the Song Role that answers that need.
  3. Perform to establish or reinforce useful Refrains.
  4. Look ahead to the role you may need next.
  5. Change roles deliberately when the transition is beneficial.
  6. Track Resonance and spend it when the payoff matters.

6. Decision Guide

  • Role already solving the problem: continue the useful performance instead of changing simply to generate Resonance.
  • Battlefield need changes: change roles when the new role meaningfully answers that need.
  • Refrain already active: consider how the next performance can exploit it before abandoning the sequence.
  • Resonance available: identify a performance where the resource changes the outcome.
  • Party in danger: answer the immediate need first; resource generation is secondary to meaningful play.

7. Positioning

Your performance sequence is only useful if it can reach the intended battlefield. Maintain enough distance and line of effect to continue performing while keeping an escape or defensive option available.

8. Specialization Play

Your Specialization changes which Song Roles, performance patterns, or resource interactions deserve the most attention. Use the base Songweaver loop as your foundation, then identify what your chosen path wants you to repeat, transition, or amplify.

9. Common Mistakes

  • Changing Song Roles without a purpose.
  • Ignoring the value of existing Refrains.
  • Spending Resonance immediately instead of recognizing a stronger upcoming moment.
  • Choosing a performance without considering the party's current need.
  • Focusing so heavily on resource generation that the actual encounter is ignored.

10. First Session

Learn the three-part engine first: Role → Refrain → Resonance. Once you understand how changing roles contributes to Resonance, begin planning multi-performance sequences.

11. Advanced Play

Advanced Songweavers think in performances rather than individual turns. They know which Refrains they want active, which role should come next, and when a Resonance spend will make the entire sequence stronger.

Quick Reference

Choose the role → Perform → Build Refrains → Transition deliberately → Build Resonance → Spend at the important moment.
