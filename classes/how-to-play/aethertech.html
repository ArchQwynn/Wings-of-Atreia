How to Play Aethertech

The Aethertech is a piloted war machine. You are managing a Large Bastion, its integrated armaments, Aether Core, Heat, defenses, repairs, and the battlefield position of the machine itself.

Your Core Loop

Choose system → Engage → Spend Core → Manage Heat → Vent/Repair → Re-engage

Think Like a Pilot

Do not treat every weapon or system as an independent button. Decide what your Bastion is trying to accomplish, then choose the integrated systems that support that plan.

Manage Aether Core and Heat

Aether Core gives you access to powerful system effects, while Heat is the cost you must respect. Spending aggressively can produce a strong turn, but overheating at the wrong moment can compromise your next sequence. Plan your venting rather than waiting for a crisis.

On Your Turn

  1. Check the Bastion's position, Heat, and available Core.
  2. Identify the most important enemy or objective.
  3. Select the integrated armament or system that best serves the task.
  4. Spend Core when the tactical payoff justifies the resource and Heat consequences.
  5. Plan for your next turn: continue pressure, vent Heat, repair, or reposition as needed.

Common Mistakes

  • Spending Core without accounting for Heat.
  • Waiting until overheating is an emergency.
  • Using the wrong integrated system for the current target.
  • Forgetting that Bastion positioning determines what your systems can accomplish.

Quick Reference

Pilot the whole machine → Spend Core intelligently → Control Heat → Keep the Bastion operational.
