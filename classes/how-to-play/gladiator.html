How to Play Gladiator

The Gladiator is a frontline combatant built around sustained pressure, Momentum, and attack chains.

Your Core Loop

Engage → Build Momentum → Maintain your chain → Spend Momentum → Keep pressure

Play the Fight, Not Just the Resource

Momentum is a combat resource. Do not treat every point as something that must be spent immediately. Build pressure through your attacks, then spend Momentum when the resulting ability or chain advances your current goal.

On Your Turn

  1. Stay engaged with the target you are pressuring.
  2. Use your available attacks to establish or continue your chain.
  3. Watch your Momentum before committing to a spender.
  4. Spend Momentum when it creates a meaningful offensive advantage.
  5. Reassess your target rather than breaking your pressure unnecessarily.

Positioning

Gladiators generally want reliable melee access. Avoid unnecessary movement that breaks your attack sequence, but do not remain in a dangerous position merely to preserve a chain.

Common Mistakes

  • Spending Momentum simply because it is available.
  • Breaking a useful attack sequence without a reason.
  • Chasing a low-value target while a better target is available.
  • Ignoring battlefield positioning.

Quick Reference

Get in → Build → Chain → Spend → Stay on the offensive.
