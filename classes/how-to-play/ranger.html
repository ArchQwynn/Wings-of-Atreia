How to Play Ranger

The Ranger is a mobile ranged combatant whose effectiveness comes from distance, Focus, traps, and choosing where the fight happens.

Your Core Loop

Position → Prepare → Trap → Attack → Reposition

Own Your Range

Do not let enemies decide the distance of the fight when you can prevent it. Establish a useful firing position, use movement to preserve that advantage, and treat terrain and traps as part of your offense.

Manage Focus

Focus supports the Ranger's tactical rhythm. Use it in ways that complement your current target and trap setup rather than spending it automatically. Think ahead about what your next attack needs.

On Your Turn

  1. Check your distance and escape routes.
  2. Identify the highest-value target.
  3. Use traps when they can shape movement or expose a target.
  4. Attack from a position that preserves your advantage.
  5. Move when necessary instead of waiting until an enemy reaches you.

Trap Synergy

Your traps are not merely defensive objects. They can establish the conditions under which your attacks become more reliable and can support your critical-hit advantages against affected creatures.

Common Mistakes

  • Standing still after the enemy closes the distance.
  • Placing traps without considering enemy movement.
  • Ignoring escape routes.
  • Spending Focus without a plan for the next exchange.

Quick Reference

Control distance → Set the battlefield → Exploit the trap → Keep moving.
