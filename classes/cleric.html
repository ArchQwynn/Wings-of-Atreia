The Cleric is Wings of Atreia’s primary restorative spellcaster. It is deliberately more durable than the d6 full casters and is rewarded for meaningful intervention: healing an ally in real danger, actually preventing damage, or removing a significant hostile effect. Divine Grace then converts that successful support into more emergency power.

Class Identity

Clerics are Atreia’s foremost restorative and protective spellcasters. Their calling is not limited to healing wounds after the fact: they preserve the party through recovery, cleansing, protection, and timely intervention while retaining the ability to fight when necessary. Divine Grace represents the reserve of sacred and restorative power through which a Cleric answers moments of genuine need.

Tradition & Outlook

The Cleric tradition grows from Atreia’s priestly disciplines and is associated with healers, battlefield ministers, temple servants, protectors of communities, and Daevas who understand survival as a collective responsibility. Clerics often value service, perseverance, mercy, duty, or faith, but they need not share one personality or theology. A militant Cleric and a gentle healer may practice the same class through very different convictions.

Appearance & Adventuring

Clerics commonly combine durable armor with practical healing or ritual equipment. In Wings of Atreia they may use Maces, Hammers, Staffs, Quarterstaffs, and shields from Round Shields to Medium Shields according to their approved access. They adventure to protect lives, confront corruption or supernatural danger, recover sacred knowledge, serve a cause, or ensure that an expedition can survive places where retreat and ordinary medicine are not enough.

Meaningful Support and Divine Grace

Maximum Divine Grace equals PB + WIS modifier, minimum 2. Long Rest restores all; Short Rest restores PB. Once per round, when you Meaningfully Support yourself or an ally with a Cleric spell or feature, regain 1 Grace. Your normal once-per-round generation becomes available again at the start of your turn, allowing an off-turn protection to qualify without double-dipping around initiative boundaries.

Meaningful Support means one of the following actually occurs: restore HP while the creature was below half maximum HP; prevent or reduce damage by at least PB; or suppress/remove a mechanically significant hostile condition or effect. Healing a full-HP creature, preventing 0 damage, refreshing temporary HP without increasing it, cosmetic effects, or intentionally manufactured harmless damage do not qualify. Self-support must satisfy the same standard.

  • Temporary HP are not healing and cannot restore a creature from 0 HP.
  • Passive aura bonuses and passive AC do not generate Divine Grace unless a feature explicitly says so.
  • A secondary Cleric rider created by another Cleric feature does not recursively trigger Twin Devotion, Sacred Intervention, Graceful Channeling, Empowered Restoration, or another support sequence unless explicit.
  • Only one Cleric rolled-healing class rider normally modifies the same individual healing instance, except Graceful Restoration may coexist with one specialization rolled-healing rider.
  • Flat Cleric save penalties do not stack; use the largest.

Specialization Reassignment

Initial choice — Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is the final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Empyrean Judgment — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Judgment Damage. On a hit, the target takes fixed Radiant damage: 80 at Levels 10-14, 100 at Levels 15-19, or 120 at Level 20.

Mass Restoration. On a hit, choose yourself and up to a number of allies equal to your Proficiency Bonus within 30 feet of you. Each chosen creature regains a fixed amount of HP: 30 + your Cleric level at Levels 10-14, 40 + your Cleric level at Levels 15-19, or 50 + your Cleric level at Level 20. At Level 20, each healed creature may also end either Frightened or Poisoned on itself. If a miss is resolved with Release the Power, choose the same number of eligible creatures in the same range; each regains half of the applicable fixed healing amount, rounded down, and the Level 20 condition-removal rider does not occur.

Example Support Sequence

  1. An ally below half HP is healed by your Cleric spell. Healing Doctrine adds PB, the event qualifies as Meaningful Support and generates 1 Grace if your once-per-round generation is available.
  2. On a later enemy turn, that ally takes heavy attack damage. Protective Grace uses your Reaction and 1 Grace to reduce damage; if at least PB damage is actually prevented, it can generate the next available off-turn Grace event.
  3. From Level 11, an Action healing spell that supports an ally can include one Twin Devotion act without granting another Action or spell.
  4. At Level 15, sustained meaningful support builds Grace Momentum; at 3 Momentum the first Grace technique each turn costs 1 less, but a technique reduced to 0 counts as used rather than spent.
