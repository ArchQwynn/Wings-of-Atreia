Sorcerer

“This will only hurt for a second.”

The Sorcerer is the raw magical-damage benchmark. It casts through Fire, Ice, Lightning, and Wind spell chains, then spends Aether Focus only when a particular spell needs more damage, area, control pressure, or reliability. Aether Focus enhances spells; it is never the fuel required to cast ordinary Sorcerer spells.

Class Identity

Sorcerers are masters of direct Aetheric spellcraft, combining destructive magic with the ability to control the shape and tempo of a fight. They can punish clustered enemies, deny movement or action, and unleash powerful spells when an opening appears. Aether Focus represents the concentration and arcane discipline required to turn immense magical potential into reliable battlefield power.

Tradition & Outlook

The Sorcerer tradition grows from Atreia’s mage disciplines and is associated with formal magical study, experimentation, academies, battlefield arcanists, and Daevas fascinated by the structure of Aether itself. Sorcerers commonly value knowledge, precision, preparation, and intellectual mastery, although their personalities range from scholarly restraint to spectacular confidence. For many, magic is both a weapon and a language through which Atreia can be understood.

Appearance & Adventuring

Sorcerers favor cloth armor and magical implements such as Orbs and Spellbooks under the Wings of Atreia equipment rules. Their appearance may reflect academic, military, ceremonial, or highly individual magical traditions. They adventure to recover lost knowledge, study Aetheric phenomena, test magical theories, defend their people with overwhelming spellcraft, or confront powers that ordinary weapons cannot adequately answer.

Core Engine — Aether Focus

Maximum Aether Focus equals PB + INT modifier, minimum 2. A Long Rest restores all expended Aether Focus. A Short Rest restores Aether Focus equal to PB, up to your maximum. The resource is an enhancement reserve: casting an ordinary Sorcerer spell never requires Aether Focus unless a spell or class feature explicitly says otherwise.

Resource Flow — Generation, Recovery, Spending & Reset

Resource FlowRule
Starting StateAether Focus is a rest-based enhancement reserve and does not reset merely because combat begins or ends. After a Long Rest it is normally full.
MaximumPB + INT modifier, minimum 2. Aether Sage and the Level 20 capstone can explicitly increase this maximum.
Rest RecoveryLong Rest restores all expended Aether Focus. Short Rest restores Focus equal to PB, up to maximum; Aether Sage can improve this recovery.
Base GenerationThe base Sorcerer does not generate Aether Focus from ordinary spell hits or damage. Combat refunds exist only where a class or specialization feature explicitly grants them.
How You Spend ItIntensify 1 Focus; Widen 1 Focus; Potent Control 1 Focus; other Sorcerer features spend Aether Focus only when their text states a cost. Ordinary spellcasting costs no Focus.
Refunds / DiscountsAether Sage and later features may discount a shaping cost or refund Focus after a qualifying shaped spell. A refund is not a second casting or a new spell trigger.
PersistenceUnused Aether Focus persists between encounters until spent or restored.
Important DistinctionAether Focus is a Sorcerer class resource. It is not environmental Aether, Refined Aether, an Aether State, or a universal damage type.

Level 1 Spell Shaping

ShapingCostExact Level 1 Effect
Intensify1 FocusWhen an eligible Sorcerer spell deals damage, increase one damage resolution by +1d6.
Widen1 FocusIncrease an eligible radius or cone by 5 ft; a line by 10 ft; or a cube by 5 ft on each side. The spell must have an area that can legally be widened.
Potent Control1 FocusChoose one creature affected by an eligible spell whose non-damaging control or movement rider requires a save. That creature has Disadvantage on its first save against that rider for this casting.
  • At Level 1, apply at most one Spell Shaping enhancement to a single spell.
  • You choose and pay the shaping cost when the spell is cast, before its affected rolls are fully resolved unless the feature says otherwise.
  • Shaping never resets a spell’s At-Will/Cooldown/Rest frequency, creates a second casting, or bypasses immunity.

Focused Casting

At Level 2 choose one Focused Casting doctrine. Focused Casting modifies a spell you shape with Aether Focus; it does not grant another spell, another Action, or a new specialization.

Focused Casting DoctrineExact Benefit
Stable ChannelOnce on each of your turns when you apply Spell Shaping to a Sorcerer spell that deals rolled damage, you may reroll one damage die rolled for that casting that shows 1 or 2. You must use the new result. Only one die can be rerolled for that casting through this doctrine.
Precise ProjectionOnce on each of your turns when you apply Spell Shaping to a Sorcerer spell with an area of effect, choose one creature you can see in that area. That creature is unaffected by that casting. This does not change the spell’s origin, range, area size, or Full Cover restrictions.
Anchored ConcentrationWhen you apply Spell Shaping to a Sorcerer spell that requires Concentration, that Concentration is Anchored. The first time before the start of your next turn that a hostile spell or feature would directly end that Concentration without making you Incapacitated, ignore that effect. Anchored Concentration cannot prevent Concentration from ending because you become Incapacitated, voluntarily end it, or begin another Concentration effect.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Spellcasting, Aether Focus and Spell Shaping

You gain Sorcerer spellcasting, Focus max PB + INT (minimum 2), Long/Short Rest recovery, and the one-enhancement Intensify/Widen/Potent Control engine. Maximum Spell Level 1.

Level 2 — Aetheric Attunement and Focused Casting

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 2. Choose Stable Channel, Precise Projection, or Anchored Concentration from the Focused Casting doctrine table above. Aetheric Attunement is the class’s formal connection between elemental spellcasting and the Aether Focus reserve. Aetheric Attunement has no separate action, resource cost, numerical bonus, or additional effect beyond the spell advancement, spell-level increase, and Focused Casting doctrine gained at this level.

Level 3 — Sorcerer Specialization

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Choose Elementalist, Spellbinder, or Aether Sage.

Elementalist — Elemental Devotion

Choose Fire, Ice, or Lightning as your Devoted Element. Once on each of your turns when a Sorcerer spell of that element successfully affects a hostile, choose one affected creature: Fire deals +PB Fire damage; Ice reduces Speed by an additional 5 feet until your next turn; Lightning imposes -1 on its next attack roll before your next turn.

Spellbinder — Arcane Seal

Once on each of your turns when a hostile creature fails a save against your Sorcerer spell, apply Anchor (Speed -10 ft) or Disrupt (no Opportunity Attacks) until the start of your next turn.

Aether Sage — Deep Reservoir

Increase maximum Aether Focus by 1. Whenever a Short Rest restores Aether Focus, restore 1 additional Focus, up to maximum.

Level 4 — Feat / ASI

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 3, and the universal Feat / ASI.

Level 5 — Greater Spell Shaping

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Intensify improves to a d8-based enhancement. Widen can add +30 feet to the range of an eligible spell instead of changing its area. Potent Control can affect one creature with Disadvantage or two creatures each with a -2 modifier, subject to the spell’s legal targets and the class non-stacking rule.

Level 6 — Advanced Spell Access

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 4. No new base action is added; your specialization receives its Level 6 feature.

Elementalist — Focused Element

Once on each of your turns after spending at least 1 Focus to shape a Devoted Element spell that affects a hostile: Fire adds 1d6 Fire to one damaged creature; Ice makes one creature that failed a save unable to make Opportunity Attacks until your next turn; Lightning deals PB Lightning to a second hostile within 10 feet of one damaged creature.

Spellbinder — Binding Geometry and Anchored Formula

While concentrating on an eligible persistent radius/sphere/cube/cylinder, use a Bonus Action once per turn to move its center 10 feet to a legal point within original casting range. Walls, lines, cones, creature/object-anchored or explicitly fixed areas cannot move this way. Once per round while you are Concentrating on a Sorcerer spell, when you fail a saving throw against an effect that would cause you to lose Concentration if that effect were applied, you may spend 1 Aether Focus to add +2 to that saving throw, potentially turning the failure into a success.

Aether Sage — Adaptive Doctrine

Gain a second different Focused Casting doctrine. After a Short or Long Rest, you may replace either current doctrine with the remaining option.

Level 7 — Elemental Mastery Progression

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Maximum Spell Level remains 4; spend advancements on the spell chains that define your build.

Level 8 — Feat / ASI

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 5, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 9 — High-Tier Spell Access

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Maximum Spell Level remains 5.

Level 10 — Specialization Advancement

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 6. Your specialization gains its Level 10 feature.

Aetheric Cataclysm — Ultimate Ability

Ultimate Activation - 100 DP. As your Action and Bonus Action, make one Ultimate spell attack against a creature you can see within 120 feet. After this Ultimate attempt resolves, you enter Ultimate Recovery during your next turn.

Primary Eruption. On a hit, the target takes fixed Force damage: 100 at Levels 10-14, 120 at Levels 15-19, or 140 at Level 20.

Cataclysm. On a hit, every other hostile creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on the target makes a DEX save against your Spell Save DC. A creature takes fixed Force damage of 50 at Levels 10-14, 60 at Levels 15-19, or 70 at Level 20 on a failure, or half that amount on a success. You may exclude allies from the sphere. Its radius becomes 30 feet at Level 20.

Elementalist — Elemental Mastery

Once on each of your turns when a Devoted Element spell deals damage, choose one damaged creature: add INT modifier to that creature’s damage and ignore resistance to that element for that damage instance. Immunity remains.

Spellbinder — Residual Constraint

Once on each of your turns when a hostile succeeds on a save against your Sorcerer spell and the failed-save effect would have reduced Speed, forced movement, denied Reactions, or imposed another non-damaging control rider, choose Speed -10 feet or no Opportunity Attacks until your next turn.

Aether Sage — Efficient Channel

The first Focus expenditure after each Short or Long Rest costs 1 less, minimum 0. In addition, once per round after you spend 2+ Focus on one Sorcerer spell and it successfully affects at least one creature, regain 1 Focus after resolution; the net Focus cost of that spell cannot be reduced below 1 by this refund.

Level 11 — Dual Shaping

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. You may apply two different legal Spell Shaping enhancements to one Sorcerer spell, paying the normal combined Aether Focus cost. The spell is still cast only once and each shaping option can be applied only once unless a feature says otherwise.

Level 12 — Feat / ASI

Gain +2 Spell Advancements, Maximum Spell Level 7, and a Feat / ASI.

Level 13 — Advanced Caster Progression

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Maximum Spell Level remains 7.

Level 14 — Final Specialization Advancement

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 8. Your specialization gains its final milestone.

Elementalist — Elemental Ascendance

Once on each of your turns after spending Focus to shape a Devoted Element spell: Fire adds 2d8 Fire to one damaged creature; Ice makes one hit/failed-save creature Speed 0 and unable to take Reactions until your next turn; Lightning makes one damaged creature unable to take Reactions and deals 2d8 Lightning to a different hostile within 15 feet.

Spellbinder — Perfect Binding

Once on each of your turns when a hostile fails a save against a Sorcerer spell shaped with Potent Control, choose Lock (Speed 0 and no Reactions) or Compression (cannot Dash and -2 next attack) until your next turn. Binding Geometry moves eligible areas 20 feet instead of 10.

Aether Sage — Triune Shaping

Dual Shaping can now apply Intensify, Widen, and Potent Control together when all are legal. If all three are applied, reduce total Focus cost by 1 to a minimum total cost of 2. Deep Reservoir’s maximum-Focus increase becomes +2, and you benefit from all three Focused Casting doctrines.

Level 15 — Near-Apex Spell Mastery

Gain +2 Spell Advancements. Maximum Spell Level remains 8.

Ascendant Aetheric Cataclysm

Your Aetheric Cataclysm now uses all Levels 15-19 values and improvements described in its Level 10 feature.

Level 16 — Feat / ASI

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and a Feat / ASI. Maximum Spell Level remains 8.

Level 17 — Apex Spellcasting Tier

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and Maximum Spell Level 9, the final universal Sorcerer spell mastery cap.

Level 18 — High-Tier Mastery

Gain +2 Spell Advancements at the final Spell Level 9 cap.

Level 19 — Empyrean Boon

Gain +2 Spell Advancements and the Empyrean Boon.

Level 20 — Perfect Sorcerer

Gain your final +2 Spell Advancements, reaching 38 total. Maximum Spell Level remains 9. You also gain Perfect Reservoir, Battle-Ready Focus, and Perfect Shaping.

Perfect Reservoir

Your maximum Aether Focus increases by 2.

Battle-Ready Focus

When you roll Initiative for genuine combat, if you have fewer than 3 Aether Focus, gain enough Aether Focus to reach 3, up to your maximum. Artificial Initiative cannot trigger this.

Perfect Shaping

Once on each of your turns when you apply one or more Spell Shaping options to a Sorcerer spell, reduce the total Aether Focus cost of that casting by 1. A single shaping option can therefore cost 0. If two or more different shaping options are applied to the same casting, the final total shaping cost cannot be reduced below 1 by any combination of Sorcerer cost reductions. Perfect Shaping does not make an illegal shaping option legal, create another casting, or reset the spell’s At-Will/Cooldown/Rest frequency.

Apex Aetheric Cataclysm

Your Aetheric Cataclysm uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements described in the Level 10 feature.

Spellcasting Example

  1. Routine turn: cast an At-Will or Cooldown Sorcerer spell without spending Aether Focus.
  2. Critical turn: decide before resolution that the spell needs more damage, area, or control and spend Focus on the legal shaping option.
  3. Level 11+: Dual Shaping lets one spell carry two different shaping options, but it is still one casting with the spell’s normal frequency.
  4. Aether Sage Level 14 can use all three legal shaping modes together; Elementalist instead gains the highest specialization damage; Spellbinder gains the strongest bounded control.
