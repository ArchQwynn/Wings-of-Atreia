Class Overview

The Templar is the system’s premier martial defender. It does not force enemies to attack it through mind control; instead, Challenge makes ignoring the Templar tactically dangerous while Resolve turns incoming pressure into protection, damage reduction, retaliation, and formation control.

Class Identity

Templars are guardian-warriors whose strength is measured by what they can endure and whom they can keep standing. They occupy the most dangerous space in a battle, challenge enemies that would otherwise overwhelm vulnerable allies, and turn personal resilience into protection for the group. Resolve is the mechanical expression of this identity: a Templar grows most meaningful when danger is real and protection requires commitment.

Tradition & Outlook

The Templar tradition grows from Atreia’s warrior disciplines but places duty, discipline, guardianship, and control above personal glory. Templars are found among legion defenders, fortress guardians, sworn protectors, escorts, and Daevas who believe power carries an obligation to stand between danger and others. Individual Templars may be stern, compassionate, devout, pragmatic, or fiercely patriotic, but the class ideal is constancy under pressure.

Appearance & Adventuring

Templars are commonly associated with plate armor, shields, and the unmistakable silhouette of a heavily protected frontline Daeva. Their equipment tends to emphasize readiness and endurance rather than ornament alone. Templars adventure because someone must hold the line: to defend a community, fulfill an oath, guard companions, reclaim threatened territory, or confront enemies that cannot safely be allowed to reach the rest of the party.

Core Engine — Resolve

Resolve represents the Templar converting battlefield pressure into defensive certainty. Unless a later feature states otherwise, Resolve becomes 0 when Initiative is rolled, becomes 0 when combat ends, and cannot be gained while you are Incapacitated.

  • Hold the Line: gain 1 Resolve when a hostile creature you can see hits you with an attack while you are within that creature’s melee reach.
  • Protect the Vulnerable: gain 1 Resolve when a Templar class feature successfully reduces, prevents, redirects, or intercepts damage that would otherwise be dealt to another creature, or when a feature explicitly says the protection qualifies.
  • Defy the Templar: gain 1 Resolve when a creature Challenged by you attacks another creature while you were a valid target for that attack.
  • You can normally gain no more than 1 Resolve during any creature’s turn. One event grants Resolve only once even if it satisfies multiple triggers.
  • Maximum Resolve: 3 at Levels 1–4; 4 at 5–8; 5 at 9–14; 6 at 15–20.

Resolve Timing Rules

  • Resolve from Hold the Line may immediately be spent on Resolute Guard against the same triggering attack’s damage.
  • Resolve generated by a successful Guardian’s Intercession is gained before redirected damage is applied, so it may be used against the redirected attack if otherwise legal.
  • Resolve generated because Guardian’s Shelter changed a save from failure to success may immediately pay for Resolute Shelter at Level 14.
  • Challenge may remain on a creature while its penalty is temporarily suppressed because you are out of range, cannot be perceived, or are not a legal target.

Signature Defensive Mechanics

Resolute Guard

When you take damage from an attack, spend 1 Resolve with no action required to reduce that damage by 1 Guard Die + your CON modifier, minimum 0. The Guard Die is d6 at Level 1, d8 from Level 5, d10 from Level 9, and d12 from Level 15. You can normally use Resolute Guard only once per turn unless a Templar feature explicitly says otherwise.

Challenge

As a Bonus Action, choose one hostile creature you can see within 30 feet. It is Challenged until the start of your next turn. Initially you can maintain one Challenge. While the creature is within 30 feet, can perceive you, and you are a valid target for at least one attack it could make, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you. Challenge is nonmagical, has no save, and is neither Charm nor Fear.

Guardian’s Intercession

When an ally within 5 feet is hit by an attack, use your Reaction to redirect that attack to yourself. Use the same attack roll. If it hits your AC, you take the attack and its damage/effects normally; if it misses your AC, the redirected attack misses. A successful interception qualifies for Protect the Vulnerable when it actually prevents the ally from receiving the attack’s damage.

Specialization Reassignment

Initial choice — Level 3. Choose one Specialization available to your class.

Reassignment checkpoints — Levels 6, 10, and 14. When gaining one of these levels, you may either continue your current Specialization or change to another Specialization available to your class.

If you change Specialization, remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the complete feature package of the newly selected Specialization through your current checkpoint. Features from different Specializations cannot be mixed, retained, or combined.

When newly entering a Specialization, make any choices required by the features you now gain. Continuing the same Specialization does not reset earlier internal choices unless an explicit feature says otherwise.

If the removed Specialization granted extra known options, selections, capacities, or Specialization-dependent maxima, remove, reselect, or recalculate them as necessary to satisfy the new path. Reassignment itself does not restore HP or class resources, and current values cannot remain above any new maximum.

Reassignment occurs only as part of gaining Level 6, Level 10, or Level 14. It cannot be performed through a rest, downtime, training, ordinary retraining, or between adventures. The Level 14 decision is the final normal Specialization choice and remains locked through Level 20 unless a later explicit rule says otherwise.

Level-by-Level Progression

Level 1 — Resolve, Resolute Guard, Challenge, Guardian’s Intercession

You gain the complete Resolve engine and the three signature defensive mechanics described above. Maximum Resolve is 3 and your Guard Die is d6.

Level 2 — Combat Discipline and Protective Reflexes

Shield Discipline: while wielding a shield, the first time each round an attack redirected through Guardian’s Intercession hits you, reduce its damage by PB before Resolute Guard. Weapon Discipline: once on each of your turns when you hit with a one-handed melee weapon, add PB to that attack’s damage. Fortress Discipline: while wearing Plate, forced movement against you is reduced by 5 ft, minimum 0, and you have Advantage on checks and saves to resist Prone. Vanguard Discipline: once each turn after moving at least 10 ft toward a hostile and ending within melee reach, you may move through hostile spaces during that movement; you cannot end occupied and OAs against you during it have Disadvantage. Retraining is available at class Feat/ASI levels.

Protective Reflexes: after successful Guardian’s Intercession, the protected ally may move up to 5 ft without provoking from the creature whose attack you intercepted, if it can legally move.

Level 17 — Unyielding Presence

Unceasing Challenge: at the start of each turn, each current Challenge satisfying its 30-ft, perception, and valid-target conditions may be maintained until your next turn without a Bonus Action. This is an additional maintenance option to Relentless Challenge.

You have two Reactions each round. The additional Reaction can be used only for a Templar class feature, not an ordinary Opportunity Attack, feat, item, or non-Templar feature. Both cannot answer the same trigger and both refresh at the start of your turn.

Level 20 — Unyielding Guardian

Battle-Ready Resolve: when you roll Initiative, begin combat with 3 Resolve instead of 2, up to your maximum.

Refuse to Fall: once per Long Rest, when damage would reduce you to 0 HP but would not kill you outright, spend 3 Resolve with no action required to drop to 1 HP instead.

Resolve Through Sacrifice: when Guardian’s Intercession or Guardian’s Shelter successfully protects another creature and Protect the Vulnerable would grant Resolve, gain 2 instead of 1. Those 2 points count as one generation event and may exceed the normal one-Resolve-per-creature-turn limit. Hold the Line and Defy the Templar remain unchanged.

Apex Empyrean Bulwark: Empyrean Bulwark uses its Level 20 damage and all Level 20 improvements.

Example Defensive Sequences

  1. An enemy attacks a Challenged ally: Defy the Templar generates Resolve because you were a valid target. If you are a Justicar and the enemy remains in reach, Retributive Challenge can answer after the attack resolves.
  2. An adjacent ally is hit: Guardian’s Intercession redirects the attack. If the redirection prevents damage to the ally, Protect the Vulnerable grants Resolve before redirected damage is applied; that Resolve can immediately pay Resolute Guard.
  3. At Level 14, Guardian’s Shelter turns an ally’s save from failure to success. Protect the Vulnerable generates Resolve, then Resolute Shelter may immediately spend that Resolve to turn the successful half-damage result into no damage.
  4. A Level 17 Bulwark intercepts a lethal attack: No One Falls can supply the emergency Intercession, Last Wall spends 1 Resolve for resistance, then Resolute Guard can reduce the already-halved damage further.
