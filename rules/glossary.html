Core Terms Every Player Should Know

TermMeaning at the Table
ActionYour main turn action.
Bonus ActionA secondary action available only when a feature, spell or rule explicitly gives an option using it.
ReactionA response to a trigger, usually outside your turn. Normally one per round unless a feature grants another.
MovementDistance you may travel during your turn, separate from your Action.
Proficiency Bonus (PB)Universal proficiency modifier that rises with character level.
Advantage / DisadvantageRoll two d20s and use the higher / lower.
Genuine CombatA real encounter using initiative and combat timing; artificial combat cannot farm resources.
Short Rest / Long RestRecovery periods of 1 hour / 8 hours.
Class Save DCNormally 8 + PB + the class ability identified by the class.
ConcentrationOnly one Concentration effect at a time; Incapacitated ends it.
Secondary riderAn extra effect caused by another feature; it does not recursively trigger the same engine unless explicitly permitted.
Boss ActionThe dedicated off-turn special-action framework used by every ordinary Boss-rank monster as defined in its statblock.
Lord ActionThe dedicated off-turn action framework used by Empyrean and Dragon Lords.
