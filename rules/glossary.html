Glossary & Core Terms

A player-facing A–Z reference for the vocabulary used throughout Wings of Atreia, with direct links to the full rules and lore pages.

How to use this page: definitions here are quick-reference summaries. Follow the “Read more” link whenever you need the complete rule, class text, or world context.
ABCDEFGIKLMPRSUW

46 terms

A

Action

Your main turn action. Many attacks, spells, class features, and other options specify that they use your Action.

Read more: Combat →

Advantage / Disadvantage

Roll two d20s and use the higher result for Advantage or the lower result for Disadvantage.

Read more: Core Resolution →

Aether

The supernatural power that underlies Daeva abilities, spellcasting, flight, and many extraordinary phenomena in Atreia.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

Aether Enhancement

The higher equipment-quality path beyond Profession +2. Aether upgrades are level-gated and handled separately from ordinary Profession work.

Read more: Equipment →

Ascension

The transformation by which a mortal becomes a Daeva and receives Wings. WoA begins with this event in Session 0.

Read more: Character Creation →

At-Will

A spell or ability frequency that may be used whenever its normal action and other requirements are met.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

B

Background

A character-creation choice representing experience and identity before or around Ascension; it provides its defined skills and related benefits.

Read more: Backgrounds →

Bonus Action

A secondary turn action available only when a feature, spell, or rule explicitly gives an option that uses it.

Read more: Combat →

C

Character Manager

The official WoA character-management method for creation records, advancement, current resources, equipment, spells, and other tracked character data.

Read more: Character Creation →

Class

Your Daeva’s primary mechanical path. WoA uses eleven classes and does not use multiclassing.

Read more: Classes →

Class Resource

A class-specific pool, charge, stance, meter, or other tracked mechanic defined by that class.

Read more: Quick Reference →

Class Save DC

Normally 8 + Proficiency Bonus + the class ability identified by your class, unless a rule states otherwise.

Read more: Core Resolution →

Concentration

A spell/effect limit: only one Concentration effect can be maintained at a time, and Incapacitated ends it. WoA does not use Concentration saving throws.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

Condition

A named rules state such as Poisoned or Incapacitated that changes what a creature can do or how rolls affect it.

Read more: Combat →

Cooldown

A spell or ability frequency that becomes unavailable after use until its stated cooldown is satisfied; WoA also uses the universal out-of-combat cooldown rule where applicable.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

D

Daeva

An Ascended immortal warrior of Atreia. Player characters in Wings of Atreia are Daevas.

Read more: Character Creation →

Daeva Points (DP)

A resource unlocked at Level 10 and earned through participation in genuine combat victories. DP powers Ultimate Abilities and persists through rests.

Read more: Daeva Points & Ultimates →

E

Empyrean Boon

A powerful universal character option gained at Level 19 according to the advancement rules.

Read more: Feats & Empyrean Boons →

Empyrean Lord

One of the great Daeva Lords whose authority and legacy shape Elyos and Asmodian civilization, together with the two historic Tower Guardians.

Read more: World →

F

Faction

Your character’s allegiance as Elyos or Asmodian. Faction affects culture, homeland, and campaign relationships rather than class statistics.

Read more: Character Creation →

Feat

A universal character option chosen at designated advancement points instead of an Ability Score Improvement where the rules allow it.

Read more: Feats & Empyrean Boons →

Flight Slot

A tracked unit of a Daeva’s flight capacity. The number of Flight Slots increases with character level.

Read more: Flight →

G

Genuine Combat

A real encounter using initiative and combat timing. Artificial encounters cannot be used to farm resources such as Daeva Points.

Read more: Combat →

I

Implement

A spellcasting equipment category that can follow the established quality and enhancement progression.

Read more: Equipment →

K

Kinah

The standard currency used for purchases, profession training, supplies, and other economic transactions.

Read more: Professions & Economy →

L

Legendary Equipment

Story-driven top-tier equipment above the Aether enhancement tiers. Legendary items are not ordinary Profession products.

Read more: Equipment →

Long Rest

An 8-hour recovery period. What it restores depends on the specific class, spell, feature, or rule.

Read more: Combat →

Lord Action

The dedicated off-turn action framework used by Empyrean Lords and Dragon Lords.

Read more: Combat →

M

Milestone Advancement

WoA’s advancement method. Characters gain levels through campaign milestones rather than experience-point totals.

Read more: Advancement →

Movement

The distance you may travel during your turn, separate from your Action.

Read more: Combat →

Multiclassing

Combining levels from multiple classes. Wings of Atreia does not use multiclassing.

Read more: Advancement →

P

Profession

A trained downtime discipline used for approved upgrades, consumables, meals, or related work. A character can have only one Active Profession at a time.

Read more: Professions & Economy →

Proficiency Bonus (PB)

The universal proficiency modifier that rises with character level and is added wherever a rule says you are proficient.

Read more: Core Resolution →

R

Reaction

A response to a trigger, usually outside your own turn. Normally you have one Reaction per round unless a feature explicitly grants another.

Read more: Combat →

Rest Spell

A spell frequency that refreshes after the specified Short Rest or Long Rest rather than by cooldown.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

S

Secondary Rider

An extra effect caused by another feature. A secondary rider does not recursively trigger the same engine unless the rules explicitly permit it.

Read more: Core Resolution →

Session 0: Ascension

The default WoA opening in which player characters begin unascended, undergo Ascension, and receive their Wings before the wider campaign begins.

Read more: Character Creation →

Shield

A defensive equipment category with its own enhancement path. Shields do not use the Profession +1/+2 upgrade stages.

Read more: Equipment →

Short Rest

A 1-hour recovery period. What it restores depends on the specific class, spell, feature, or rule.

Read more: Combat →

Specialization

A class path first chosen at Level 3. WoA allows reassignment decisions at the established Level 6, 10, and 14 checkpoints.

Read more: Classes →

Spell Advancement

When a spell advances, its Spell Level increases by +1. If that new Spell Level is not referenced by the spell’s scaling or milestones, record the new level but make no other mechanical change.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

Spell Level

A spell’s advancement value. It is distinct from character level and changes through the Spell Advancement rules.

Read more: Aether & Spellcasting →

U

Ultimate Ability

A powerful class ability unlocked at Level 10 that uses Daeva Points and follows the universal Ultimate activation and miss rules.

Read more: Daeva Points & Ultimates →

W

War Asset (WA)

The strategic resource used by the Abyss Warfront system for armies, units, and fortress-scale conflict.

Read more: Legions & Warfronts →

Warfare

Large-scale Warfront play. Daeva Points and Ultimate Abilities are not usable in Warfare.

Read more: Legions & Warfronts →

Wings

The physical and supernatural manifestation received through Ascension, enabling Daeva flight and following its own equipment-quality progression.

Read more: Flight →
