Mantra Placement

Mantras are manifested fields of spiritual Aether that the Chanter summons into the battlefield.

Placement Rule

When you activate or replace a Mantra, you may place it at a point you can see within 30 feet of yourself. The Mantra is centered on that chosen point and remains at that location until dismissed, replaced, or ended by another rule. It does not follow the Chanter after placement.

The Chanter does not need to remain within the Mantra's radius to maintain it.

Placement Range vs. Mantra Radius

  • Placement Range: You may place or replace a Mantra at a visible point within 30 feet.
  • Mantra Radius: The area measured outward from that Mantra's placement point.
  • Increasing a Mantra's Radius does not increase its placement range unless a feature explicitly says so.

Multiple Mantras

When a feature allows the Chanter to maintain multiple Mantras, each Mantra has its own placement point. Activating or replacing one Mantra places that Mantra without moving the others.

Example

A Chanter stands 20 feet behind the frontline and can see an ally surrounded by enemies. The Chanter uses a Bonus Action to activate Valor and places the Mantra at a point near that ally. Valor's effect extends from that placement point, allowing the Chanter to remain behind the frontline while the Mantra supports the nearby group.

Table Rule: This rule applies to all Chanter Mantras unless a specific Mantra or feature states otherwise. The 30-foot placement range is separate from the Mantra Radius.
