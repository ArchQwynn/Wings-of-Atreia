Player FAQ

A player-safe reference for Wings of Atreia rules that are easy to misread by habit or commonly need clarification at the table.

Authority. The FAQ explains the rules; it does not replace them. When a FAQ answer and the current governing rule differ, use the current Player Handbook/Core Rulebook and later explicitly approved synchronized rulings. This website version incorporates the current Ultimate miss procedure and the approved Spell Advancement clarification.

Character Creation & Advancement

Do I choose a race or species?

No. Every standard player character is a Daeva. Choose Elyos or Asmodian as faction/culture. The two factions use the same universal Daeva mechanical foundation.

How do I assign Ability Scores?

Assign 16, 14, 14, 12, 10, and 8 freely among STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, and CHA. There is no ability-score rolling or point buy.

Can I multiclass?

No. A Daeva advances in one class from Level 1 through Level 20.

Do characters gain XP?

No. Advancement is Milestone only. The GM awards levels for meaningful campaign progress.

When do I choose an ASI or Feat?

Universal ASI/Feat choices occur at Levels 4, 8, 12, and 16. Level 19 grants an Empyrean Boon, not another normal ASI/Feat choice.

What does a Background give me?

A Background gives exactly two Skill Proficiencies chosen from that Background's eligible skill list and can establish narrative relevance or permission where appropriate. It does not automatically grant Advantage, Expertise, or guaranteed success.

Does duplicate proficiency become Expertise?

No. Proficiency adds PB once. Expertise exists only when an explicit feature grants it.

d20 Tests, Actions & Combat

Are natural 1 and natural 20 automatic on every d20 Test?

No. Ability Checks and Saving Throws compare the final total to the DC. Attack rolls keep their explicit natural-1 automatic miss and natural-20 automatic hit/critical rules.

How does Help work?

A meaningful helper gives +2 to one Ability Check. Help does not grant Advantage. There is no Assist Attack, and Help does not modify Death Saves.

Can Grapple or Shove replace one attack from Extra Attack?

No. Grapple and Shove are each a full Action.

How do I stand from Prone?

Standing from Prone requires your full Action and consumes no movement.

Does flanking automatically grant Advantage?

No. Wings of Atreia has no universal Flanking rule and no universal Facing rule.

Is there a Surprise Round?

No. In a genuine ambush, the GM may allow one appropriate pre-Initiative attack. That is discretionary and is not an extra round.

How is 3D distance measured?

When horizontal and vertical separation both matter, use the greater of the two distances rather than adding them.

Does taking damage at 0 HP automatically add failed Death Saves?

No. Ordinary damage while already at 0 HP does not alter the Death Save track. A natural 1 on a Death Save is exactly one failure.

Spellcasting

Does Wings of Atreia use spell slots or cantrips?

No. There are no spell slots and no cantrip category. Each spell has its own frequency: At-Will, Cooldown, Short Rest, or Long Rest.

Does ordinary damage force a Concentration save?

No. Ordinary damage does not test Concentration. Concentration ends only according to the Wings of Atreia Concentration rules and explicit effects.

Can I buy, copy, or learn spells from scrolls?

Not as a universal rule. Spells are class-granted and unlock through class progression and their printed prerequisites.

Does every Spell Advancement make the spell mechanically stronger?

No. One Spell Advancement raises one eligible unlocked spell by exactly +1 Spell Level. A Spell Level is not automatically a mechanical improvement breakpoint.

If the new Spell Level is not listed in that spell's Spell-Level Scaling or Milestone Improvements, record the higher Spell Level normally but do not change damage, healing, range, area, duration, riders, or any other effect. Do not invent compensation. The progression-only Spell Level remains valid permanent progression, may satisfy prerequisites, and counts toward the next listed improvement.

Can I skip an unexciting Spell Level and jump to the next improvement?

No. Each Spell Advancement increases the selected eligible spell by exactly one Spell Level. Progression-only levels are still real levels.

Does casting a Bonus Action spell prevent me from casting another spell that turn?

No universal D&D-style Bonus Action spell restriction exists. Follow the actual Action/Bonus Action timing and the individual spell text.

Daeva Points & Ultimates

When do I gain Daeva Points and unlock my Ultimate?

Beginning at Level 10, eligible Daevas can earn DP from genuine combat. Maximum DP is 100. At 100 DP, the class Ultimate is ready.

Does only the character who lands the killing blow gain DP?

No. Eligible Level 10+ Daevas who meaningfully participated receive the full qualifying post-combat DP award; it is not divided by killing blows.

What happens when my Ultimate attack misses?

After the miss is confirmed, the player chooses one of two final resolutions:

Release the Power: spend all 100 DP and resolve one-half, rounded down, of the Ultimate's fixed primary damage plus one-half of any direct Ultimate healing, restoration, or repair amount that would have occurred on a hit. Normal weapon/Bastion damage, area or secondary-target damage, temporary HP/protection, forced movement, conditions/status effects, condition removal, and other riders do not occur. There is no next-dawn lockout.

Hold the Power: resolve no Ultimate effect, retain all 100 DP, and that Ultimate cannot be attempted again until the next dawn. Rests and Aetheric Reconstitution do not end the lockout early.

Ultimate Recovery occurs after either choice.

What happens after an Ultimate attempt?

Your next turn is an Ultimate Recovery turn whether the Ultimate hit, used Release the Power, or used Hold the Power. During that turn you cannot make weapon or spell attacks, deal damage, restore HP, or impose hostile conditions/status effects. You may still use legal movement, defensive, utility, environmental, and non-healing aid options. Reactions remain available, including Opportunity Attacks. Recovery ends at the end of that turn.

Does a critical hit increase an Ultimate's fixed output?

No. Fixed Ultimate output is not increased by the critical hit. Only rolled normal weapon/Bastion damage dice double when applicable.

Can resting, equipment, consumables, or class features restore DP?

No. DP persists through rests and Aetheric Reconstitution but is not restored by those systems. DP cannot exceed 100, be transferred, or have the 100-DP Ultimate cost reduced.

Flight

Are Wings just cosmetic?

No. Every Daeva has functional Wings and uses the dedicated exploration-flight and Combat Flight Slot systems.

Does higher altitude automatically grant Advantage?

No. Use only explicit aerial rules such as Descending Attack, Exposed Wings, altitude, range, and cover interactions.

Equipment, Professions & Economy

Does armor inherently give Stealth Advantage or Disadvantage?

No. Armor creates no inherent Stealth Advantage or Disadvantage unless an explicit rule says otherwise.

Is there a universal magic-item attunement limit?

No universal attunement-slot subsystem exists.

Can Professions craft complete weapons and armor from nothing?

No. Weaponsmithing, Armorcrafting, and Handicrafting are primarily upgrade professions under the approved equipment ladder rather than a general manufacture-from-nothing system.

How many Professions can be Active?

One Profession is Active at a time. Changing the Active Profession occurs only on level-up. Ranks and prior progress in inactive Professions are retained.

Legions & Warfronts

Do I use my ordinary AC, damage dice, spells, and class resources directly as Warfront stats?

No. Warfront scale is intentionally separate. Use the Warfront rules unless an effect explicitly says it transfers across scales.

What are a PC's Warfront values?

A participating PC uses ATK 3 while attacking and DEF 3 while defending. PCs are not Units, have no Tier or END, and cost no WA.

Can I gain or spend DP or use my Ultimate during a Warfront?

No. DP and Ultimates are inaccessible for the entire Warfront operation, including embedded character-scale missions. Existing DP is retained until the operation ends.

Can PCs capture Warfront objectives by themselves?

No. Objective and grid control follows the Unit rules. PCs do not independently capture Warfront objectives.

When are player-created Legions available?

Player-created Legions are campaign-gated organizational content. At least one founder must meet the required Level threshold, and the GM must confirm that the campaign has reached the required public state. Reaching the Level threshold by itself does not unlock or reveal hidden campaign information.

Class Rules

Can I preload class resources by staging fake combat?

No. Class resources are generated only by their explicit triggers. Fake Initiative, harmless staged attacks, allies, environmental tricks, unrelated consumables, or manufactured pseudo-combat do not create resources unless a rule explicitly says they do.

Does every party need a Templar and Cleric?

No. Wings of Atreia does not require an MMO-style tank/healer/DPS composition. Different classes make different problems easier, but legal party composition is not defined by MMO raid roles.

Does an Aethertech permanently lose the Bastion if it reaches 0 HP?

No. Use the current Aethertech Bastion recovery/redeployment procedure on the Aethertech class page. A disabled Bastion is not a permanent loss of the class chassis.
