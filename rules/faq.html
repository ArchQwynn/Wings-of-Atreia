Player FAQ

A player-safe reference for Wings of Atreia rules that are easy to misread by habit or commonly need clarification at the table.

Authority. This FAQ explains the rules; it does not replace them. Use the current governing rules page for the complete operative text. If a FAQ answer and the current governing rule differ, the current website rules reference takes precedence.

Character Creation & Advancement

Do I choose a race or species?

No. Every standard player character is a Daeva. Choose Elyos or Asmodian as faction/culture. Both factions use the same universal Daeva mechanical foundation.

How do I assign Ability Scores?

Assign 16, 14, 14, 12, 10, and 8 freely among STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, and CHA. There is no ability-score rolling or point buy.

Can I multiclass?

No. A Daeva advances in one class from Level 1 through Level 20.

Do characters gain XP?

No. Advancement is Milestone only. The GM awards levels for meaningful campaign progress.

When do I choose an ASI or Feat?

Universal ASI/Feat choices occur at Levels 4, 8, 12, and 16. Level 19 grants an Empyrean Boon, not another normal ASI/Feat choice.

What does a Background give me?

A Background gives exactly two Skill Proficiencies chosen from that Background's eligible skill list and can establish narrative relevance or permission where appropriate. It does not automatically grant Advantage, Expertise, or guaranteed success.

Does duplicate proficiency become Expertise?

No. Proficiency adds PB once. Expertise exists only when an explicit feature grants it.

Can I change my Specialization after choosing it?

Yes, but only at the normal reassignment checkpoints. Choose the initial Specialization at Level 3. When gaining Level 6, Level 10, and Level 14, you may continue the current Specialization or switch to another available to your class. Level 14 is the final normal Specialization choice through Level 20.

If I switch Specializations, do I keep features from my old path?

No. Remove every feature granted by the previous Specialization and replace it with the new Specialization's complete feature package through the current checkpoint. Recalculate path-dependent choices, capacities, and maxima as required. Reassignment itself restores no HP or class resources.

Can I change Specialization during downtime or by training?

No. Normal reassignment happens only while gaining Level 6, 10, or 14. Rest, downtime, training, or time between adventures does not create another reassignment opportunity.

When do I choose an Empyrean Boon?

You choose an Empyrean Boon at Level 19, following the character's level-up rules.

d20 Tests, Actions & Combat

Are natural 1 and natural 20 automatic on every d20 Test?

No. Ability Checks and Saving Throws compare the final total to the DC. Attack rolls retain their explicit natural-1 automatic miss and natural-20 automatic hit and critical-hit rules.

How does Help work?

A meaningful helper gives +2 to one Ability Check. Help does not grant Advantage. There is no Assist Attack, and Help does not modify Death Saves.

Can Grapple or Shove replace one attack from Extra Attack?

No. Grapple and Shove are each a full Action; they do not replace one attack within the Attack Action or substitute for Extra Attack.

How do I stand from Prone?

Standing from Prone requires your full Action and consumes no movement.

Does flanking automatically grant Advantage?

No. Wings of Atreia has no universal Flanking rule and no universal Facing rule.

Is there a Surprise Round?

No. When one side genuinely catches another unaware, the GM may allow one appropriate pre-Initiative attack at discretion. It is not an extra round and is not automatic.

How is 3D distance measured?

For WoA aerial targeting, total distance is the greater of horizontal distance or vertical distance. Do not add them together or use Pythagorean distance.

Does forced movement provoke Opportunity Attacks?

No. Forced movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks unless an explicit rule says otherwise. The normal Opportunity Attack rule applies when a hostile creature you can perceive voluntarily leaves your melee reach.

Can I convert an Action into a Bonus Action, or a Bonus Action into an Action?

No. There is no universal Action-to-Bonus-Action or Bonus-Action-to-Action conversion. A feature must explicitly allow the change.

Can I make an Unarmed Attack?

Player characters do not have a universal Unarmed Attack option. The normal Attack Action requires an approved class weapon unless an explicit feature creates an exception.

Does taking ordinary damage at 0 HP add failed Death Saves?

No. Ordinary damage received while already at 0 HP has no effect on HP or Death Saves and cannot kill unless an explicit exceptional effect says otherwise. A natural 1 on a Death Save counts as one failure under the current rule.

Does healing a Dying character clear their Death Save track?

Yes. Valid healing that raises a creature at 0 HP to at least 1 HP ends the ordinary Dying/0-HP Unconscious state, clears accumulated Death Save successes and failures, and wakes the creature unless another independent effect still causes Unconsciousness.

Does Temporary HP wake or stabilize a creature at 0 HP?

No. Temporary HP is a separate damage buffer. It does not wake, stabilize, end Dying, or substitute for actual HP.

Spellcasting

Does Wings of Atreia use spell slots or cantrips?

No. There are no spell slots and no cantrip category. Each spell has its own frequency: At-Will, Cooldown, Short Rest, or Long Rest.

Does ordinary damage force a Concentration save?

No. Ordinary damage does not create a universal Concentration save. Concentration ends according to the Wings of Atreia Concentration rules and explicit effects.

Can I buy, copy, or learn spells from scrolls?

Not as a universal rule. Spells are class-granted and unlock through class progression and their printed prerequisites. They cannot be bought, sold, copied from spellbooks or scrolls, or learned from vendors through ordinary commerce.

Does every Spell Advancement make the spell mechanically stronger?

No. One Spell Advancement raises one eligible unlocked spell by exactly +1 Spell Level. A Spell Level is not automatically a mechanical improvement breakpoint.

If the new Spell Level is not listed in that spell's Spell-Level Scaling or Milestone Improvements, record the higher Spell Level normally but do not change damage, healing, range, area, duration, riders, or any other effect. Do not invent compensation. The progression-only Spell Level remains valid permanent progression, may satisfy prerequisites, and counts toward the next listed improvement.

Can I skip an unexciting Spell Level and jump to the next improvement?

No. Each Spell Advancement increases the selected eligible spell by exactly one Spell Level. Progression-only levels are still real levels.

When can I spend a Spell Advancement?

Normally, only when your character gains a level. Unspent Spell Advancements may be banked indefinitely, but they cannot normally be spent during an adventure, between encounters, or during a rest.

Can I retrain a Spell Advancement after spending it?

There is no universal spell retraining. Once spent, a Spell Advancement is permanent unless a specific class feature, explicit story effect, or GM-approved exceptional circumstance directly permits reassignment.

Does casting a Bonus Action spell prevent me from casting another spell that turn?

No universal D&D-style Bonus Action spell restriction exists. Follow the actual Action/Bonus Action timing and the individual spell text.

What happens if I lose my required implement?

If your class feature or spell requires an implement, losing, dropping, stowing, or being disarmed of that implement prevents only new spells and class features that require it until an appropriate implement is equipped again. It does not automatically end a spell already cast or dismiss an already-summoned Spirit unless that specific effect says so.

Can I cast while merely carrying my required implement?

No. When a feature requires an equipped implement, it must be equipped and usable according to its hand requirement. Merely carrying it is insufficient.

How does a Cooldown spell recover?

In genuine combat, a Cooldown X spell becomes available at the start of the caster's Xth turn after the casting turn. Outside genuine combat, it becomes available again 1 minute after use unless the spell states a longer restriction.

Daeva Points & Ultimates

When do I gain Daeva Points and unlock my Ultimate?

Beginning at Level 10, eligible Daevas can earn DP from genuine combat. Maximum DP is 100. At 100 DP, the class Ultimate is ready and may be activated.

Does only the character who lands the killing blow gain DP?

No. Eligible Level 10+ Daevas who meaningfully participated receive the full qualifying post-combat DP award. It is not divided and does not depend on the killing blow. DP is awarded only after combat has fully ended.

What happens when I activate my Ultimate?

Activating an Ultimate expends all 100 DP and consumes both your Action and Bonus Action. You cannot activate it unless both are available. The individual class Ultimate still controls its target selection, range, movement, positioning, weapon, Bastion, and other activation requirements.

Does my Ultimate require an attack roll?

No. An Ultimate does not require an attack roll. Once activated and its stated requirements are satisfied, its primary effect automatically applies to the designated primary target. The primary target does not make an attack-related roll or saving throw to avoid that primary effect.

Do secondary targets automatically suffer the Ultimate's effects?

Not necessarily. Secondary targets make saving throws whenever the individual Ultimate specifies them. Those saves determine whether they avoid or reduce the damage or effects as stated by that Ultimate. Removing the attack roll does not remove saving throws expressly required for secondary targets.

Are Ultimate damage and healing dice rolled?

No. The Ultimate's own direct damage or healing dice use their maximum value, using the values for the character's current class-level band: Levels 10–14, Levels 15–19, or Level 20. Normal weapon or Bastion weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally.

Can an Ultimate critically hit?

No. An Ultimate has no attack roll and therefore cannot score a critical hit. Its fixed output is not increased by critical-hit rules. Normal weapon or Bastion damage dice remain rolled normally and are not doubled by a critical hit from the Ultimate.

Do Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability, and damage reduction apply?

Yes. Defenses apply normally. Automatic primary resolution does not override Immunity, Resistance, damage reduction, or another rule that prevents an effect from applying.

Can Extra Attack or another feature repeat or multiply my Ultimate?

No. An Ultimate is not the Attack Action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, Reactions, or similar features. No effect can repeat an Ultimate or multiply its fixed output.

What happens on my next turn after using an Ultimate?

Your next turn is an Ultimate Recovery turn. During that turn, you cannot take an Action or Bonus Action. You may still move normally, fly or change altitude if applicable, use Reactions (including Opportunity Attacks), interact with objects or the environment, and use consumables such as potions. Permitted object interactions do not require an Action or Bonus Action. Ultimate Recovery ends at the end of that turn.

Can resting, equipment, consumables, or class features restore DP?

No. DP persists through Short Rests, Long Rests, and Aetheric Reconstitution but is not restored by those systems, consumables, spells, equipment, or class-resource effects. DP cannot exceed 100, be transferred, or have its cost reduced.

Can I gain or spend DP or use an Ultimate during a Warfront?

No. DP cannot be gained or spent and Ultimates cannot be activated during a Warfront operation, including character-scale missions embedded in that operation. Existing DP is retained but inaccessible until the Warfront ends.

Flight

Are Wings just cosmetic?

No. Every Daeva has functional Wings and uses the dedicated exploration-flight and Combat Flight Slot systems.

What altitude layers exist?

There are four airborne altitude layers: Low 30 ft, Mid 60 ft, High 90 ft, and Legendary 120 ft. Ground level is 0 feet and is not an altitude layer. Only Daevas with Legendary Wings may voluntarily ascend to the Legendary layer.

What happens when I voluntarily change altitude?

Voluntarily ascending or descending one altitude layer consumes all movement for that turn. You cannot voluntarily ascend and descend during the same turn. Dash does not permit an additional altitude-layer change.

How fast can I move during Combat Flight?

Combat Flight speed is based on the Daeva's default 30-foot walking speed: 45 ft for horizontal flight without changing altitude, 30 ft when ascending by 5 feet or more, and 60 ft when descending by 5 feet or more. These values do not recalculate from modified walking speed or other movement bonuses. Dash does not multiply or increase them.

Does higher altitude automatically grant Advantage?

No. A melee attack has Advantage when the target is at least 5 feet lower than the attacker when the attack is made. The attacker does not need to descend a minimum distance during that turn. Spellcasting receives no inherent altitude-based Advantage or Disadvantage.

How does Exposed Wings work?

A lower-altitude physical ranged attacker has Advantage against a higher-altitude winged airborne target because the target's wings are exposed. This does not apply to wingless creatures flying through Saturated Aether. A higher-altitude ranged attacker gains no automatic Advantage merely for being above the target.

How do Flight Slots work?

A stable airborne Daeva spends 1 Flight Slot at the end of each turn. A grounded Daeva recovers 1 Flight Slot at the end of each grounded turn. A Short Rest restores half the maximum, rounded down; a Long Rest restores all. Use the current Flight page's level table for the character's maximum.

What happens when I run out of Flight Slots while airborne?

You immediately fall. When Flight Slot depletion causes the fall, you may use your Reaction for a DC 12 Dexterity save. On success, fall damage is halved; on failure or without a Reaction, you take full fall damage. A specific feature such as Controlled Descent may change the result.

How do Aether States affect flight?

Stable Aether uses normal flight rules. Depleted Aether fully restricts Aether-supported flight; an airborne Daeva entering it loses flight and falls, subject to the applicable Emergency Landing Reaction. Saturated Aether removes Combat Flight Slot expenditure for Daevas and can grant eligible wingless creatures flight equal to their normal Speed. Specific transition and falling rules apply when crossing between Aether States.

Is Exploration Flight the same as Combat Flight?

No. Exploration Flight uses its own level-based Flight Time and distance progression. It is separate from Combat Flight Slots and combat movement speeds. Use the Exploration Flight table on the governing Flight page.

Does gliding consume Flight Time or Flight Slots?

Exploration gliding requires at least 30 feet of descent, functioning open wings, and an Aether State that permits flight. It travels 3 feet horizontally per 1 foot descended, consumes neither Flight Time nor Flight Slots, always loses altitude, and has a 1-hour cooldown. Master Glider improves the ratio to 4:1.

Equipment, Professions & Economy

Does armor inherently give Stealth Advantage or Disadvantage?

No. Armor creates no inherent Stealth Advantage or Disadvantage unless an explicit rule says otherwise.

Is there a universal magic-item attunement limit?

No universal attunement-slot subsystem exists.

Can Professions craft complete weapons and armor from nothing?

No. Weaponsmithing, Armorcrafting, and Handicrafting upgrade eligible existing equipment. They do not provide a general system for crafting Weapons, Armor, Implements, or Shields from scratch.

How many Professions can be Active?

One Profession is Active at a time. Changing the Active Profession occurs only when the character gains a level. Ranks, paid entry fees, and completed Training Progress in inactive Professions are retained, but an inactive Profession cannot be used or advanced.

What is required to become an Apprentice?

Apprentice rank requires 100 Kinah and 3 full Training Days, with a qualified Apprentice or Master trainer in the same Profession.

What is required to become a Master?

Master rank requires the character to be Level 10 or higher and already an Apprentice in that Profession, plus 500 Kinah and 5 additional full Training Days with a qualified Master trainer in the same Profession.

Can I train myself or train while adventuring?

No. A character cannot train themselves. A Training Day is one exclusive full in-game day; the trainee and trainer cannot adventure, undertake missions, travel for an adventure, perform other downtime work, or produce Profession items that day. Training Days need not be consecutive.

Can I use an inactive Profession?

No. An inactive Profession retains its rank and completed Training Progress but cannot be used or advanced. It can be reactivated under the level-up Profession-change rule without paying an already-paid rank entry fee again.

Do equipment quality bonuses stack?

No. Equipment progression uses replacement tiers rather than stacking every previous tier's bonus. Apply the current item's qualifying tier and its listed effects; do not add superseded tier bonuses on top.

Legions & Warfronts

Do I use my ordinary AC, damage dice, spells, and class resources directly as Warfront stats?

No. Warfront scale is intentionally separate. Use the Warfront rules unless an effect explicitly says it transfers across scales.

What are a PC's Warfront values?

A participating Level 1–10 PC uses ATK 3 while attacking and DEF 3 while defending. A Level 11–20 PC uses ATK 4 while attacking and DEF 4 while defending. PCs are not Units, have no Tier or END, and cost no WA.

How many Warfront rerolls does each PC receive?

Each participating PC has a maximum of 3 rerolls for the entire Warfront. A PC may choose to reroll a Warfront die they personally rolled. All three rerolls may be spent on the same original roll if the player chooses. Once spent, they are unavailable for the rest of that Warfront.

Can I gain or spend DP or use my Ultimate during a Warfront?

No. DP and Ultimates are inaccessible for the entire Warfront operation, including embedded character-scale missions. Existing DP is retained until the operation ends.

Can PCs capture Warfront objectives by themselves?

No. PCs do not independently capture Major Ground Objectives or Open Abyss grids. Objective and grid control follows the Warfront Unit rules.

When are player-created Legions available?

Player-created Legions are campaign-gated. The Campaign Director or controlling GM must explicitly make Legion Founding available. One founding player must be Level 11 or higher; other founding or joining members must be Level 3 or higher and have reached their Specialization or Archetype choice where applicable. Reaching Level 11 alone does not unlock the system.

Class Rules

Can I preload class resources by staging fake combat?

No. Class resources are generated only by their explicit triggers. Fake Initiative, harmless staged attacks, allies, environmental tricks, unrelated consumables, or manufactured pseudo-combat do not create resources unless a rule explicitly says so.

Does every party need a Templar and Cleric?

No. Wings of Atreia does not require an MMO-style tank/healer/DPS composition. Different classes make different problems easier, but legal party composition is not defined by MMO raid roles.

What happens when an Aethertech Bastion reaches 0 HP?

The Bastion becomes Disabled. The pilot is ejected to the nearest legal space, takes 1d6 Force damage, and the ejection does not provoke Opportunity Attacks. The disabled Bastion retains its current Core and Heat. A Short Rest repairs it by half its maximum HP; a Long Rest repairs it to maximum HP. Once it has at least 1 HP, it is operational again.
