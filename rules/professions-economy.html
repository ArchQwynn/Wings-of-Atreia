Consumables, Professions & Economy

Currency

Kinah is the only universal currency. WoA does not use denominations.

Professions

Wings of Atreia uses five Professions: Weaponsmithing, Armorcrafting, Handicrafting, Alchemy, and Cooking. A character may have only one Active Profession at a time. The only Profession ranks are Apprentice and Master. There is no Profession XP, no intermediate rank, and no separate recipe, formula-book, manual, or recipe-learning subsystem. Earning a rank automatically grants the standard functions assigned to that rank.

A Training Day is one exclusive full in-game day. The trainee and trainer cannot adventure, undertake missions, perform adventuring travel, conduct other downtime work, perform Profession production, or complete other substantive activities that day. Training Days need not be consecutive. An interrupted day grants no Training Progress, but previously completed Training Days remain.

A character cannot train themselves. One qualified trainer may instruct up to four trainees of the same rank category during one Training Day. When a PC provides training, that PC receives the one-time entry fee: 100 Kinah per Apprentice trainee or 500 Kinah per Master trainee. NPC or institutional fees go to that provider.

Profession Supplies

Only Basic and Advanced supply grades exist mechanically. Specific ore, alloy, herb, spice, monster-part, crystal, reagent, or regional names are flavor only and do not change grade, quantity, value, or effect.

SupplyBasicAdvanced
Weapon Material100 Kinah300 Kinah
Armor Material100 Kinah300 Kinah
Handicraft Material100 Kinah300 Kinah
Alchemical Reagent25 Kinah100 Kinah
Cooking Ingredient10 Kinah50 Kinah

Daily Merchant Stock. For each Basic Profession supply type a merchant carries, roll 1d8 once for that current in-game day. For an Advanced supply type, the GM first decides whether that merchant has Advanced stock at all. If not, stock is 0 and no roll is made; if yes, roll 1d4 once for that day. Roll separately for each supply type. Purchases reduce remaining stock; returning to the shop does not reroll it.

Profession supplies may also enter play through missions, exploration, salvage, encounters, or NPC/faction rewards. There is no Gathering Profession and no default harvesting roll. Supply categories cannot substitute for one another.

There are no facility quality tiers. Facility access fees, when any apply, are determined by the GM or the fiction.

Weaponsmithing, Armorcrafting, and Handicrafting each require one exclusive full Profession Work Day per upgrade stage. If interrupted, the upgrade is incomplete, materials are not lost, and another full Work Day is required. Materials are consumed only when the work is successfully completed.

Weaponsmithing

Weaponsmithing upgrades an existing eligible Weapon obtained through the normal equipment-acquisition rules; it does not craft Weapons from scratch.

UpgradeRankMaterialsTimeResult
Common -> Profession +1Apprentice2 Basic Weapon Materials1 Work Day+1 to Hit
Profession +1 -> Profession +2Master2 Advanced Weapon Materials1 Work Day+2 to Hit

Upgrades are sequential. Weaponsmithing changes only the approved quality bonus; it does not change damage dice, damage type, weapon properties, reload/firing rules, class eligibility, Critical Edge, or other weapon traits.

Armorcrafting

Armorcrafting upgrades an existing eligible Armor item; it does not craft Armor from scratch and never upgrades Shields.

Alchemy

Alchemy produces approved recovery and treatment consumables. Preparation requires 1 hour per consumable. If interrupted, no product is created and reagents are not consumed; reagents are consumed only when preparation is successfully completed.

Wind Serums restore Flight Slots only and cannot bypass Depleted Aether or other flight restrictions. Aether Cure remains an NPC-purchased special consumable only and is not an Alchemy formula.

Alchemy does not create combat buffs, elemental-resistance elixirs, environmental-protection elixirs, Darkvision or underwater-breathing effects, Aether-status stabilization, DP restoration, Ultimate Recovery removal, class-resource restoration, cooldown resets, or permanent bonuses.

Cooking

An Apprentice Meal uses 2 Basic Cooking Ingredients per serving. A Master Meal uses 2 Advanced Cooking Ingredients per serving. One hour prepares a batch of up to 6 servings of the same Meal type and rank. Every serving consumes its full ingredient cost. Different Meal types or ranks require separate batches. If preparation is interrupted, no meals are completed and ingredients are not consumed.

A creature may benefit from only one Profession Meal effect at a time. Consuming a new Profession Meal replaces the previous Meal effect. The Meal benefit lasts until the creature completes its next Long Rest. A consumed Profession Meal also counts as that creature's ration for that specific meal/time.

Services, Persistence, and Commerce

An Active Profession user may perform Profession work for another character; the recipient does not need the Profession. Completed Profession results remain valid if the practitioner later changes Profession. Equipment quality stays attached to the item through transfer, trade, sale, or new ownership.

NPC practitioners may provide the same services as PCs of the same rank and must obey the same rank, material, facility, work-time, sequential-upgrade, and other core requirements. NPC availability and service fees are GM-controlled; not every settlement is guaranteed to have every Profession practitioner.

There is no mandatory PC-to-PC service fee for ordinary Profession work. Training fees are the explicit exception. Professions do not create guaranteed income loops: sales and commissions require an actual buyer or client, and demand and rewards remain GM-controlled.

Aether Cure

Aether Cure is an NPC-purchased special consumable and is not an Alchemy formula. Where an appropriate Apothecary exists, it costs 750 Kinah and has no universal 1-per-PC-per-day limit. Use an Action and make DC 15 Wisdom (Medicine). On success, remove all Conditions affecting the target except Exhaustion. On failure, remove nothing. The Cure is consumed either way.

Availability

  • Common. Normally available where the appropriate merchant or service exists.
  • Limited. Normally found only in major settlements, capitals, military hubs, or specialized establishments.
  • Restricted. Requires special access, faction authority, a specific NPC, campaign progress, or GM approval.

There are no random merchant-stock rolls except Profession supply stock. Profession supplies use the per-type daily 1d8 Basic / GM-presence-then-1d4 Advanced procedure in this chapter.

Services

  • General Merchants. Food, drink, clothing, and narrative goods.
  • Apothecaries. Life Serums, Wind Serums, and Aether Cure. Aether Cure from an appropriate NPC costs 750 Kinah and is not universally limited per day.
  • Blacksmiths/Armorers. Repairs only; not routine weapon/armor sales.
  • Aethermasters. Aether equipment enhancement and specialized Aether services in appropriate major locations.
  • Flight Transporters. Paid inter-region transport; exact fare may vary by route.
  • Teleporters. Transport to/from the Abyss; exact fare may vary by location.
Final Inventory Slot Rules. Weapon 2 slots each; Armor 4; Shield 4; Obelisk 6; Wings 4; Ammunition 100 per slot; Potions/Serums 99 per slot; Profession Supplies 100 units per slot by same type + same grade; Kinah 0.
Final Aethermaster Costs. Aether +1 costs 200 Refined Aether and Aether +2 costs 500. Level 10+ required. Weapons/Armor/Implements require Profession +2; Shields/Wings do not. Aether +2 remains a direct full-cost choice from an eligible base.
