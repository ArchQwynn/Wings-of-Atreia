Feats & Empyrean Boons

ASI choices, all Universal Feats, and the six Level 19 Empyrean Boons.

Source: Wings of Atreia Player Handbook v3.10 — Publication Master, 8 September 2026. This page is a player-facing reference extracted from the handbook rather than a replacement ruleset.
ASI / Feat ChoicesUniversal FeatsEmpyrean Boons

Whenever your class grants a Feat / Ability Score Improvement, choose one: +2 to one Ability Score; +1 to two different Ability Scores; or one eligible Universal Feat. An Ability Score normally cannot exceed 20 through an ASI.
