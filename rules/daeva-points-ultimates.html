Daeva Points & Ultimate Abilities

Daeva Points & Ultimate Abilities

LEVEL 10 UNIVERSAL FEATURE. Beginning at Level 10, a Daeva can accumulate up to 100 Daeva Points (DP). At 100 DP, the Daeva's class Ultimate Ability is ready and may be attempted. A successful Ultimate attack expends all 100 DP. If the Ultimate attack misses, the player chooses Release the Power or Hold the Power after the miss is confirmed, as described below.

Earning DP. At the end of genuine combat, the GM totals the DP values of eligible hostile creatures defeated in that combat. Every Level 10 or higher Daeva who meaningfully participated gains that full total; the award is not divided and never depends on the killing blow. DP is awarded only after combat has fully ended, so DP gained from a victory cannot ready another Ultimate during that same combat.

Climactic Boss. Climactic Boss is a DP-award designation, not a new Monster Compendium Rank. The GM may use it for a Boss that serves as the decisive threat of a major story arc; an ordinary Boss remains worth 10 DP.

Eligible Defeat. A hostile creature counts when the party overcomes it through genuine combat, whether it is killed, reduced to 0 HP, captured, forced to surrender, or decisively driven from the encounter. Training, sparring, staged encounters, deliberately farmed enemies, allied or controlled creatures, summoned creatures, repeated forms of the same creature, and opposition that never represented a genuine threat award 0 DP. The GM makes the final eligibility ruling.

Persistence and Restrictions. DP persists between encounters and through Short Rests, Long Rests, and Aetheric Reconstitution. Resting, consumables, spells, equipment, and class-resource effects cannot grant or restore DP. DP cannot exceed 100, be transferred, have its cost reduced, or be spent for any purpose other than the character's class Ultimate unless a future rule explicitly says otherwise. A character below Level 10 cannot earn or bank DP.

Warfront Exclusion. DP cannot be gained or spent and Ultimate Abilities cannot be activated during a Warfront operation. This includes character-scale missions embedded in that operation and the Fortress Core Brigadier-General encounter. A character retains existing DP while the Warfront is in progress.

Ultimate Attack Roll. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available, but may still move and take Reactions normally. When the Ultimate requires an attack roll, roll 1d20 + the applicable ability modifier + the character's Proficiency Bonus, with Advantage. Advantage and Disadvantage interact normally, and other explicit modifiers apply normally. The attack is not an Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features.

Fixed Ultimate Output, Miss Choice, and Critical Hits. Damage or healing dice printed as the Ultimate's own direct numerical output are not rolled; use the maximum value of those dice. Normal weapon or Bastion weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally. On a hit, resolve normal weapon/Bastion damage when applicable, all fixed Ultimate damage or healing, and the Ultimate's full legal riders, then expend all 100 DP. On a miss, normal weapon/Bastion damage and full-hit riders do not occur. After the miss is confirmed, the player chooses one resolution. Release the Power: expend all 100 DP and resolve one-half, rounded down, of the Ultimate's fixed primary damage and one-half of any direct Ultimate healing, restoration, or repair amount that would have occurred on a hit. Area or secondary-target damage, temporary HP or other protective effects, forced movement, condition or status effects, condition removal, and other riders do not occur. If a restoration or repair sets a target to an HP threshold rather than rolling dice, determine how much HP the full hit would have restored from the target's current state and restore half that amount, rounded down. Hold the Power: resolve no Ultimate damage, healing, restoration, repair, or other effect and retain all 100 DP, but that Ultimate cannot be attempted again until the next dawn. Short Rests, Long Rests, and Aetheric Reconstitution do not end this restriction early. A critical hit does not increase fixed Ultimate output; only rolled normal weapon/Bastion damage dice double under the normal critical-hit rule. Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability, and damage reduction apply normally.

Ultimate Recovery and Rules Identity. Regardless of whether the Ultimate hits or which miss option is chosen, after the Ultimate attempt resolves the character's next turn is an Ultimate Recovery turn. During that turn, the character cannot make weapon attacks or spell attacks, deal damage, restore Hit Points, or impose conditions or harmful status effects on enemies. The character may move or fly normally, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Search, Use Object, provide Help or other non-healing aid, interact with objects or the environment, and use other legal non-offensive utility or defensive options that do not violate those restrictions. Reactions are unaffected and function normally, including Opportunity Attacks. Ultimate Recovery ends at the end of that turn. An Ultimate remains a class feature, not a spell, class resource, Attack action, or ordinary Bastion system; it does not consume ammunition, a spell use, a cooldown use, or another class resource unless its text explicitly says so. Ordinary on-hit riders apply only when their own triggers are satisfied, and no effect can repeat the Ultimate or multiply its fixed output. Use the Ultimate values for the character's current class-level band: Levels 10-14, Levels 15-19, or Level 20.
