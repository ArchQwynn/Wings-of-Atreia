Daeva Points & Ultimate Abilities

LEVEL 10 UNIVERSAL FEATURE. Beginning at Level 10, a Daeva can accumulate up to 100 Daeva Points (DP). At 100 DP, the Daeva's class Ultimate Ability is ready and may be activated. Activating an Ultimate expends all 100 DP. An Ultimate automatically applies its primary effect to its designated primary target and does not require an attack roll.

Earning DP. At the end of genuine combat, the GM totals the DP values of eligible hostile creatures defeated in that combat. Every Level 10 or higher Daeva who meaningfully participated gains that full total; the award is not divided and never depends on the killing blow. DP is awarded only after combat has fully ended, so DP gained from a victory cannot ready another Ultimate during that same combat.

Climactic Boss. Climactic Boss is a DP-award designation, not a new Monster Compendium Rank. The GM may use it for a Boss that serves as the decisive threat of a major story arc; an ordinary Boss remains worth 10 DP.

Eligible Defeat. A hostile creature counts when the party overcomes it through genuine combat, whether it is killed, reduced to 0 HP, captured, forced to surrender, or decisively driven from the encounter. Training, sparring, staged encounters, deliberately farmed enemies, allied or controlled creatures, summoned creatures, repeated forms of the same creature, and opposition that never represented a genuine threat award 0 DP. The GM makes the final eligibility ruling.

Persistence and Restrictions. DP persists between encounters and through Short Rests, Long Rests, and Aetheric Reconstitution. Resting, consumables, spells, equipment, and class-resource effects cannot grant or restore DP. DP cannot exceed 100, be transferred, have its cost reduced, or be spent for any purpose other than the character's class Ultimate unless a future rule explicitly says otherwise. A character below Level 10 cannot earn or bank DP.

Warfront Exclusion. DP cannot be gained or spent and Ultimate Abilities cannot be activated during a Warfront operation. This includes character-scale missions embedded in that operation and the Fortress Core Brigadier-General encounter. A character retains existing DP while the Warfront is in progress.

Ultimate Activation and Resolution

Activation Cost. Activating an Ultimate consumes both the character's Action and Bonus Action. The character cannot activate it unless both are available. Individual class activation instructions govern target selection, range, movement, positioning, weapon, Bastion, and other requirements; these requirements remain in effect.

Automatic Primary Resolution. An Ultimate does not require an attack roll. Once activated and its stated requirements are satisfied, its primary effect automatically applies to the designated primary target. The primary target does not make an attack-related roll or saving throw to avoid the primary effect. The Ultimate is treated as successfully resolved against the primary target for purposes of its stated primary damage, healing, restoration, repair, conditions, restrictions, and other primary effects. Class-specific on-hit effects resolve as part of this automatic primary resolution.

Secondary Targets and Saving Throws. Secondary targets make saving throws whenever the individual Ultimate specifies them. These saving throws determine whether those targets avoid or reduce damage or effects as stated by that Ultimate. Removing the attack roll does not remove, replace, or bypass saving throws expressly required for secondary targets.

Fixed Ultimate Output. The Ultimate's own direct damage or healing dice are not rolled; use their maximum value. Normal weapon or Bastion weapon damage and damage dice from other features remain rolled normally. Use the Ultimate values for the character's current class-level band: Levels 10–14, Levels 15–19, or Level 20.

Damage and Effect Resolution. Resolve normal weapon or Bastion weapon damage where applicable, all fixed Ultimate damage, healing, restoration, and repair, and all other stated effects. Apply all legal class-specific riders whose conditions are satisfied. No miss resolution exists because an Ultimate cannot miss.

Defenses. Resistance, Immunity, Vulnerability, and damage reduction apply normally. Automatic application does not override immunity or any other rule that prevents an effect from applying.

No Critical Hit. Because an Ultimate does not use an attack roll, it cannot score a critical hit. Fixed Ultimate output is not increased by critical-hit rules. Normal weapon or Bastion damage dice are rolled normally and are not doubled by a critical hit from the Ultimate.

No Duplication or Multiplication. An Ultimate is not the Attack action and cannot be duplicated by Extra Attack, Quick Shot, Akimbo, additional-action effects, reactions, or similar features. No effect can repeat an Ultimate or multiply its fixed output.

Ultimate Recovery

After activating and resolving an Ultimate, the character's next turn is an Ultimate Recovery turn. During that turn, the character cannot take an Action or Bonus Action.

During Ultimate Recovery, the character may:

  • Move normally.
  • Fly or change altitude, if applicable.
  • Use Reactions normally, including Opportunity Attacks.
  • Interact with objects or the environment.
  • Use consumables, such as potions.
  • Perform permitted object interactions without spending an Action or Bonus Action.

Ultimate Recovery ends at the end of that turn.

Ultimate Rules Identity

An Ultimate remains a class feature, not a spell, class resource, Attack action, or ordinary Bastion system. It does not consume ammunition, a spell use, a cooldown, or another class resource unless its class text explicitly says otherwise. All class-specific Ultimate effects, target limits, ranges, durations, and other requirements remain in effect. The universal rules govern activation, automatic primary resolution, secondary saving throws, DP expenditure, and Ultimate Recovery.
