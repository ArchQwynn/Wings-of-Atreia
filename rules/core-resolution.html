22. d20 Tests & Core Resolution

d20 Tests

Ability Checks, attack rolls, and saving throws use a d20. Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and meaningful. If success is automatic, impossible, or consequence-free, no roll is needed.

Ability Checks

Choose the ability that best fits the approach, then add Proficiency Bonus if the character is proficient in the relevant skill. Expertise doubles the PB contribution for that proficiency.

WoA retains six abilities and eighteen skills. A specialized Background can authorize a character to attempt obscure knowledge, but it does not automatically modify the roll.

Saving Throws

A saving throw uses the specified ability modifier and adds PB only if the creature is proficient in that saving throw. Ties are resolved according to the specific system; otherwise meeting a DC succeeds.

Save DCs

Default effect Save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + the relevant ability modifier, unless an explicit class, spell, item, monster, or environmental rule gives a different DC.

Advantage and Disadvantage

When a rule grants Advantage, roll two d20s and use the higher. When it grants Disadvantage, roll two and use the lower. If both apply, they cancel unless a specific rule says otherwise. Resolve Advantage/Disadvantage before applying numeric modifiers.

Help

Help never grants Advantage. A meaningful helper grants +2 to one Ability Check. Normally only one helper can contribute. There is no Assist Attack and Help cannot modify Death Saves.

Passive Perception, Passive Investigation & Search

Passive Perception = 10 + your Wisdom (Perception) modifier. Passive Investigation = 10 + your Intelligence (Investigation) modifier. Advantage on the relevant check adds +5 to the passive score; Disadvantage subtracts 5. Multiple instances of the same source do not stack, and Advantage and Disadvantage cancel normally. Hide is opposed by Passive Perception; ties favor the observer. An active Search uses the GM-selected check, commonly Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation), against the relevant DC or opposing Stealth; ties favor the Searcher where opposed. Repeated checks require a meaningful change in circumstances.
