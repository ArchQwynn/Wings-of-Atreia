PLAYER-CREATED LEGIONS

A player-created Legion is a lightweight organization for shared identity, missions, resources, and cross-faction cooperation when the campaign permits it. Legion membership does not create a new character progression track or grant automatic combat bonuses.

When a Legion Can Be Founded

  • Campaign Gate. The Campaign Director or controlling GM must explicitly declare Legion Founding available. Reaching Level 11 by itself does not unlock the system.
  • Founding Player. One Founding Player must be Level 11 or higher. That character becomes the initial Legion Leader.
  • Other members. Every other founding or joining member must be Level 3 or higher before joining and must already have reached their Specialization or Archetype choice where applicable. A Level 1-2 character cannot join early.
  • Membership has no maximum size. There is no minimum number of additional founders; a qualifying Founding Player may establish the Legion alone if all other founding requirements are satisfied.
  • Elyos and Asmodians may belong to the same Legion once the required campaign state is active. Every member retains their original faction identity and its normal consequences.

Founding Fund and Treasury

  • Founding requires 5,000 Kinah deposited into the new Legion Treasury. Eligible founding members may contribute toward the total.
  • Before establishment, the Founding Fund cannot be withdrawn. If founding is abandoned, each contributor receives their contribution back. Once established, the 5,000 Kinah remains usable Treasury money.
  • Any Legion member may deposit Kinah. Only the Legion Leader may withdraw Kinah or authorize spending.
  • Legion missions may reward participating members, the Treasury, or both as determined by the GM.
  • There is no passive Legion income, automatic salary, interest, business-profit engine, or mandatory recurring membership dues.

Leadership and Membership

  • The only default roles are Legion Leader and Legion Member.
  • The Legion Leader admits and removes members, controls permissions for Legion assets, may transfer leadership, and may formally dissolve the Legion.
  • Leadership may be transferred to any existing legal Legion member. The new Leader does not need to be Level 11; Level 11 is a founding requirement only.

Member Benefits

  • Members may display the Legion name, colors, crest, and insignia. Ordinary Legion identification occupies no Inventory Slot.
  • Members may accept Legion missions and may use resources, facilities, contacts, and support the Legion actually possesses when the Legion Leader permits it.
  • Where the campaign's cross-faction arrangements recognize the Legion, its insignia provides a legitimate reason for Elyos and Asmodian members to operate together. It does not grant diplomatic immunity or unrestricted access.
  • Legion membership grants no automatic Hit, AC, damage, HP, saving throw, skill, Advantage, Spell DC, DP, class-resource, equipment-quality, or personal Inventory bonus.

Legion Storage and Headquarters

  • An established Legion has one secure shared Storage location with a capacity of 100 Inventory Slots. Normal Inventory Slot and stacking rules apply; Kinah remains in the Treasury and uses no Storage Slots.
  • Any Legion member may deposit eligible items. Only the Legion Leader may withdraw items or authorize another member to retrieve them.
  • Legion Storage is stationary and cannot be accessed remotely. It never increases a member's personal Inventory capacity in the field.
  • A Legion may designate one legitimate location as its Headquarters. The Headquarters is simply its meeting place and Storage location; it grants no automatic bonuses and has no upgrade track.

Lightweight Rule. There is no Legion XP, Legion Level, officer hierarchy, skill tree, research tree, reputation meter, headquarters progression, or other default Legion management subsystem.

41. Player-Facing Warfront Rules

Warfronts resolve formation-scale warfare separately from ordinary character-scale combat. PCs can participate, but ordinary class statistics do not replace Warfront ratings unless a Warfront rule explicitly says so.

TermPlayer Meaning
War Asset (WA)Abstract operation budget for formations, equipment, logistics and support.
UnitFormation-scale token with ATK, DEF and END; some units also use SIEGE.
Battle ExchangeCommitted attacking Units roll ATK and committed defenders roll DEF; successful rolls create separate Hits.
Decision WindowAfter an exchange, sides may Continue, Reinforce, Pull Back or Retreat.
Battlefront / GridStrategic position where formations engage.
InfantryOnly formation type that captures major ground objectives or Open Abyss grids.

PC Warfront Participation

  • Each participating PC uses one fixed d6: ATK 3 while attacking and DEF 3 while defending.
  • PCs are not Warfront Units, have no Tier or END, and cost no WA.
  • Ordinary Warfront Hits cannot target or be assigned to PCs.
  • PC Warfront ratings are not modified by ordinary class features, equipment, spells, Wings or character-scale bonuses unless a Warfront rule explicitly names PCs.
  • PCs cannot capture Major Ground Objectives or Open Abyss grids.
  • At least one allied Warfront Unit must accompany PCs in an active Battlefront/grid.
  • At battle start, each PC declares Ground or Aerial assignment for that battle.

Funding an Operation

The GM declares the actual Base WA available to each side before force selection. The system provides recommended baselines for an ordinary full-strength operation, but the GM may adjust either side for the campaign situation. The player side is told its own declared Base WA before selecting Units.

Recommended Baseline WA

These are recommended baselines, not mandatory budgets. The GM may raise or lower the actual Base WA because of manpower, prior losses, reinforcements, garrison strength, logistics, surprise, or other scenario circumstances. WA represents available military resources rather than a guarantee of equal encounter balance.

Player-Funded Supplemental WA. Only the player-controlled side may buy supplemental WA. Spend 1,000 Kinah for +1 WA, to a maximum of 20% of the player side's declared Base WA, rounded down. Unused operation-specific WA is lost after the operation.

42. Fortress and Open Abyss Player Procedures

Fortress Wars

  • Only Infantry captures major ground objectives.
  • Siege attacks Fortification Points and cannot proceed beyond the Main Gate.
  • Engineer is defense-only. Each Engineer has 3 total Repair uses for the entire operation; each successful repair restores 1 lost FP and uses 1 Repair, movement never refreshes that capacity, and the Engineer is expended after the third successful repair. Standard Fortresses allow at most 2 defending Engineers and Major Fortresses at most 4; eliminated or expended Engineers still count against the operation-wide cap unless the scenario explicitly grants an exception. A single Fortification can receive at most 1 FP of Engineer restoration at the same end-of-turn timing. Saboteur improves one Siege attack; Assassin is capped at three uses per side for the entire Fortress War.
  • Levy Infantry are ATK 1 / DEF 1 and use normal Tier END. Their WA cost is I=2, II=3, III=4, IV=5, V=6. Levy cannot receive Support ATK/DEF bonuses or Support END restoration, cannot receive Artillery's bonus Infantry Attack Die, but still gain Artillery's permission to attack Aerial Units. Levy alone cannot capture or recapture a Major Ground Objective; at least one surviving non-Levy Infantry Unit must be present when control changes.
  • The Fortress Core can transition from Warfront scale into the locked character-scale Brigadier-General procedure when the scenario conditions are met.

Open Abyss Battles

  • Uses mirrored 3×3 active grids plus protected Reserve Lines.
  • Only eligible Infantry captures enemy-controlled grids. Levy Infantry cannot change grid control by themselves; at least one surviving non-Levy Infantry Unit must be present when control changes.
  • Aerial Units may move up to two connected grids under the Open Abyss movement rules; most other Units move one.
  • Reserve is protected and is not itself a Battlefront.
  • Siege is excluded from Open Abyss Battles.
  • Assassin Specialists have no Open Abyss cap.
  • If PCs are Cut Off with no open route to Reserve, they remain in Warfront play and cannot Retreat until a valid route reopens. They continue using the fixed PC ATK 3 / DEF 3 participation rules and never capture a grid by themselves. The Fortress Core Brigadier-General procedure is the only established automatic Warfront-to-character-scale handoff.
Scale Separation. Do not transfer ordinary AC, weapon quality, spell attack bonuses, class resources or Combat Flight Slot expenditure directly into Warfront dice unless a Warfront rule explicitly says so.
