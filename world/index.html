The Shattered World

Atreia was once a complete world organized around the Tower of Eternity. The Cataclysm shattered it into two great surviving halves with an immense broken interior between them.

Elysea forms the southern half of shattered Atreia, while Asmodae forms the northern half. Their inhabited surfaces face inward toward the remains of the Tower and toward one another. Between them lies Reshanta, the Abyss: a vast physical expanse of floating islands, fortress territories, ancient ruins, and open Aetheric void.

Beyond the central Elysea–Asmodae–Reshanta theater lies Balaurea. Only part of that wider frontier is established in detail for the current campaign era.

Finished atlas illustrations will be added later. The geographic descriptions below reflect the current Wings of Atreia world canon.

Southern Atreia

Elysea

Elysea forms the southern half of shattered Atreia and is the homeland of the Elyos. Its geography ranges from fertile farmland and temperate frontier territory to deserts, ancient forests, mountains, scorched wastelands, and the great floating capital of Sanctum.

Sanctum

Sanctum is the political, military, religious, and cultural heart of Elyos civilization. It is a monumental floating city defined by radiant architecture, terraces, towers, temples, civic institutions, and extensive Aetheric construction.

Oriel

Oriel lies beneath Sanctum on the same greater floating geography. It is the quieter residential counterpart to the capital, characterized by cultivated estates, gardens, homes, workshops, and maintained natural spaces.

Poeta

Poeta is a fertile island region of plains, farms, forests, lakes, streams, and small settlements. Its principal geographic and campaign landmarks include Akarios Village, Cliona Lake, Daminu Forest, Kabarah Strip Mine, and Timolia Mine.

Verteron

Verteron occupies the northern part of Elysea's principal mainland. It is a temperate defensive frontier of grasslands, coastlines, forests, rocky uplands, old ruins, and established roads. Important locations include Verteron Citadel, Tolbas Village, Verteron Observatory, Cantas Coast, Tursin Garrison, Zumion Ruins, and the Road to Eltnen.

Eltnen

Eltnen lies in the southwest of the Elysea mainland and is distinctly arid. Dry plains, desert, badlands, rocky valleys, scarce water, and surviving pockets of vegetation define the region.

Major locations include Eltnen Fortress, Agairon Village, Golden Bough Garrison, Eltnen Observatory, Eracus Temple Cavern, Kaidan Headquarters, Kuriullu Mountain, Manduri Forest, Geroch Caldera, Lepharist Bastion, the Road to Verteron, and the Road to Heiron.

Heiron

Heiron occupies the southeastern part of the Elysea mainland. It is a broad region of mountains, deep forests, valleys, lakes, rivers, old ruins, and dangerous wilderness.

Important locations include New Heiron Gate, Heiron Observatory, Arbolu's Haven, Jeiparan Village, Kishar Observation Post, and Indratu Fortress. Storm Circle may appear on more detailed maps.

Theobomos

Theobomos is a separate eastern island marked by scorched terrain, barren rock, ancient ruins, fire-scarred landscapes, and isolated surviving areas of habitation.

Important locations include Meniherk Excavation Camp, Observatory Village, Jamanok Inn, The Scorchlands, Fregion's Flame, Theobomos Lab, and Adma Stronghold.

Northern Atreia

Asmodae

Asmodae forms the northern half of shattered Atreia and is the homeland of the Asmodians. Its regions include dark forests, rugged plains, mountains, frozen valleys, glaciers, damaged farmland, corrupted wilderness, and monumental cities shaped by life beneath perpetual night.

Pandaemonium

Pandaemonium is the political, military, religious, and cultural capital of Asmodian civilization. Its monumental dark-stone architecture, elevated structures, halls, towers, and Aetheric engineering make it one of the great cities of Atreia.

Pernon

Pernon occupies the southern part of the same island as Pandaemonium. It is a quieter residential region of estates, gardens, workshops, communities, and cultivated natural spaces.

Ishalgen

Ishalgen is a rugged southern island of forests, rocky highlands, cold waters, isolated settlements, and old traditions. Important locations include Aldelle Village, Anturoon Crossing, Lake Tunapre, Munihele Forest, Odella Plantation, and The Forsaken Hollow.

Altgard

Altgard occupies the southeastern portion of Asmodae's main landmass. It is a settled and militarily important frontier of rugged plains, woodland, hills, waterways, fortifications, and contested wilderness.

Major locations include Altgard Fortress, Altgard Observatory, Basfelt Village, Heart of Impetusium, Altgard Ice Lake, Black Claw Village, MuMu Farmland, Lepharist Encampment, and the Road to Morheim.

Morheim

Morheim stretches across the western part of Asmodae and is one of its most environmentally varied regions. Mountains, steep valleys, forests, rocky passes, desert-like areas, colder terrain, and other contrasting landscapes coexist throughout the region.

Important locations include Morheim Ice Fortress, Alsig Village, Desert Garrison, Morheim Observatory, Mt. Musphel, Salintus Desert, Ice Claw Village, Mist Mane Village, Lepharist Citadel, Sky Temple of Arkanis, the Road to Altgard, and the Road to Beluslan.

Beluslan

Beluslan occupies the northern reaches of Asmodae and carries the strongest frozen identity of the major Asmodian regions. Snowfields, glaciers, frozen waters, mountains, ruins, and severe weather dominate the landscape.

Important locations include Beluslan Fortress, Besfer Refugee Camp, Red Mane Cavern, Beluslan Observatory, Alquimia Stronghold, Alukina's Palace, Glacier Peaks, Hoarfrost Fortress, Mosbear Snowfield, Bakarma Barracks, and the Road to Morheim.

Brusthonin

Brusthonin is a separate western island of rugged coastlines, dark forests, damaged farmland, old settlements, ruins, polluted regions, and areas undergoing reclamation.

Important locations include Baltasar Hill Village, BuBu Village, Settlers Campsite, Adma Stronghold, Iollu Forest, Nahor Lake, Old Nahor Castle, Polluted Waste, The Golden Coast, and Brusthonin Mithril Mine.

The Great Warfront

Reshanta / The Abyss

Reshanta, commonly called the Abyss, fills the vast shattered interior between Elysea and Asmodae. It is a physical region of floating islands, fortress territories, ancient structures, broken landmasses, and enormous open Aetheric voids.

Flight is fundamental to travel and warfare in Reshanta. Its major islands are not connected like conventional continents, and vast gaps of open air separate many strategic locations.

Reshanta is divided into three great zones: Upper Abyss, Core / Eye of Reshanta, and Lower Abyss.

Upper Abyss

The Upper Abyss lies closer to Asmodae and contains several of the most important fortress networks in Reshanta.

Bloodburn Reach

Bloodburn Reach forms the major central fortress cluster of the Upper Abyss. Its principal locations include Kysis Isle and Kysis Fortress, Miren Island and Miren Fortress, Krotan Rock and Krotan Refuge, and the Eye of Reshanta Entrance.

Other Major Upper Abyss Locations

  • Ruins of Roah
  • Roah Fortress
  • Lake Asteria
  • Asteria Fortress
  • Sword's Edge
  • Tigraki Island
  • Black Cloud Island

Other known landmarks include Drakenfall, Drakenwreck, Grave of Claws, Isle of Silence, The Flinders, Collapsed Ancient Temple, Garden of the Dead, The Shattered Temple, Garcikhan Stronghold, Clocktower Plain, Soul Stream, Zephyr Stream, Mirage Cave, and Dryrock.

Core / Eye of Reshanta

The Core forms the central strategic heart of the Abyss. At its center lies the Eye of Reshanta, one of the defining geographic features of the shattered world.

The Divine Fortress stands within the Core and is one of the most important military objectives in Reshanta.

At the beginning of the Wings of Atreia campaign, the Core is dominated by the forces of Dragon Lady Ereshkigal. This makes the central Abyss a hostile strategic barrier between the northern and southern zones.

Abyssal Splinter exists as a specialized campaign location associated with the Divine Fortress but is not a primary world-map landmark.

Lower Abyss

The Lower Abyss lies closer to Elysea and contains the principal Elyos and Asmodian staging bases in Reshanta as well as several major fortress objectives.

Terminon

Terminon Landing and Terminon Fortress form the principal Elyos foothold in Reshanta. At the start of the campaign, Terminon is the established Elyos holding in the Abyss.

Primum

Primum Landing and Primum Fortress form the principal Asmodian foothold in Reshanta. At the start of the campaign, Primum is the established Asmodian holding in the Abyss.

Sulfur Archipelago

The Sulfur Archipelago is one of the principal fortress regions of the Lower Abyss. Important locations include Sulfur Fortress, Isle of Roots, Sulfur Flow, Sulfur Swamp, Isle of Disgrace, and Isle of Reproach.

Wings of Siel Archipelago

The Wings of Siel Archipelago is another major strategic region of the Lower Abyss.

  • Siel's Left Wing
  • Siel's Right Wing
  • Siel's Western Fortress
  • Siel's Eastern Fortress
  • Heart of Siel
  • Grave of Steel
  • Twilight Battlefield
  • Forsaken Isle
  • Island of Exile

Other Lower Abyss orientation points include Leibo Island, Solitary Island, and Shade Island.

Starting Abyss Control

  • Elyos: Terminon
  • Asmodians: Primum
  • Ereshkigal's forces: Core and Divine Fortress

Other major Abyss fortresses are strategic objectives whose control may change through campaign play and the Warfront system.

Deferred Frontier

Balaurea

Balaurea lies beyond the central Elysea–Asmodae–Reshanta theater and is closely associated with the Balaur and Dragon Lords.

Its detailed Wings of Atreia geography is intentionally deferred for later development. The currently established campaign-relevant locations are:

  • Inggison — principal Elyos-accessible Balaurea frontier
  • Gelkmaros — principal Asmodian-accessible Balaurea frontier
  • Silentera Canyon — major connecting subterranean route
  • Beshmundir Temple — major known site deeper in the Balaurean theater

Additional Balaurean regions should not be assumed to be active Wings of Atreia campaign geography unless they are explicitly added to canon later.
