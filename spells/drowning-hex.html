Spiritmaster Spell

Drowning Hex

Water Spirit · Debuff / Control

Source: Wings of Atreia Player Handbook v3.10, Integrated Spell Compendium.
ClassSpiritmaster
ChainWater Spirit
Unlock Level7
PrerequisiteWater Sigil Spell Level 3
Casting TimeAction
Range60 ft
FrequencyCooldown 2
DurationInstantaneous
ConcentrationNo
RoleDebuff / Control

Base Effect. One creature within 60 feet makes a Constitution save. On a failure, until the end of your next turn it cannot take Reactions and its Speed is halved.

Spell-Level Scaling. At Spell Levels 2, 4, 6, and 8, a failed save also deals +1d6 Cold damage.

Milestone Improvements

Spell LevelMilestone Improvement
3On a failed save, the target cannot begin a new Concentration effect until the start of your next turn.
5Duration becomes until the start of your second turn after casting, with a new save at the end of the target’s turn.
7If your Water Spirit is within 15 feet of the target, the target cannot Dash even on a successful save until the end of its next turn.
9A failed-save target cannot become Invisible.
