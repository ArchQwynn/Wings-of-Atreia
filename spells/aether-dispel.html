Sorcerer Spell

Aether Dispel

Arcane · Utility

Source: Wings of Atreia Player Handbook v3.10, Integrated Spell Compendium.
ClassSorcerer
ChainArcane
Unlock Level5
Prerequisite—
Casting TimeAction
Range60 ft
FrequencyShort Rest 1
DurationUntil start of your next turn
ConcentrationNo
RoleUtility

Base Effect. Choose one magical effect on a creature or object and make an Arcana check using INT + PB against that effect's applicable DC; if it has no listed applicable DC, use DC 15. On a success, suppress the chosen effect until the start of your next turn. If you succeed by 5 or more, you may instead end a minor removable effect.

Spell-Level Scaling. This spell improves at Spell Levels 3, 5, 7, and 9 as listed below.

Milestone Improvements

Spell LevelMilestone Improvement
3Gain +1 to the Arcana check.
5Range becomes 90 feet.
7After a successful check, gain +1 to your next Sorcerer spell attack before the end of your next turn.
9You may target a magical area effect occupying up to a 10-foot-radius area instead of a creature or object.
