Cleric Spell

Aegis Field

Protection · Area Protection

Source: Wings of Atreia Player Handbook v3.10, Integrated Spell Compendium.
ClassCleric
ChainProtection
Unlock Level7
PrerequisiteSacred Ward Spell Level 3
Casting TimeAction
Range60 ft
FrequencyCooldown 2
DurationUntil start of next turn
ConcentrationNo
RoleArea Protection

Base Effect. Create a 15-foot-radius protective field centered on a point within 60 feet until the start of your next turn. Allies inside gain +1 AC and Advantage on saves against being Frightened.

Spell-Level Scaling. At Spell Levels 4 and 8, radius increases by 5 feet.

Milestone Improvements
