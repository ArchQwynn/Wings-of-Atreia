About

Wings of Atreia

A standalone D&D-based tabletop roleplaying game inspired by the world and themes of Aion.

What this site is

This website is the player-facing online rules and setting reference for Wings of Atreia. It is designed for fast use during play and easy access on desktop or mobile devices.

Publication role

The formal Wings of Atreia books remain the authoritative publications. This site is intended as a convenient indexed reference and should be synchronized only from approved material.
